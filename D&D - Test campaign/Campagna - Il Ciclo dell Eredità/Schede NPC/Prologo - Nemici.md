### **Vargan il Cinereo**
**Initiative** +2 (12)
**AC** 16  
**HP** 82 (11d8 + 33)  
**Speed** 30 ft.  

![[Pasted image 20260605155928.png]]

**Gear** Chain Mail, Heavy Crossbow, Warhammer  
**Senses** Passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common plus one other language  
**CR** 4 (XP 1 100; PB +2)
#### Traits
**_Emissary of Corruption_**. When hit by the Warhammer of Corruption, any creature that's wielding a Shield or an Armor must succeed a DEX throw (CR 13) or suffer one of the following condition: 
- The shield looses 1 AC. If it reaches 0, the shield is destroyed.
- The armor looses 1 AC and deals 1d4 acid damage to the wielder
#### Actions
**_Multiattack_**. The tough makes two attacks, using Warhammer or Heavy Crossbow in any combination.
**_Warhammer of Corruption_**. _Melee Attack Roll_: +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit_: 10 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, + 5 (1d8+1) Acid damage and if the target is Large or smaller, the tough can push the target up to 10 feet straight away from itself (ST on STR CR 13).
**_Heavy Crossbow_**. _Ranged Attack Roll_: +4, range 100/400 ft. _Hit_: 13 (2d10 + 2) Piercing damage
