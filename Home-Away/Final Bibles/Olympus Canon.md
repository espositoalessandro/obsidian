# 1. Source hierarchy

Olympus information in this file is divided into three levels.

## A. BLits-confirmed canon

Information explicitly stated or shown clearly in:
- Jock Studio demos;
- official BLits development diaries;
- official BLits character / game material.

Treat this as external canon unless Home/Away explicitly diverges.

## B. Strong visual / demo inference

Information strongly suggested by maps, backgrounds, or game presentation but not explicitly stated in text.

Use it confidently for staging, but do not describe it as something BLits formally confirmed.

## C. Home/Away working canon

A continuity choice made for the novel where official canon is incomplete.

These rules are binding for the Final rewrite unless this file is deliberately updated.

# 2. Olympus University — general identity

**BLits-confirmed / established project canon**

Olympus University is a large, prestigious, socially dense university built around:
- academics;
- athletics;
- clubs;
- God Houses;
- campus social life.

The campus is large enough that movement between locations matters and is represented through an interactive campus map in Jock Studio.

BLits describes locations around Olympus University as individually travelable.

For Home/Away, the university should feel like a real campus rather than a collection of disconnected VN backgrounds.

Use four practical campus zones:

### Residential Zone

- residential common spaces;

### Academic Zone
Contains:

- classrooms;
- laboratories;
- study areas;

### Sports Zone

- practice fields;

# 4. Residential system

## 4.1 What BLits actually confirms

BLits Development Update #20 explicitly describes **Matt's Room as being "in the dorms just like Ace."**

The same update separately presents **Dionysus House** as a campus location known for parties.

This is important:

> **Dorms and Houses are not the same system.**

A student's God House should not automatically be treated as the building or wing where that student sleeps.

## 4.2 Residential Hall structure

### Strong visual/demo inference + Home/Away working canon

For the Final rewrite, treat student housing as a **large shared Residential Hall / dorm complex**, internally divided by:
- floors;
- wings / corridors;
- individual rooms;
- shared floor or wing common areas.

This reflects the way the residential structure is visually presented in the demo and gives Home/Away a consistent staging model.

Unless later official canon contradicts it, use this as the novel's residential layout.

## 4.3 House membership does not determine dorm placement

**Final rewrite rule**

Students belonging to different athletic Houses may live in the same Residential Hall system.

Do **not** write:
- Apollo Residence;
- Apollo dorm;
- House Apollo as though it is Leo's bedroom building;
- sports-House names as automatic residential assignments.

Use:
- Residential Hall;
- the dorm;
- Residential Zone;
- floor;
- wing;
- room number.

Taiga and Caleb's **Room 317** belongs to the ordinary dorm system.

## 4.4 Floor parties

A **floor party** is a residential event, not a God House event.

Students from other floors/wings may plausibly attend, and invited friends may also attend.

Therefore, an Apollo player such as Leo can plausibly appear at a dorm-floor party without implying that he lives in House Apollo.

If useful for continuity, Leo may live elsewhere in the same Residential Hall / dorm complex.

His exact room location does not need to be established unless the story later requires it.

# 5. God Houses

## 5.1 Core distinction

**BLits-confirmed structure, with Home/Away wording**

God Houses are best understood as **club / athletic organizations with their own identity and campus spaces**, not as residential colleges.

They are:
- social;
- athletic / club-linked;
- visible around campus;
- associated with dedicated spaces or House locations;
- capable of hosting events and parties.

## 5.2 Festival evidence

BLits Development Update #25 says that during the Olympus Festival:
- the various Houses around campus set up their own booths and activities;
- these booths function as major club fundraisers.

This strongly reinforces:

> **House = organized club / athletic identity**, not dorm assignment.

## 5.3 Known House/team links

### Apollo
**Home/Away established canon**
- soccer-linked House;
- Leo is part of Apollo soccer;
- Taiga joins Apollo through soccer;
- House Apollo can function as a team / social space.

- Dionysus House exists as a physical/social location;
- BLits specifically describes Dionysus House as known for large parties.

- Griffin Usher is described by BLits as a co-captain.

### Other varsity sports confirmed at Olympus

# 6. House Apollo in Home/Away

House Apollo should be treated as a **soccer-linked campus organization / team social space**.

It may contain:
- a common room;
- places for players to meet;
- informal team gatherings;
- film / tactical meetings when appropriate;
- food nights;
- parties or celebrations;
- social overlap with other athletes.

It is **not** where all Apollo players necessarily sleep.

A player can:
- train with Apollo;
- belong to House Apollo;
- spend time in House Apollo;
- then return to an ordinary dorm room elsewhere on campus.

This distinction should be preserved throughout the Final rewrite.

Use these rules during Final rewriting:

2. **House Apollo ≠ Apollo Residence.**

8. The Agora is in Olympus City, not on campus.
