## Olympus cast voice overlap

**First discovered:** Whole-draft assessment  
**Scope:** Character / dialogue  
**Affected chapters:** To audit  
**Status:** Open

### Issue

Several recurring Olympus characters lack sufficiently distinct speech and social behavior. Caleb, Scott and Enzo currently stand out more clearly than much of the supporting cast.

### Final rule or intended fix

Differentiate recurring characters through sentence shape, social instinct, humor mechanism and failure mode rather than catchphrases alone.

### Cross-novel checks

- [ ] Recurring cast inventory completed
- [ ] Weakest voices identified
- [ ] Character Voices Bible updated
- [ ] Early appearances checked
- [ ] Later appearances checked
