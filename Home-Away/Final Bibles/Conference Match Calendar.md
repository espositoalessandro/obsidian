## How to use this file

The calendar separates established story material from the fixtures needed to complete the season:

- **F** - already established in a Final chapter.
- **D** - retained from the current or completed Draft, or an agreed rewrite decision. Preserve the scene's job; details can still change during rewriting.
- **W** - a new working assignment: date, opponent, venue or background result.
- **D/W** - a retained result with a newly assigned opponent or venue.

Dates use **2026 as the working year**. The prose does not need to state it. Calendar arithmetic must support the story rather than force revisions to otherwise coherent Final scenes.

For spring conference fixtures and points, use this file instead of the older match passages in `Final Bibles/Master Calendar.md` and `Bibles/Olympus Match Ledger.md`. Those files contain pre-rewrite assumptions. No manuscript text is changed by this reference.

## Competition format

- **10 squads; double round robin.** Each club plays the other nine once at home and once away.
- **18 matches per club; 90 matches overall; nine home and nine away.** Every round contains five fixtures.
- Three points for a win, one for a draw, none for a defeat.
- Conference starts **Saturday 24 January** and ends **Saturday 2 May**: about fourteen weeks elapsed, rather than eighteen uninterrupted weekly Saturdays.
- Most rounds are on Saturdays. Five midweek rounds fit the full season around the Spring Break gap: **4 February, 25 March, 1 April, 22-23 April and 28 April**.
- **No conference games from 10 through 19 April**, while Taiga and Leo are at Camp Buddy. The last pre-break round is 4 April; Olympus resume on 22 April.
- Autumn Apollo matches are friendlies and do not contribute to this table. Do not restore a separate reserve conference schedule.
- The **Sunday 1 March Pinetree match counts as the away conference fixture**. The joint sessions and mixed small-sided games do not count. The home return is 1 April.
- First legs occupy rounds 1-9; return legs occupy rounds 10-18. The return order differs from the first-leg order. No club has more than three consecutive home or away games.

Kickoff times remain open unless needed by a scene. Exceptions already useful to the story: Westlake on 14 March starts at **15:00**; Redmont-Pinetree on 23 April at **19:30**; Pinetree's 26 April game at **13:00**. On the final day Hartwell-Pinetree finishes before Olympus kick off at St. Vincent. Do not make all final-day kickoffs simultaneous.
