### Chapter 3 - 

Friday evening, Caleb was getting ready in the room. Taiga was dressing up as well. Nothing fancy, just presentable.

"I still think this is a very bad idea."

"It'll be just an hour. And we'll be close by."

Caleb had made the mistake of agreeing to attend a floor party. 
Past-Taiga made the larger mistake of attending as well and now Present-Taiga was complaining. 

"I thought you hate parties."

"I don't hate parties."

“You left orientation because somebody was playing music too loudly.”

“That was different.”

“How exactly?”

“There were two speakers playing different songs.”

Taiga considered.

“That is actually criminal.”

“That's exactly it.”

The party was hosted on the second floor of the residence hall. It was no big event, at least that's what Caleb said, but Taiga could still eyeball at least forty people.
