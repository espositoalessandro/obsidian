### Chapter 11 - Thresholds

By Monday morning, Central Park looked like a park again.

The lanterns were gone. The temporary stage had disappeared. Somebody had removed the pumpkins before students could start kicking them around campus, which was probably wise.

Aaron and Sofia's upcoming date became a recurring subject in the Apollo group chat, until Aaron threatened to leave it. Enzo responded by changing the group name to **AARON'S SUPPORT NETWORK**, which lasted almost ten minutes before Gav changed it back.

Then November started doing what November apparently did at university: everything became due.

Professor Hall assigned their final paper and spent ten minutes explaining that three citations couldn't magically turn six pages of opinions into an academic paper. Chemistry added another lab report. Rao put the last Foundations assignment online and casually mentioned the final as if that wasn't the kind of sentence that ruined people's week.

Taiga started spending more time in the library. When the library got too crowded, he worked in his room instead. 
Caleb had developed an entire system involving two timers, a spreadsheet and scheduled breaks. Taiga refused to participate on principle and then slowly started using the breaks anyway.

Life outside classes and training kept happening too.
He ate with the team after practice sometimes. Stayed at House Apollo once after film because Gav had ordered too much food. Nora kept stealing the chair beside him in Political Institutions. 
Enzo slowly forgot the whole Dynamite story. Leo did not, obviously. At least he never brought it back in front of Enzo.
Caleb and Maya dragged him to Starbuffs one Sunday morning and then spent half of breakfast discussing whether a trail near Acropolis would still be usable after the first snow.

For almost three weeks, Olympus simply continued.

Soccer didn't slow down either. If anything, Mercer got worse.

Taiga played twenty-six minutes in one friendly, then thirty in the next. Nothing particularly interesting happened in either. 

Training was another story. 
The first weeks had been full of pauses. Stop. Reset. Explain where everyone was supposed to be. Explain why. Repeat.

Now Mercer mostly expected them to know. A bad press got a whistle and a surname. A bad run got a whistle and a stare.
Sometimes that was worse.

"Again!"

Taiga jogged back toward the touchline. They were working on a reduced-size pitch, eight against eight, with the space compressed enough that every bad touch immediately became somebody else's problem.

Scott rolled the ball to Gav. Taiga checked over his shoulder. Aaron was deeper, between him and goal, while Leo was moving inside.

Gav passed forward and Leo came toward the ball.

A month earlier, Taiga would've seen Leo moving and gone toward him too. Or passed straight to him. Or done something equally useful for the defender following him.

Now he saw the defender move with Leo. Space opened behind him and Taiga ran.

Leo received with his back to goal, touched the ball once and sent it around the defender into the space Taiga was already attacking.
Taiga reached it before Aaron could close him down and drove a low ball across the middle. Price got a foot to it, but Scott saved.

"Fuck."

"Again!" Mercer shouted.

They played another fifteen minutes before Mercer finally blew twice.

"Enough."

Several people immediately bent over. Enzo dropped onto his back.

"I have died."

"You say that every Thursday," Aaron said.

"Because every Thursday he kills me."

Mercer ignored them.

"Saturday. Northbridge. Same time as usual. I'll send the group tonight."

Players started toward the bottles. Taiga had barely taken a sip before Mercer called him.

"Akatora."

Taiga stopped and approached him.

"You're starting. Left wing."

Starting? For a second, he thought he'd misunderstood.

"Starting?" he repeated.

"Yeah."

Mercer waited, but nothing useful came out.

"Uh... Alright," he just said.

"Good." 

Mercer nodded and left.

Taiga went to the locker room, still metabolizing the thought of him actually starting. 
He lifted his shirt, shoved it into his bag and sat down to untie his boots.
Starting. Actual fucking starting. Not first team, sure, but... that should mean something. Right?

"You alright?"

Taiga snapped out and looked up. Leo was standing in front of him, towel around the waist and still damp from the shower.

"Mh? Yeah."

"You were staring at the floor like it had personally offended you or something."

"Maybe it had."

Leo smiled. "The nerve!"

He grabbed his stuff from his locker and sat beside him, drying off his hair with a towel.

"Did Mercer say something requiring that much computation?"

"Not really, I'm just—" Taiga gestured vaguely. "Starting against Northbridge."

Leo stopped and looked at him in surprise. "Seriously?"

"I hope?"

"Shit, that's a big milestone!"

Taiga pulled out his flip-flops and a towel from the bag.

"Yeah, big deal. It's a friendly, I've already played against them."

"Dude, it's your first start. You can celebrate a little."

He stopped for a second. "Maybe."

Leo frowned. "Okay, that was unexpectedly fast."

"You sound disappointed," said Taiga while finishing undressing. "Did you want me to say I'll probably fuck up?"

"N-No! Obviously, I just—" He stopped for half a second. "You know, I expected it."

"Well, I'll probably fuck it up anyway. What's the point in worrying about it now?"

Leo laughed. "Look at you."

Taiga shook his head and opened his bag to retrieve his shampoo and shower gel.
When he turned, Leo was still looking at him and smiling.

"What?"

"Nothing, nothing."

Taiga raised an eyebrow and turned to go to the showers.

---

When he went outside the locker room, he found Evan talking to Mercer. Mercer said something Taiga couldn't hear, Evan nodded, and then the conversation ended.

Mercer walked away, while Evan spotted Taiga. A bright smile appeared on his face.

"There he is."

"No. Don't."

"I just wanted to congratulate you."

"Don't do that."

Evan laughed and fell into step beside him. They headed toward the main campus. For a while they didn't talk about football at all. Evan complained about professors moving deadlines, other seniors having regular panic attacks and, in general, about having the feeling that Olympus was trying its best to get rid of him.

"You never told me what you're studying," Taiga asked at some point.

Evan shrugged. "Well, you never asked!"

Taiga blinked. Yeah, Caleb-virus was evidently airborne.

"I'm in Modern Languages major. Mostly Spanish and a bit of French here and here."

"Oh. That's pretty cool."

"It is! Besides, most people don't know but Spanish is the most spoken language in the world right now. So I hope it'll serve me well."

"Is it?"

"Just in our team, there's two native Spanish speaker, plus me."

Taiga frowned. "Who?"

"Who? Leo and Enzo of course. Didn't you know they were from Santo Domingo?"

Right. He didn't think about the implications of that.

"Yes, I just didn't think about it. They speak English normally, though."

"Yeah, they must have studied it since they were children, I've never actually asked them."

"Probably."

"What about you though? I only know you're in the same Chemistry class with Leo."

"I'm..." Taiga twitched his mouth. "Actually, I'm still undeclared."

Evan shrugged. "That's fine. I've declared my major almost in May when I was a freshmen."

"I'm considering Computer Science."

"Then you're already ahead of me. If I'm not wrong, Leo's CS as well right?"

"Unfortunately."

"Heh. Well, you'll have company at least."

"Unfortunately."

Evan smiled. They continued for a couple of minutes without speaking. Then Taiga asked:

"What are you doing after graduation?"

"I haven't got the faintest freaking idea."

Taiga stared at him. 

"Dunno, I'll probably end up in some company. Or maybe I'll be a teacher. Or even a tour guide operator, that'd be nice. Free travel!"

"Wh- Seriously, you don't know?"

"Well, it's not like I'm graduating tomorrow, right?"

"Still-"

"I think I'll try out a thing or two once I'm outta here. See what clicks. And what allows me to pay for an actual house."

Taiga stayed silent for a couple of seconds.

"Right. Fair enough."

Evan turned toward him. "How about you?"

"I've not even chosen a major."

"No, no. I mean, what are *you* doing once I'm out?"

What?

Ah. Right. The team.

"If you're talking about the team, I don't know. Besides, it's not up to me to decide."

"It kinda is, Taiga." Evan said.

"What you mean?"

"Look, I think you know that you're the only one who's training specifically for that position. Right?"

"Mercer said that it isn't decided yet."

"And that's true. I don't think you're ready for it. Yet."

They stopped outside the dorm building.

I think you know that during the tryouts there were way better player than you," Evan continued.

Taiga frowned. "Yeah. I know."

"Some of them where actual winger. Yet, here you are."  

He paused a second, then pointed at the dorm building.

"And there they are. "

Taiga didn't answer.

"Look, what I'm trying to say is that I think Mercer is betting on you. He could have picked another winger and keep you in development for the whole year or as a reserve."

Taiga gulped and, after a couple of seconds, said "That's a risky gamble."

Evan smiled. "Yeah. But Mercer is a good coach. He knows what he's doing. I had my doubts at the beginning, but I'm starting to see why he did that."

Then he put a hand over Taiga's shoulder and smiled.

"And that's why I want you to wear my shirt next year."

Taiga felt his stomach tying up tight. Evan gently patted his shoulder and removed the hand.

"No pressure, huh?" Taiga said.

"Oh no. Pressure. I know it'sucks, but I want you to know what's at stake. Just in case you thought you didn't matter that much."

"That's... fucked up."

Evan laughed hard. "Yeah."

Taiga smiled as well.

"...Thanks," he said, after a while.

"Anything for my little kohai."

Taiga blinked.

"Don't ever say that again."

Evan smiled. "Anyway, don't make that face. I'm not donating you my job because Mercer gave you one start in November."

"Yeah, I know."

"Good. Because I'm planning to remain significantly better than you until graduation."

Taiga laughed. "Go fuck yourself."

"There he is."

***

Somehow Nora was getting more unbearable than usual. As weeks passed, she spent more and more time in the library. Her army of notes, Post-it, books and colored tabs had grown enough to require half a table every time. 
The good thing about this was that she was so focused on her studies that annoying Taiga had become secondary.

The bad thing, however, was that whoever tried to talk to her while she was deep in a paragraph, basically risked their life.

Taiga discovered this once when he asked about Oliver while she was clearly struggling writing an assignment for Hall.

"He's better."

"Is he still in the ridiculous cone?"

"No."

"So, the infection has passed?"

"I don't know."

Taiga waited a couple of seconds.

"Is that how I usually sound like?"

She pointed a pen towards him with a murderous look.

"Taiga. Not now."

He raised his hands in surrender. "Alright, sorry."

Geez.

It was the day after his talk with Evan. Besides all the soccer stuff that was still sitting like a concrete block in his stomach, he was still thinking about how chill he was about his future.

*I'll try out a couple of things and see what sticks.*

That was a stupid way of picking decisions. 

Maybe, though, having an always opened tab of the school program sitting in the background with the "Computer Science" voice already selected in the dropdown was even more stupid.

Taiga glanced at Nora. She was resting her head on both hands while intensely looking at the screen of her laptop.

Pointless. He didn't need another fucking conversation about how he'd already picked two CS classes for the next semester.

His phone buzzed.

**Leo:** pls tell me you've already done rao's criminal assignment

Talk about bad timing.

**Taiga:** working on it

**Leo:** now?? 

**Taiga:** why

**Leo:** i need emotional support 😭😭

**Taiga:** wrong person

**Leo:** shared pain is still emotional support?

**Taiga:** what if i'm not in pain?

**Leo:** then it means you want to suffer alone
**Leo:** which i can respect 

Idiot.

**Taiga:** can you?

**Leo:** i could try really badly

Taiga laughed from his nose.

**Taiga:** library, second floor
**Taiga:** just pretend nora isn't here

**Leo:** why 🤨

**Taiga:** for your own safety

**Leo:** i'll be invisible

He didn't. Obviously. Nora just gave him an annoyed "Hi" when he arrived, then Leo earned two stares and one "shut up" in about ten minutes because he kept trying to make conversation with her.

Taiga just gave him an "I told you" look when he turned to him after that. Eventually, he finally pulled out his laptop.

"This assignment is pointlessly cruel," he said with a frown, looking at the screen with intense disgust.

Taiga leaned over to look at his screen. 

"What's your problem?"

"The fucking partition."

He scanned his code for a minute. The overall structure was right, except...

"Why did-"

Leo put a hand on his mouth. Taiga was slightly taken aback from the sudden and uncalled physical contact.

"No. Don't. I'll figure it out."

He removed his hand. After a couple of seconds, Taiga just shrugged.

"Suit yourself."

"Stick to emotional support."

"Alright. It's a stupid error."

Leo turned towards him and raised an eyebrow.

"Okay, that definitely needs some work."

Across the table, Nora snorted. Taiga glared at her.

"Mind your assignment."

She didn't say anything, but Taiga saw her exchanging a knowing glance with Leo. 
Suddenly, he missed Caleb and his room.

After fourteen minutes, Leo tried another run and the test bed was still red. His forehead went straight on the keyboard of the laptop.
Taiga glanced over that pathetic scene. The cursor was leaving a trail of "bbbbbbbb" on the file.
He still hadn't caught the error.

"Need more emotional support?"

Leo raised his middle finger without lifting his head.

"This is fucking pointless, why can't I just use other arrays?" he said.

"I guess it's part of the exercise."

Leo slowly turned his head, still on the keyboard.

"Who's side are you on?"

Taiga smiled. "I'll be there if you need me."

"Not until you wipe out that smug face of yours."

His smiled widened in response and turned to his own algorithm.

After a minute, Leo finally lifted his head. He cleared the "bbbb" mess and stared at the code for another couple of minutes.

"Fine. Alright, just tell me," he said.

Taiga leaned over. "It's the partition function."

Leo looked at it. "Yeah, thank you very much."

"You're assigning the index to the starting position of the array."

"And?"

"Then the first thing you do is cycle on the next one."

"How-"

He stared at the screen for a couple of seconds. Realization suddenly changed his expression.

"Oh, Jesus fucking Christ."

"You're welcome."

"I hate this shit."

"Then why did you pick CS?"

"Because messing with websites was funnier."

"You built a website?"

"Yeah, it was for a school project. We did some basic web stuff in class and they asked us to build a page for a school event."

"And that made you decide your future career?"

Leo shrugged. "I liked it enough to mess with basic stuff on my own."

"That's it?"

"Uh... yes?"

What the fuck was wrong with everyone?

He continued. "At orientation they told me that I could give it a try and eventually changed later if I didn't like it."

"That's the same shit they told me."

"I guess they just run on premade speeches."

Taiga snorted. "Yeah, probably."

"You're still undeclared?"

"Yeah."

Leo raised an eyebrow. After a couple of seconds, he just said "Alright."

Taiga narrowed his eyes.

"What?"

"What what?"

"You said alright, but your face said otherwise."

"I'm just surprised, that's it."

Jesus. Why was everybody so obsessed with him being undeclared?

"Why? People keeps telling me that it's normal to stay undeclared on first semester."

"I didn't mean like that, I know that it's perfectly fine. I'm just surprised because the other people I know that are still undeclared, haven't actually picked a subject they like."

"What do you mean?"

"I mean, they're doing all general classes. Chemistry, Calculus, Biology, you know. You said that you picked two CS classes next semester, though."

"That doesn't fucking mean I've decided anything."

Leo blinked. That came out way sharper than he intended. Taiga saw Nora glancing over as well now.
Great.
He took a breath.

"Sorry, I didn't mean to snap like that."

"It's alright."

And now the air was awkward. Nice job, dumbass. Leo went back to his assignment, since Taiga wasn't able to say anything else. He looked at Nora, who caught his glance and sarcastically arched her mouth upward.

Taiga opened the Olympus courses tab again and stared at it. 
What was wrong with him?
Almost everyone he had talked with was not entirely convinced about their future as well. Yet, none of them were being such dramatic pussies for a simple major declaration.
Instead, each time he opened the portal and looked at the *submit* button, he ended up staring at that fucking page for minutes, until eventually deciding he was still not sure enough.

Which was stupid. He had basically already decided to give CS a try.
Just like that stupid of Aaron. He had already decided he wanted to ask Sofia, yet every time he chickened out.
And Aaron asked him for advice? When he couldn't even bring himself to press a button that would change basically nothing?

Taiga rubbed his face, trying to snap out from the spiraling. God, how he wished he had a shutdown button for his stupid brain.

Shutdown?

*"Shut my brain off until I've gone far enough that I can't chicken out anymore."*

He laughed behind his hands. Nora looked up.

"You finally lost it?"

Taiga looked back, still smiling.

"That was long ago."

Then he opened the portal, quickly selected "Computer Science" from the major dropdown, and pressed *submit* before thinking too hard about it.

A loading spinner appeared over the page, followed by a green checkmark shortly after.
His profile now reading:

**TAIGA AKATORA**
**Current program:** *Computer Science*

He immediately closed the tab before his brain could catch up. Then he leaned back on the chair and took a deep breath.

Nothing absolutely changed. That was the worst part of it. Aaron had an actual excuse to chicken out.

Leo looked at him and then at Nora. "Should we worry?"

Nora looked confused as well. "Maybe?"

"Both of you can go fuck yourselves."

Nora turned back to Leo. "Okay, he's fine."

Leo chuckled. "Looks like it."
