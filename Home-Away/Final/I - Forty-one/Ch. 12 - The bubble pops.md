### Chapter 12 - The bubble pops

December arrived and it brought snow and finals.

The campus turned white after a rather intense blizzard went on for a whole night. Everybody had to deal with thirty centimeters of snow.

Unfortunately, people didn't have the time to wander around and actually enjoy the scenery. 
A few days before the finals week, no one asked "how are you?" anymore. The answer was always the same.

After the last training of the year, House Apollo's common room had stopped being a soccer house at all.

There were still boots by the door, jackets hanging over chairs and somebody's ball trapped under one of the couches, but every table in the common room was covered with laptops, notes and half-empty cups. Scott had taken the coffee table and was working through flashcards. Gav had three different books open around him. Enzo had somehow built a spreadsheet to calculate one of his grades and was now asking Leo why eleven slash eleven had turned into a date.

Taiga looked at the scene: Leo was desperately trying to make Enzo understand why the spreadsheet kept interpreting eleven slash eleven as a date, while Enzo was deflecting every bit of information trying to reach him.
That lasted fifteen minutes, before Leo surrendered and just fixed the damn sheet.
Taiga laughed once and went back to Hall's review.

He had taken the end of the long table near the wall. Political Institutions was Monday morning. Chemistry was Tuesday. Calculus was Thursday. Foundations came last on Friday.

Hall's review sheet was open in front of him with two pages of notes beside it.
The questions were broad enough to be annoying. Institutional legitimacy, emergency power, federalism, executive authority.
Monday was going to be cruel.

***

"I really hope I don't have to build any bridge here, Mr. Akatora."

Hall was smiling at him. Taiga put the sheet on the desk, on top of the others.

"I wrote them."

"We'll see."

That sounded ominous enough that Taiga wanted to leave as soon as possible.
Outside, Nora waited until the classroom door closed.

"That's basically affection from him," she said, smiling.

"That's basically a threat."

"Same thing with professors."

With Political Institutions over, Nora had already finished all her exams. She had a relaxed, smug expression that made her more unbearable than usual.

"When are you going home?" Taiga asked.

"My mum's gonna pick me up in a couple of hours. You?"

"My train's Saturday morning."

"So, still the same plan? Camp for a week, then home?"

"Yeah."

She smiled. "You're gonna miss me, won't you?"

Taiga just gave her an eloquent look.

"I knew it."

"Say hello to Oliver from me."

"You'll call me and say that yourself."

"You wish."

"You will."

Taiga shook his head. A whole semester had passed, yet she was still annoying as hell.

***

That afternoon brought the last soccer session of the semester. 

Mercer shortened the last session to less than an hour. It was cold enough that Enzo had spent most of warmup complaining about his fingers, even though Aaron kept reminding him he did not need his fingers to play soccer.

At the end, Mercer blew twice and called everyone in.
Gav started passing out sheets.

"Break program," Mercer said. "Three weeks. You are not going to lose all your fitness in three weeks."

Mercer continued explaining the schedule. Three sessions a week. Running, strength work, touches when possible. Nothing complicated.
Taiga took a sheet and glanced through it.

"Rest but don't slack off. I'm not starting on January trying to recover three weeks of nothing. So, follow the sheet or January will be harsh."

Someone groaned, but Mercer ignored them.

"You also know the conference season starts next semester. Olympus is playing in it, obviously. Some of those matches could bring in scouts from professional teams. We've got everything we need to compete near the top, so be ready for it."

Most of the freshmen exchanged surprised and scared looks.

"That's all. Get out of here."

***

Foundations ended Friday just after noon.
Professor Rao watched the last few students submit and then closed her laptop.

"If any of you email me tonight asking whether your final compiled correctly after you already submitted it, I will assume the answer is no."

Rao continued. "Enjoy the break. Touch grass. Preferably without trying to optimize it."

That made a few people laugh. Taiga felt a bit insulted, even if he couldn't exactly articulate why.

He left and stopped outside the building.

That was it.

No more classes. No training. Nothing due.
For the first time in months, there was nowhere he was supposed to be.

Campus was already half empty. He went back to the dorm, taking a quick detour to wander around the central park. 

Without the oppressing thought of the finals, the campus was actually really nice when covered in snow. Also, with half the people, it was also significantly quieter.
The fountain at the center was shut and the water frozen in the concrete basin.

Around him, there was almost nobody. Just a couple strolling in the distance and a few people reaching the entrance gate with suitcases and duffels.

Caleb had left that morning with Maya around nine. When Taiga got back to his room, the silence was different.

Caleb's chair was pushed all the way under the desk. His charger was gone. The glass of water that normally sat beside his laptop had disappeared with everything else.

Taiga dropped his bag and pulled the suitcase farther out from under the bed.

He had put two shirts inside when his phone buzzed.
Once.
Again.
Apparently peace had lasted too long.

**Leo:** taigaaaa
**Leo:** pls tell me you're still here 😭

**Taiga:** where else should i be?

**Leo:** i dunno everybody left at once 😭😭😭
**Leo:** i just finished fcs and suddenly i'm alone

**Taiga:** aren't you leaving with enzo tomorrow?

**Leo:** you really want to leave me all day with enzo? 
**Leo:** i already have to suffer through 10 hours tomorrow
**Leo:** i need a break

**Taiga:** like?

**Leo:** acropolis rink is open 

**Taiga:** congratulations to the rink

**Leo:** wanna come with me?

He stared at the screen for a second. Leo continued before he could answer:

**Leo:** PLEASE
**Leo:** apparently everybody suddenly has families 🥲

**Taiga:** you also have a family

**Leo:** yes and they have been informed i arrive tomorrow

Taiga looked at the two shirts in the suitcase.
Packing would take twenty minutes. Maybe thirty.
He could do it later.

**Taiga:** when

**Leo:** 4:10 central station?

**Taiga:** 4:20, i need a shower

**Leo:** thankssssss 😭😭😭😭😭

Taiga locked the phone, put another shirt into the suitcase and left it open.
