### Chapter 11

By Monday morning, Central Park looked like a park again.

The lanterns were gone. The temporary stage had disappeared. Somebody had removed the pumpkins before students could start kicking them around campus, which was probably wise.

Aaron and Sofia's upcoming date became a recurring subject in the Apollo group chat, until Aaron threatened to leave it. Enzo responded by changing the group name to **AARON'S SUPPORT NETWORK**, which lasted almost ten minutes before Gav changed it back.

Then November started doing what November apparently did at university: everything became due.

Professor Hall assigned their final paper and spent ten minutes explaining that three citations couldn't magically turn six pages of opinions into an academic paper. Chemistry added another lab report. Rao put the last Foundations assignment online and casually mentioned the final as if that wasn't the kind of sentence that ruined people's week.

Taiga started spending more time in the library. When the library got too crowded, he worked in his room instead. 
Caleb had developed an entire system involving two timers, a spreadsheet and scheduled breaks. Taiga refused to participate on principle and then slowly started using the breaks anyway.

Life outside classes and training kept happening too.
He ate with the team after practice sometimes. Stayed at House Apollo once after film because Gav had ordered too much food. Nora kept stealing the chair beside him in Political Institutions. 
Enzo slowly forgot the whole Dynamite story. Leo did not, obviously. At least he never brought it back in front of Enzo.
Caleb and Maya dragged him to Starbuffs one Sunday morning and then spent half of breakfast discussing whether a trail near Acropolis would still be usable after the first snow.

For almost three weeks, Olympus simply continued.

Soccer didn't slow down either. If anything, Mercer got worse.

Taiga played twenty-six minutes in one friendly, then thirty in the next. Nothing particularly interesting happened in either. 

Training was another story. 
The first weeks had been full of pauses. Stop. Reset. Explain where everyone was supposed to be. Explain why. Repeat.

Now Mercer mostly expected them to know. A bad press got a whistle and a surname. A bad run got a whistle and a stare.
Sometimes that was worse.

"Again!"

Taiga jogged back toward the touchline. They were working on a reduced-size pitch, eight against eight, with the space compressed enough that every bad touch immediately became somebody else's problem.

Scott rolled the ball to Gav. Taiga checked over his shoulder. Aaron was deeper, between him and goal, while Leo was moving inside.

Gav passed forward and Leo came toward the ball.

A month earlier, Taiga would've seen Leo moving and gone toward him too. Or passed straight to him. Or done something equally useful for the defender following him.

Now he saw the defender move with Leo. Space opened behind him and Taiga ran.

Leo received with his back to goal, touched the ball once and sent it around the defender into the space Taiga was already attacking.
Taiga reached it before Aaron could close him down and drove a low ball across the middle. Price got a foot to it, but Scott saved.

"Fuck."

"Again!" Mercer shouted.

They played another fifteen minutes before Mercer finally blew twice.

"Enough."

Several people immediately bent over. Enzo dropped onto his back.

"I have died."

"You say that every Thursday," Aaron said.

"Because every Thursday he kills me."

Mercer ignored them.

"Saturday. Northbridge. Same time as usual. I'll send the group tonight."

Players started toward the bottles. Taiga had barely taken a sip before Mercer called him.

"Akatora."

Taiga stopped and approached him.

"You're starting. Left wing."

Starting? For a second, he thought he'd misunderstood.

"Starting?" he repeated.

"Yeah."

Mercer waited, but nothing useful came out.

"Uh... Alright," he just said.

"Good." 

Mercer nodded and left.

Taiga went to the locker room, still metabolizing the thought of him actually starting. 
He lifted his shirt, shoved it into his bag and sat down to untie his boots.
Starting. Actual fucking starting. Not first team, sure, but... that should mean something. Right?

"You alright?"

Taiga snapped out and looked up. Leo was standing in front of him, towel around the waist and still damp from the shower.

"Mh? Yeah."

"You were staring at the floor like it had personally offended you or something."

"Maybe it had."

Leo smiled. "The nerve!"

He grabbed his stuff from his locker and sat beside him, drying off his hair with a towel.

"Did Mercer say something requiring that much computation?"

"Not really, I'm just—" Taiga gestured vaguely. "Starting against Northbridge."

Leo stopped and looked at him in surprise. "Seriously?"

"I hope?"

"Shit, that's a big milestone!"

Taiga pulled out his flip-flops and a towel from the bag.

"Yeah, big deal. It's a friendly, I've already played against them."

"Dude, it's your first start. You can celebrate a little."

He stopped for a second. "Maybe."

Leo frowned. "Okay, that was unexpectedly fast."

"You sound disappointed," said Taiga while finishing undressing. "Did you want me to say I'll probably fuck up?"

"N-No! Obviously, I just—" He stopped for half a second. "You know, I expected it."

"Well, I'll probably fuck it up anyway. What's the point in worrying about it now?"

Leo laughed. "Look at you."

Taiga shook his head and opened his bag to retrieve his shampoo and shower gel.
When he turned, Leo was still looking at him and smiling.

"What?"

"Nothing, nothing."

Taiga raised an eyebrow and turned to go to the showers.

---

When he went outside the locker room, he found Evan talking to Mercer. Mercer said something Taiga couldn't hear, Evan nodded, and then the conversation ended.

Mercer walked away, while Evan spotted Taiga. A bright smile appeared on his face.

"There he is."

"No. Don't."

"I just wanted to congratulate you."

"Don't do that."

Evan laughed and fell into step beside him. They headed toward the main campus. For a while they didn't talk about football at all. Evan complained about professors moving deadlines, other seniors having regular panic attacks and, in general, about having the feeling that Olympus was trying its best to get rid of him.

"You never told me what you're studying," Taiga asked at some point.

Evan shrugged. "Well, you never asked!"

Taiga blinked. Yeah, Caleb-virus was evidently airborne.

"I'm in Modern Languages major. Mostly Spanish and a bit of French here and here."

"Oh. That's pretty cool."

"It is! Besides, most people don't know but Spanish is the most spoken language in the world right now. So I hope it'll serve me well."

"Is it?"

"Just in our team, there's two native Spanish speaker, plus me."

Taiga frowned. "Who?"

"Who? Leo and Enzo of course. Didn't you know they were from Santo Domingo?"

Right. He didn't think about the implications of that.

"Yes, I just didn't think about it. They speak English normally, though."

"Yeah, they must have studied it since they were children, I've never actually asked them."

"Probably."

"What about you though? I only know you're in the same Chemistry class with Leo."

"I'm..." Taiga twitched his mouth. "Actually, I'm still undeclared."

Evan shrugged. "That's fine. I've declared my major almost in May when I was a freshmen."

"I'm considering Computer Science."

"Then you're already ahead of me. If I'm not wrong, Leo's CS as well right?"

"Unfortunately."

"Heh. Well, you'll have company at least."

"Unfortunately."

Evan smiled. They continued for a couple of minutes without speaking. Then Taiga asked:

"What are you doing after graduation?"

"I haven't got the faintest freaking idea."

Taiga stared at him. 

"Dunno, I'll probably end up in some company. Or maybe I'll be a teacher. Or even a tour guide operator, that'd be nice. Free travel!"

"Wh- Seriously, you don't know?"

"Well, it's not like I'm graduating tomorrow, right?"

"Still-"

"I think I'll try out a thing or two once I'm outta here. See what clicks. And what allows me to pay for an actual house."

Taiga stayed silent for a couple of seconds.

"Right. Fair enough."

Evan turned toward him. "How about you?"

"I've not even chosen a major."

"No, no. I mean, what are *you* doing once I'm out?"

What?

Ah. Right. The team.

"If you're talking about the team, I don't know. Besides, it's not up to me to decide."

"It kinda is, Taiga." Evan said, and stopped.

"Why?"

"Look, I think you know that you're the only one who's training specifically for that position. Right?"

"Mercer said that it isn't decided yet."

"And that's true. But during the tryouts there were way better player than you."

Taiga frowned. "I know."

"Some of them where actual winger. Yet, here you are."  

He paused a second, then pointed at the dorm building.

"And there they are."

Taiga didn't answer.

"Look, what I'm trying to say is that Mercer is betting on you. He could have picked another winger and keep you in development for the whole year. Even more. Instead, he kept you. He chose to focus on you for that specific position."

Taiga gulped and, after a couple of seconds, said "That's a risky gamble."

Evan smiled. "Yeah. But Mercer is a good coach. He knows what he's doing. That's why I trust you."

Then he put a hand over Taiga's shoulder.

"And that's why I want you to wear my shirt next year."

Taiga felt his stomach tying up tight. Evan gently patted his shoulder and removed the hand.

"No pressure, huh?" Taiga said

"Oh no. Pressure. I know it'sucks, but I want you to know what's at stake. Just in case you thought you didn't matter that much."

"That's fucked up."

Evan laughed hard. "Yeah."
