### Chapter 9 - Thresholds

Mercer finally blew his whistle.

"Okay. Enough, everyone."

People exhaled. Someone dropped to the ground; others just rested their hands on their knees.

"No match this weekend, as you already know," coach continued. "Take it easy and rest. No film on Monday either."

"Is this Halloween or Christmas?" said Aaron.

"We got our Santa Coach!" answered Enzo.

Luckily for him, Mercer had already gone.

Taiga went to the touchline to pick up his bottle. People started going into the locker room while he collected the cones.

A small group of players stayed outside, chatting on the field. Leo was among them.

Taiga dropped the mesh bag into storage and went back to the field. As he approached the group of players, Leo noticed him. A bright smile appeared on his face, and he ran toward Taiga.

"Hey! You're staying as well?"

Taiga frowned. "I said I'd come."

Leo shrugged, the smile still there. "I know."

"Then why do you sound surprised?"

"It's—"

He paused for half a second, then waved it off.

"Forget it." Leo grinned. "Glad you're here!"

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "Yeah, sure."

The bus arrived and kids immediately started pouring onto the field.

"FORTY-ONE!"

Taiga turned. Little Matt was running straight at him.

Leo tilted his head. "I told you this would happen."

Taiga sighed. "Yeah."

The clinic started, and the youth coach divided them into groups. Taiga took one of the dribbling groups while Leo handled another. The hard part wasn't getting them to do the exercise. It was keeping them in line.

"Taiga! My shoe's untied!" one boy cried at one point.

Taiga sighed and crouched.

"Okay, you're good now."

He stood and glanced at the other groups. Leo was grinning at him and giving him a thumbs-up.

Taiga flipped him off in response, then lowered his hand half a second later when he remembered there were children around.

That made the asshole's grin even wider.

The clinic lasted about an hour. As the sun began to set, the kids rushed back toward the bus.

Some of them waved at him.

"Bye, Taiga!"

Taiga smiled and waved back.

"You definitely have a certain charm with kids."

He turned. Leo was walking toward him.

"I'm charming, period."

Leo chuckled. "Of course."

They finished picking up the cones and went to the locker room with the others.

"So, Hunter's exhibit is tomorrow, right?" Leo asked while untying his shoes.

Taiga pulled off his thermal shirt. "Yeah."

"How far is his college?"

"About four hours by train."

"Shit. You're staying there?"

"Yeah, we booked a hotel nearby."

"You're coming back for the party?"

"I'm taking an early train Saturday. I should be back around noon."

Leo nodded. "Cool."

Taiga grabbed his towel and went to the showers. Hot water was a blessing after training.

Leo took the stall beside his.

"You're dressing up for the party?" Taiga asked.

"Obviously! I love dressing up."

"Figured. Which one?"

"Mmm..."

Leo went silent for a couple of seconds.

"Not telling you," he said.

"Why not?"

"Spoilers."

"Fuck off."

"Eheh. What about you?"

Taiga shampooed his hair. "Don't think I'll have the time for anything."

"It doesn't have to be fancy, you know."

"I know. I just have standards."

Leo laughed. "You can literally just cut two holes in a white sheet."

Taiga considered it. Surely that way nobody would bother him.

"I’ll keep it as my escape plan."

"I'll keep an eye out for ghosts, then."

Taiga smiled.

Idiot.

They had agreed to meet at the main station and take the local line to the university together.

**Keitaro:** 😅 fair enough

His train pulled in at 11:44. Close enough.

**Taiga:** where are you?

**Keitaro:** We're on a bench under the arrivals screen. The big one.

**Taiga:** coming

Taiga found them a couple of minutes later. Hiro saw him first and raised a hand.

"TAIGA!"

Keitaro grabbed his hand and lowered it. "Shh, Hiro! There are people here."

Taiga sighed and walked over. He tried to suppress a smile.

"Hey."

Keitaro hugged him before he could prepare for it. Taiga let him.

"You look good!" he said.

"Yeah, I've never seen you in actual clothes," Hiro added.

"It's just a shirt."

Keitaro smiled. "You look good in a shirt then!"

They were wearing shirts as well. He had gotten the dress code right, at least.

Then Natsumi, Lee and Eduard arrived.

...Never mind.

Natsumi had gone with a proper suit, even a freaking bow tie. Lee also had a simple shirt, but he went heavy with the gel on his hair.

Then there was Eduard.
He appeared from the platform dressed like he’d misunderstood _student art exhibition_ as _fashion week_.

Rust-colored pinstripes from shoulders to ankles, wide trousers, and a blue denim shirt with an enormous collar spread over the jacket. Amber glasses covered half his face and, on top of that, he carried a cream leather bag with golden hardware.

But the best part was that he walked like the station was a freaking runway.

"Hellooooo~"

They went silent for a couple of seconds. Keitaro then said:

"Woah, Eduard! You look amazing."

Eduard giggled and did a small pirouette on one foot. 

"Aw, thanks darling. By the way, you look fabulous as well!"

Then he looked at Taiga and gasped, putting a hand dramatically over his mouth.

"Oh. My. God. Am I seeing things?"

He rushed toward him with his mouth open, slowly lowering his glasses.

"Taiga, dear, when did you decide to start looking like an actual model? You absolutely slay in that shirt!"

There he was.

"Hey Ed. You look good."

Lee adjusted his glasses. "The rust and blue work particularly well together because they sit at opposite positions on the color spectrum. The contrast makes both colors appear more pronounced."

"Right, of course. Hi Lee."

"Good morning to you as well," he said, smiling.

Natsumi approached as well. He was carrying flowers. 

"Damn it, Natsumi! You two are making us look way underdressed now," said Hiro.

Natsumi looked worried.

"Oh, sorry, I didn't mean to make you uncomfortable. Did I overdo it?"

Keitaro smiled. "Not at all, you look great. Ignore Hiro."

Taiga's phone buzzed. Yoichi.

"What?"

"Where the fuck are you?"

"Where Keitaro wrote they were."

"I don't see any freaking screen."

Taiga sighed. "Where are you?"

"I don't fucking know."

"What's around you?"

It took Taiga ten minutes to guide Yoichi to them.
He arrived with cargo jeans, heavy boots and a black sherpa trucker jacket. A duffel bag was hanging from his shoulder with another smaller one carried by hand.

"What the fuck is Spaghetti-Boy wearing?"

"Uh, excuse me? I'll have you know this is from the latest Eastwood fall collection."

Taiga looked at Yoichi. "What the fuck are *you* wearing?"

"Clothes. Duh."

"Yeah, camp clothes."

Keitaro and Hiro giggled.

"The fuck are you laughing at?"

Natsumi smiled. "You look great, Yoichi."

Yoichi turned and stared at him.

"You look like a penguin."

Natsumi gave a sheepish smile. "Well—thanks?"

Then Yoichi held the smaller duffel out to Taiga. It was pretty heavy indeed. 

Taiga frowned and opened the zip. It was full of something wrapped in a plastic bag, but he couldn't tell what it was.

"What the heck is this?"

Yoichi shrugged. "Told you, I don't know."

Taiga exhaled and closed the bag.

"We should drop our luggage at the hotel first," Natsumi said.

"Let's go."

---

They reached Hunter's campus by 2:30. Taiga stared at the entrance gate: definitely less pretentious than Olympus'. 
At least this one didn't have statues.

Hunter's school occupied an old industrial block that somebody had converted by removing anything practical and replacing it with exposed brick, concrete and huge windows.

Yoichi stopped on the sidewalk.

"What the fuck is this?"

Keitaro lifted his camera.

"Art school?"

"It looks unfinished."

"I think that's kind of the point."

The main building's lobby had been decorated for Halloween without technically becoming Halloween. Black paper shapes hung from steel cables. Projected shadows moved across one wall. A student in white face paint stood behind a welcome table beneath a sign reading:

**UNQUIET: STUDENT EXHIBITION — FEAR, UNEASE, THE UNCANNY**

Another student walked past wearing a skirt made of what looked like translucent plastic strips.

Yoichi watched them go.

"Are we sure this isn't the costume thing?"

"That's tomorrow," Taiga said.

"Right."

"Guys!"

Taiga and the others turned. Hunter was walking towards them, frantically waving one hand.

"You actually came!"

Hiro grinned and lifted a pastry box. "I brought emotional support!"

Natsumi held out the flowers. "And I brought something a little more traditional."

Hunter took a moment to process.

"You didn't have to bring anything! I'm just happy that you are actually here."

Natsumi smiled. "It's nothing."

Hunter looked at all of them. "You all look amazing. I still can't believe you're actually here! Hold on a sec, I'll drop these things and show you around."

Then he ran off with the flowers and the box.

Eduard then raised a hand. "Was I the only one who noticed the eye bags? Anyone?"

Lee answered, "Yes. He was clearly sleep-deprived. Although, given the occasion and Hunter's nature, I'd consider this a foreseeable outcome."

"Aw, poor Hunter. Pressure must be terrible for him right now," said Keitaro.

Yoichi crossed his arms.

"Tch. Isn't art all about showing people your work? He'd better get used to it."

Hiro scratched his head. "I totally get him. When my professors taste what I cook, I always expect them to throw up at some point. Like, actually throw up."

"Because you're a fucking drama queen, Torch-Head." 

"And you have the sensibility of a cat, with the brain of a dog."

Natsumi stepped between them. "Okay, don't start you two."

Hunter returned after a few minutes out of breath. "Okay..." He took another breath. "We can go now."

"You didn't need to rush," Keitaro said.

"I- I didn't want you guys to wait. A- Anyways, follow me, this way!"

He was definitely nervous.

Hunter guided them through a small park and into the biggest building, apparently dedicated entirely to exhibitions. 
His section was on the far wall of the second room. Five pieces were illuminated by a yellow light hanging from the ceiling. 

The first three sat close together beneath a single block of text. Human figures without faces, drawn in charcoal.
One showed a person sitting on the edge of a bed, shoulders bent forward. Another focused on hands gripping fabric. The third was a back turned toward a window where the outside had been left almost completely white.

The fourth was watercolor. It pictured an empty shoreline, narrow dock, water so dark that the reflection looked like a second sky.

The last one was a graphite drawing of a hallway, with an open door at the far end. Light was coming through it.

Taiga wasn't exactly an artist, but those were ominous as fuck.

"This guy looks like shit," Yoichi said, pointing at the first one.

"It's not a guy, stupid dog," said Hiro next to him.

"Then what the fuck is it?"

"It's..."

Hiro frowned.

"I don't know. It looks human, I guess."

Hunter giggled. "It's not supposed to be anything specific."

Yoichi grinned. "Then it could be a guy." 

Taiga was looking at the watercolor canvas. The shoreline made him think of Camp Buddy, although it clearly wasn't. Hunter approached. 

"Yeah... I took inspiration from a draft I made on the pier the first year."

"Why did you change it?"

He shrugged. "Because I didn't want it to be a photo. I just wanted it to carry familiarity even if the place is different."

Taiga frowned. "Doesn't this only work for you, though?"

"Well, it worked for you too, didn't it?"

He looked back at the painting.

"Yeah."

Hunter smiled. "Then mission accomplished."

"It's still two people." 

"I like to think that this kind of view carries memories for everyone."

Taiga looked at the title tag.

*Greatest Memories*

He smiled.

"Keitaro is gonna sue you, you know?"

Hunter laughed. "I asked him."

After a while, they all ended up gathering around the last piece.

"What is this one?" Keitaro asked.

"It's- Uh... a hallway."

"Oh my God! It reminds me of a hideous nightmare I had a couple of nights ago!" Eduard said.

Lee raised an eyebrow.

"The one where you woke up and found a burglar in your wardrobe room stealing all your clothes?"

"Don't even say it!"

Keitaro took a photo, then lowered the camera.

"What does it represent?"

"It's simply a hallway outside a lecture hall. I kept walking past it around the same time every afternoon," Hunter said. "I liked the light, but I tweaked the proportions a bit."

Taiga looked at the open door.

"That's it?"

Yoichi pointed at it.

"Door's creepy, though."

Hunter shrugged. "Not every art piece carries deep messages. Sometimes the artist just likes it."

A woman with a shaved sidecut appeared and wrapped one arm around Hunter's shoulders.

"Hey, you're still alive."

"Y-Yeah, mostly."

“You finally stopped moving that third piece around?”  

“Yes.”  

“Oh, thank God.”

She looked at the group.

"Your friends?"

Hunter nodded.

"Yeah. This is Taiga, Keitaro, Hiro, Natsumi, Yoichi, Eduard and Lee. Guys, Elena. We share a couple of classes."

"Nice to meet you all! Oh, Hunter, before I forget, Miles wanted to go into town after the exhibit. You in?"

"Oh- That's nice, but... I'll be with them tonight."

Elena smiled. "Yeah, I figured. Well, good luck then! I better get back to my work before Marlowe shows up."

"Eheh, yeah. Later!"

Elena ran off.
Natsumi put a hand on Hunter's shoulder.

"You could've gone with them, you know."

"No way! You guys came all the way here! I can always hang out with them."

Natsumi smiled. "It's good to see you've settled well."

"Y-Yeah. They are good people."

Taiga listened to all of that. He smiled, but decided to say nothing.

"The perspective compression is intentional?" Lee asked at some point.

Hunter turned towards him and blinked.

"Uh- yeah, mostly. The real hallway is longer."

"I thought so. The light transition would be weaker at the original depth."

Hunter stared for half a second. Then he gave a sheepish smile.

"Yeah. Exactly."

Eduard, from the next wall, called, "Lee, stop academically interrogating the artist."

"I asked one question."

"And somehow it had to defend a thesis."

Hunter laughed.

“He's fine.”

Taiga watched Lee nod once and move to the next work. Apparently everybody had brought their own version of support.

The exhibition slowly filled. By five-thirty, Taiga understood why Hunter had sounded nervous. People kept stopping, looking, talking. 
Sometimes to Hunter. Sometimes about Hunter's work while Hunter stood close enough to hear. 
Horrible.
Taiga would rather play ninety minutes under Mercer's direct observation than stand beside something he'd made while strangers described what it meant. Hunter did his best. His hands kept finding each other in front of him and he laughed too quickly at compliments.

Once, after an older man spent five minutes explaining the symbolism of the empty corridor, Hunter looked over at Taiga with eyes that said *do not fucking laugh*. 
Taiga and Hiro had to leave the room. They ended up beside a window in the third gallery, then they burst out laughing.

"Okay, I wasn't going to laugh while Hunter could see us, but 'fear of adulthood' is a lot to put on somebody who drew a hallway because the light looked nice," Hiro said between laughs.

A few minutes later, Hunter popped in and narrowed his eyes at them.

All three of them laughed again.

Later, the exhibit finally ended. Hunter was visibly exhausted, but had a satisfied look on his face. He took them to a place right off campus.

Hiro gave him approximately four minutes after they sat down.

“Okay. Now that strangers are no longer diagnosing your hallway symbolism, how do you actually feel?”

Hunter exhaled. "Tired. Also hungry."

Hiro raised an eyebrow. "Emotionally?"

Keitaro laughed. "You couldn't let him eat first?"

"I gave him four minutes."

Hunter smiled. "I guess... relieved?"

Edward put his phone on the table. "If you wanted a dramatic transformation, we could still arrange one. New outfit. Lighting cue. Maybe a fan."

"Ahah, no I'm alright thanks."

Lee said, “The work was completed before tonight. The exhibition only changed who could see it.”

Eduard turned slowly toward him. “That was almost poetic.”

“It was an observation.”

Hunter looked between them. “Weirdly, that's helpful.”

Then food arrived and silence followed. At least for a couple of seconds.
Hiro complained about a knife-skills practical turning carrots into geometry homework. Natsumi complained about a coach changing pool times without warning. Eduard called a vending-machine sandwich Lee had eaten *triangular sadness* while Lee continued insisting it contained an adequate amount of protein.

Then Hunter asked to see Keitaro's umbrella-repair lady photographs. Keitaro brightened and passed over his phone.
