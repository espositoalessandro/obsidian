### Chapter 9 - Thresholds

Mercer finally blew his whistle.

"Okay. Enough, everyone."

People exhaled. Someone dropped into the ground, others just rested on their knees. 

"No match this weekend, as you already know," coach continued. "Take it easy and rest. No movie on Monday either."

"Is this Halloween or Christmas?" said Aaron.

"We got our Santa Coach!" answered Enzo.

Luckily for him, Mercer had already gone.
Taiga went to the touchline to pick up his bottle. People started going into the lockers, while he went collecting cones.
A small group of players stay outside, chatting on the field. Leo was among them. 

Taiga dropped the mesh bag into the storage and went back to the field. As he approached the group of player, Leo noticed him. A bright smile appeared on his face and he ran towards Taiga.

"Hey! You're staying as well?"

Taiga frowned. "I said I'd come."

Leo shrugged, his smile didn't leave. "I know."

"Then why do you sound surprised?"

"It's-"

He paused half a second, then waved it off.

"Forget it." Leo said, then he grinned. "Glad you're here!"

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "Yeah, sure."

The bus arrived and kids immediately started pouring into the field.

"FORTY-ONE!"

Taiga turned: the little Matt was running straight at him.

Leo leaned his head. "I told you this would happen."

Taiga sighed. "Yeah."

The clinic started and Mercer assistant divided them into groups. Taiga assisted the group with dribbling drills while Leo was managing another one. The hard part was not making them doing the right exercise, but to keep them in line.

"Taiga! My shoe untied!", cried one boy at some time.

Taiga sighed and crouched. 

"Okay, you're good now."

He stood up and glanced at the other groups. He saw Leo grinning at him and raising one thump up.
Taiga just flipped him off in response, then hid the hand half a second later because he remembered there were children.
That made the little fucker's grin even wider.

The clinic lasted about one hour. As the sun began to set, children rushed again towards the bus.
Some of them waved at him.

"Bye Taiga!"

Taiga smiled and waved them off as well.

"You definitely got a charm for children."

He turned, Leo was walking towards him.

"I am charming, fullstop."

Leo chuckled. "Of course."

They finished picking up all the cones and went to the lockers with the others.

"So, Hunter exhibit is tomorrow right?", asked Leo while untying his shoes.

Taiga pulled off his thermal shirt. "Yeah."

"How far is his college?"

"About four hours by train more or less."

"Shit. You're staying there?"

"Yeah, we booked a room right outside it."

"You're coming back for the party?"

"I'm taking the train pretty early on Saturday. I'll be here early in the morning."

Leo nodded. "Cool."

Taiga grabbed his towel and went to the showers. Hot water was a bless with dried sweat on the skin. 
Leo took the stall beside his.

"You're dressing up for the party?", asked Taiga.

"Obviously! I love getting in costumes."

"Figured. Which one?"

"Mmm..."

Leo went silent a couple of seconds.

"Not telling you", he said.

"Why not?"

"Spoilers."

"Fuck off."

"Eheh, what about you?"

Taiga shampooed his hair. "Don't think I'll have the time for anything."

"It doesn't have to be fancy, you know."

"I know. I just have standards."

Leo laughed. "You can literally just cut two holes in a white sheet."

Taiga considered. Surely that way nobody would bother him.

"I'll keep it as an eventual escape plan."

"I'll look up for ghosts, then."

Taiga smiled.

Idiot.
