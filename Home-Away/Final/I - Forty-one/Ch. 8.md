### Chapter 8

"AARON! WHAT THE FUCK?"

Taiga covered his mouth to hide his laugh. Scott wasn't exactly happy.

Aaron raised a hand towards the goal, face red.
Enzo grinned.

"Thanks for the donation!"

Aaron turned. "Fuck you."

Then Mercer whistled.

"Bennett."

He closed his eyes and turned towards the coach.

"...Yeah."

"If the backheel pass works, you're clever. If it doesn't, you've just given Reyes the ball five meters from goal."

Aaron lowered his head. "It usually works."

"Today it didn't. This is soccer, not roulette."

A couple of people laughed. Aaron muttered something Taiga couldn't hear and jogged back into position.
When the next ball reached him, he played the easiest pass available straight into Taiga's feet.

Taiga looked at him, raising an eyebrow.

"What?" Aaron said.

Taiga sent it back. "Nothing."

"That was a judgmental nothing."

"You're imagining things."

"You've been here long enough to become annoying."

Taiga shrugged and smiled.

"Pretty sure I arrived that way."

Then Mercer whistled and they restarted. Aaron looked pissed for about a minute.

Apparently, putting on an Apollo shirt didn't change people that much. Aaron was still Aaron: a show-off, taking things personally and getting over them seconds later. Enzo was still lazily chaotic, whether there was a ball involved or not. Evan wore the same proud smile whenever one of the younger players got something right.

"TAIGA! GO INSIDE!"

...Scott was another matter.

Gav pointed at the grass between Taiga and the touchline.

"You're standing in the right place at the wrong time."

Taiga frowned. "The fuck does that mean?"

"Means position isn't a point; it's a relationship."

He stepped closer and started drawing with the toe of his boot.

"You, their fullback, Leo. That's a triangle. Right now you're flattening it."

Taiga stared at the lines in the grass.

"I liked soccer better when geometry wasn't involved."

"I'm just explaining why you're making Leo's life harder."

Taiga was left to decode whatever the hell Gav meant.

Mercer whistled and he tried again. He saw Leo coming, their fullback still there. 
Okay, timing.
Leo came toward the ball. Their fullback glanced at Taiga, then shifted a step inside with him.
Taiga saw it and went.
By the time the defender turned back, Taiga already had the space.
The ball reached him on the run. Two touches forward, then he cut it back inside to Leo.
Taiga saw him carrying two defenders, creating enough space for Enzo to run. Simple pass; Enzo only had to put his foot on it. Clean as fuck.

Leo was usually pure chaos. Off the pitch, he could forget something existed the second it left his sight. On it, nothing seemed to escape him.
Two months ago, Taiga would've noticed the pass. Now he noticed the two defenders Leo had dragged with him before making it.
Yeah, he was good.

If only he didn't spend half of his oxygen allowance on talking.


---

Taiga had just pulled a clean shirt over his head when Enzo climbed onto the bench between two rows of lockers. 

"Ladies and gentlemen."

There he went.

"You dumped me last weekend. Since I love you all, I'll give you a chance to redeem yourselves."

Scott immediately threw his clothes into his bag and grabbed his shoes.
Most of the others simply ignored him.

"Friday. We invade Arcadia."

Aaron looked interested.

"Arcadia is actually nice."

Enzo pointed and smiled. "One in."

"I didn't say yes."

"You did, implicitly."

Leo raised his head. "I'm in, but only if we go to the arcade!"

"We will. That's two. Ask Zoe as well."

Then he pointed. "Gav."

"No, I have an assignment."

"Due?"

"Monday."

"Then you've got all weekend. Three in. Scott?"

Gav didn't even try to argue back. Scott was tying his shoes as quickly as he could.
Not fast enough, though.

"I- Uh... I don't know?"

"Good, four."

"I'm in as well," Evan said.

Enzo looked absurdly pleased with himself. Then he pointed at Taiga.

"Forty-one!"

Taiga closed his locker.

"What."

"You ever been to Arcadia?"

"No."

"Then we're fixing that."

Taiga looked at him. 

"Fix what?"

"Doctors say going out is good for your health, so you must come."

"No."

Enzo didn't even flinch.

"Good. So, it's me, Leo, Aaron and potentially Sofia—"

"I didn't say yes."

"—Scott is spiritually confirmed, Gav is in denial—"

"I'm not coming."

"-Zoe hasn't answered yet and Evan. That's settled."

He looked satisfied, even if half of the people he mentioned didn't actually agree.

Taiga pushed the door open and stepped into the corridor. His phone buzzed inside his bag before he'd made it ten steps.

**⚽️ Apollo ☀️**

**Enzo:** friday 6:25 olympus station  
**Enzo:** arcadia  
**Enzo:** attendance mandatory unless you have a very good excuse

**Gav:** I have an assignment.

**Enzo:** denied

Taiga smiled and put the phone away.

---

As the end of October approached, decorations slowly started accumulating all over the campus. 

Olympus was definitely serious about it: plastic pumpkins covering almost every post light, skeleton masks applied on the statues, graves in the patches of central park and eerie lights at night. And, of course, actual pumpkins with candles inside everywhere.

Every conversation around campus was dominated by two topics: the Halloween party and spring registration.

Taiga hated talking about both.

"So, registrations are open."

Nora was walking beside him on the way to the library.

"Good for them," Taiga answered.

"Have you decided slash have any idea slash even considered what to pick?"

"Yes."

Nora narrowed her eyes.
"Okay, I set that up too easily, my bad."

Taiga smiled.

"I'm considering going full STEM next semester."

"I'm not surprised."

"Why?"

She shrugged.

"Call it female instinct."

"That's bullshit."

"So which classes?"

Taiga pulled out the schedule Park made for him.

**Data Structures**  
**Discrete Structures**  
**Calculus II**  
**General Chemistry II**

Nora looked at it for a couple of seconds. 

"Major?"

"Not yet."

She exhaled.

"You don't need me to tell you that this is basically a CS major, right?"

"Right, I don't."

Nora chuckled. 

"Okay. Anyway, it's a good program."

"And you?"

"I've chosen Political Science as my major."

"Of course you have."

"What can I say? I love arguing."

"That I totally agree on."

"Oh, fuck you."

They sat at their usual spot on the second floor. Apparently, the library was sacred enough to be spared the decorations.
That, or the librarian kept destroying all of them until students eventually stopped trying.

Halfway through his Chemistry assignment, Taiga saw Nora grimacing at her phone. 

"What?"

She looked at him. "Oliver isn't feeling well."

"Who's Oliver?"

Nora turned her phone. A beagle was sitting in the back of a car wearing a cone. He didn't look happy about it.

"Your dog?"

"Yeah. Shouldn't be anything serious. The cone is amazing, though."

"He doesn't seem to agree."

Nora laughed. "Definitely not."

"What happened?"

"Probably some skin infection."

Taiga nodded and went back to his assignment. On a separate tab, there was the Olympus website with the major declaration page.
Just there, opened.

After a while, Nora asked, "You never actually told me how the hike went, by the way."

Taiga looked up. "You didn't ask."

He froze a second at what he just said. Was Caleb infecting him too?

"Okay, then: how was the hike Taiga?"

There was a hint of mockery in her tone.

"It was good. Full of people, but the view was nice."

"Did you go to the actual Acropolis ruins?"

"Nope."

"Good. We're going there some time."

"That sounded threatening."

She simply shrugged. "I want to see them too."

"This doesn't justify the military tone."

Nora smiled. "You didn't say no, though."

"You didn't give me a time."

"Okay, next Sunday?"

"No."

She laughed, "Fuck you."

---

Taiga reached the station at 6:13. Scott was already there, sitting on a bench.

"So you came?"
"H-Hey, you came"

They stopped for a second. Both of them had spoken at the same time.
They smiled. Taiga sat beside him and went first.

"Yeah, decided to give it a go. I was free anyway."

"Oh, that's... good."

Scott started fidgeting nervously with his hands. Taiga didn't had anything else to say, so they stayed there in silence.

Aaron and Sofia arrived shortly after, followed by Gav.
They both looked at Taiga with surprise, but didn't say anything, besides "Hey."

Taiga heard Enzo and Leo approaching way at least a minute before they actually showed up.
Apparently, Enzo was mad because Evan had bailed. Like, really mad. 

"It wasn't his fault," he heard Leo saying.

"I tell you when I'll see him I'll-"

Enzo looked at Taiga and stopped for a second. His expression changed completely.

"TAIGA! You actually came!"

He ran towards him with his arms opened. Taiga remained sat and simply raised a leg to keep him at a safe distance.

"I can still change my mind."

"Eheh, alright. But I'm happy you're here!"

It seems he'd already forgotten about Evan.
Leo was smiling as well and waved at him.

"Hey AG. Good to see you!"

Taiga waved back.
He was a little taken aback from seeing all of them in casual clothes. Soccer consumed so much of their contact that seeing them without athletic gear always required a tiny recalibration.

The train left at 6:34 on time

They boarded together and found seats across two facing rows. Enzo spent the first ten minutes trying to convince Gav that Roberto was a better name for a future pet than for a hypothetical child. Aaron joined only to make the argument worse, while Scott had the face of someone who wished to be literally anywhere else. 
Leo checked the Arcadia page and announced that the bowling lanes were already booked until nine.

“Good,” Gav said. “One fewer thing Enzo can turn into a tournament.”

Taiga sat by the window and listened while campus gave way to the denser center of Olympus City.

 Agora looked less like a shopping center and more like somebody had attempted to build an indoor district. Three levels with a glass roof. Escalators crossing over open space. Stores packed along broad walkways beneath enormous signs. Food smells from six directions.
 People. 
 Too many people.
 
Taiga stopped after they entered.

“This is hell.”

Enzo looked at him. "Nope, this is just the *entrance* of hell."

Taiga sighed and moved on.
They headed toward Arcadia. The entrance was impossible to miss. Purple-white signage stretched across two storefronts. Inside, screens flashed, machines chimed, and somebody yelled in victory from somewhere deeper inside. 
Taiga stopped again. Aaron followed his gaze.

“This is more your thing than the mall.”

“What makes you think that?”

“You're already looking at the racing machines.”

“Coincidence.”

They bought a card and put some credit on it. Apparently, Taiga wasn't the only one eyeing the racing machines because as soon as they were done, Enzo yelled

"Let's do the racing first come on!"

Nobody had a preference so they went.

Enzo chose it because there were enough linked seats for all of them. Taiga took one and, when vehicle selection appeared, chose something fast. 
Leo looked over.

“That car has terrible handling.”

“So?”

He grinned. "So I hope you know what you're doing."

Taiga didn't. 
He immediately accelerated after the countdown and crashed straight into a wall at the first corner.

"Fuck-"

Leo started laughing.

Scott went immediately first first, followed by Enzo. Leo was third but Aaron was tailing him with Gav right behind him.
At some point, Aaron rammed Leo into a wall, who shouted,

“That was deliberate!”

“You were in my lane," answered calmly Aaron.

“There are four lanes\!”

"Then pick another."

By the fourth corner, Taiga had learned how to curve without crashing, which was something. 

He managed to surpass Leo who was still recovering from the crash.

“Asshole!”

Taiga smiled. "You parked in the middle of a race."

At some point, the race became less about winning and more about ruining specific people. Scott's car was nowhere to be seen, though.
He ended first, almost fifteen seconds from Enzo.

Taiga stared at him. Scott had barely spoken during the race and apparently destroyed all of them.

"Do you play this?"

"S-Sometimes."

"How often is sometimes?"

Scott looked like someone caught attempting a robbery.

"I- Uh, it's just... M-my brother has the home version, so..."

"GUYS!"

Enzo yelled from the other side of the room. He found a rhythm game.
Nobody looked really convinced, apart from Leo.

"Forty-one, come on!", said Enzo.

"No."

"You're an athlete."

"I play soccer, I don't step on squares."

"Soccer helps, you know. It's all about feet movement."

"No."

Leo grinned. "You scared?"

Alright. Fuck him. Taiga stepped onto the platform.
Enzo blinked. 

"That worked embarrassingly fast."

"Shut up."

The song started. 
Taiga lasted forty seconds before understanding that being athletic did not mean his feet appreciated receiving symbolic instructions at high speed. 
Leo was better, but not dramatically. He still missed enough to swear.
Enzo was clearly in his territory, though.

At one point Taiga stepped left when the game wanted right, nearly collided with Leo on the adjacent platform and grabbed the side rail. Leo laughed without missing his next input.

“Wrong way\!”

"I KNOW."

"You're not stepping, you're beating up the machine!"

"Just shut up."

Enzo finished with the highest score, by a margin. 
Taiga stepped off.

"I fucking hate this place."

Leo looked at him.

“You were having fun thirty seconds ago.”

“Prove it.”

Aaron raised a hand, Gav too. Even Scott was moving his hand.
Taiga walked away, flipping them off from behind.

"Fuck all of you."

---

Sofia and Zoe arrived around eight. By then, Taiga had lost at racing, rhythm, a fighting game and a basketball shooting machine that Aaron took far too seriously. Enzo had been missing for about half an hour.

Hey beat Gav at air hockey though, enough to restore a small amount of dignity. 

"Where's Enzo?", said Zoe.

"Lost in the action," said Gav.

"How did-"

"GUYS! LOOK!"

Enzo reappeared, and he was not alone. 

Taiga blinked. Aaron covered his eyes with a hand.

Gav spoke first. "No."

Enzo was proudly holding a giant stuffed shark against his chest.

"His name is Roberto!"

"Absolutely not."

"You haven't even met him."

"I don't need to."

Meanwhile, Aaron had been immediately challenged by Sofia to basketball. Of course, he accepted with a smug.
Which didn't last. Sofia won by twelve.
Aaron stared at her.

"You play soccer."

She shrugged. "So do you."

"Yeah, this is hands."

Sofia raised her hands. "Apparently, I got good hands as well."

Aaron blinked. "Rematch."

Zoe leaned toward Taiga. “He's going to spend his entire balance on this.”

“Probably, yeah.”

Leo appeared on Taiga's other side.

“Come on.”

Taiga looked over.

“Where exactly?”

“Gav found a two-player shooter.”

“Why should I care?”

“You said the racing machine was rigged. New chance to restore your honor.”

Taiga followed him. The machine had two positions with two plastic rifles mounted to a cabinet, giant screen, generic city-under-attack premise. Gav was already there with Enzo in the first cabinet

“Perfect. Teams. You and Leo vs me and Enzo.”

"Why do I get him?" Taiga asked

Enzo looked around.

“Because you're standing next to him.”

Excellent matchmaking logic. 
They started. The game was pretty stupid, but also fun. Target appeared, moved, then disappeared. They simply had to shoot, reload or take cover.
Thirty seconds later, they were locked in.

“Left.”

Taiga swung left. Shot.

“Behind bus.”

Leo got it. 

“Reloading.”

“Go.”

“Watch out for the window.”

“Got it.”

“Right side.”

“I see him.”

Enzo shouted from the adjacent machine.

“Stop communicating\!”

Gav said, “That is the game.”

“They're acting like professional marines.”

Taiga laughed. Their stage ended and scores appeared: 3456 vs 4512. 
Enzo looked at Gav as if it was his fault.

"Again."

Taiga looked at the continue countdown.

**CONTINUE?**
**10,9,8...**

Leo glanced at him.

“You in?”

Taiga tapped his card. 

“Again.”

“Let's go.”

Stage two loaded and they went at it again. 

"On your left Taiga!"

"You got one at your right."

They both turned in the opposite directions and their shoulder touched for a couple of seconds. Leo was warm despite the cool air-conditioning.
Then target moved and they moved as well.

They won again. Enzo immediately wanted a third one.

"No. Accept it," said Taiga.

Leo nodded. "Let's get some food."

They walked back toward the group.
