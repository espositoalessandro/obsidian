### Chapter 2 - 

After the orientation week, Olympus stopped pretending to be nice.

His first real Political Institutions lecture almost ruined his mood. Taiga had chosen the course because he needed a general-education credit and its description sounded less terrible than Philosophy of Art. Within twelve minutes, he reconsidered every decision that had led him there. Professor Hall spoke quickly. Not impossibly. Just quickly enough that Taiga realized his old habit of listening and trusting himself to remember everything was not going to work. He opened his laptop.
