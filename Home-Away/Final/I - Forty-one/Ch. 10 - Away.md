Half of the contestants remained. Zoe walked in and Sofia cheered.
Aaron leaned towards Taiga.

"I didn't tell her."

"Why?"

"I don't know. My brain turns into soup whenever I'm about to ask."

"It's always soup."

"Fuck you."

Taiga smiled. "So what now?"

Aaron took a deep breath. "I don't know. I'll go to bed and think about this evening for the next ten years, cursing myself."

"Sounds like a plan."

They both kept looking at Zoe.

"You know, sometimes I envy you," Aaron said after a while.

"Where did that come from?"

"I've seen you asking unapologetic questions to everyone. Teammates, professors, Mercer."

Taiga didn't answer.

"How do you do it? How do you manage to be so blunt?"

"What the fuck does that even—"

"Just tell me."

Taiga looked at Zoe going back with the others.

"Sometimes I think people are wrong. Sometimes just stupid. Other times, I don't understand something and I hate that."

"That's it?"

"What did you expect?"

"I don't know. Wisdom."

Taiga smiled without warmth. "I won't call my fucking attitude 'wise'."

"At least you actually say what you want."

"And it often gets me into trouble."

Taiga crossed his arms.

"Look, I don't want to talk about this now, but let's just say that I spent an awful lot of time not caring about what people thought about me."

Aaron frowned, but remained silent. Taiga turned towards him.

"That's where you get the bluntness. Pretending you don't care."

"Pretending?"

"Yeah. If I can't pretend, things usually go shit. There was a time—"

He stopped and bit his lip.

"Never mind."

For a few seconds, neither of them spoke. Then Aaron said,

"I really care about her."

"Yeah, I figured."

"What would you do in my place?"

"Probably fuck things up."

Aaron chuckled. "Okay, what would you *like* to do?"

Taiga thought for a few seconds.

"Shut my brain off until I've gone far enough that I can't chicken out anymore."

Aaron crooked his mouth. "That'd be nice."

Ten minutes later, the contest was over. Zoe and Nora came back, visibly disappointed by the result.
They complained about the winners for a while. Then, they said goodbye to the others and started walking towards the dorm.

Aaron stared at Sofia walking away for several seconds. Then Taiga saw him take a deep breath and suddenly he ran towards her.

"Soph! Wait a sec."

Okay. He didn't expect that. Enzo appeared beside him.

"What the heck did you tell him?"

Taiga shrugged. "Nothing I expected to make him go like this."

"Well," said Evan. "Whatever it was, it looks like it worked. Look!"

Sofia was smiling. When Aaron finished talking, her smile widened and she shoved his shoulder lightly. Then she nodded.

They watched Aaron come back, failing badly at hiding the smile on his face.

"Well?"

Aaron looked at all of them.

"Next Saturday."

Enzo made a sound like Olympus had won a final.
Sofia turned from twenty meters away and laughed, pointing at him.

"By the way, don't ever ask Enzo for this again!"

He shut up, offended, while everyone else laughed.
That lasted maybe five seconds. Then he declared the mission a success and somehow dragged Gav, Evan, Aaron and Scott to the dance floor.
Aaron went with them after thirty seconds of resistance.

Taiga considered it for maybe half a second, but eventually decided to call it a day. He noticed that Leo wasn't with them, though.

He looked around and found him sitting alone on a stone bench a few meters away.
Weird.

Taiga approached. "Dead?"

Leo looked up. "Mh? Yeah, temporarily."

"Aren't you joining them?"

"I'm still considering. You?"

"No."

"I figured. I think I'm hitting the sack too, though."

He stretched, then stood up.

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "I didn't know you were able to do this."

"What?"

"Be tired."

"Oh," he laughed. "Glad I can still surprise you."

"Whatever."

They walked away from the party. A minute later, the crowd had already thinned down. There were only a few couples scattered around, one or two groups and a few people already dismantling their booths.

Leo stayed silent for most of the walk to the dorms. Taiga left it alone. He just looked tired.

Outside the residential building, Leo looked up.

"Whew. That was a hell of a party."

"It was alright."

"Alright? Are we actually giving proper compliments now?"

Taiga rolled his eyes.

"Go to sleep."

He grinned. For fuck's sake, wasn't he passing out a few moments ago?

"By the way, I still want to know the lore of that nickname."

"Here's the lore: Yoichi is an idiot. End of it."

Leo's mouth twitched. "I guess I'll ask him someday."

"Don't you have anything better to do with your life?"

"Probably."

"Then get at it."

"Heh. Sure. Goodnight, AG."

Taiga exhaled and gave him a look.

He raised his hands. "Okay, okay."

Then:

"Night Dynamite."

Taiga shoved him into his elevator.
