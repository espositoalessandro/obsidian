### Chapter 19 - Fallout

Taiga stood out there for what felt like an eternity. When his fingers began to hurt for the cold, he started towards his room.

The corridor was silent, most of the people were probably still in the common lodge. Scott hadn't returned yet as well.

He sat on his bed in the empty room. Now the only noise was the heater clicking from a distance.

Just an hour before, he was enjoying his hot chocolate with Leo and everyone. Now that seemed like it was someone else's life. 

He unlocked his phone and opened Leo's chat. Their last messages were from that very morning, talking about Enzo pouring a disgraceful amount of syrup on his pancakes.

**Leo:** it's disgusting even for me

Taiga caught himself smiling. He stopped immediately.
He tapped on the textbox. His fingers lingered on the keyboard for a couple of seconds.

*sorry*

He deleted it. Sorry? Really?

*you didn't do anything wrong*

Of course he hadn't. But that was not the point. Leo already knew that.

*you weren't wrong ab*

Taiga stopped mid sentence. Was that actually true? Like, Leo was obviously wrong about Taiga not wanting him around. But... what about Taiga himself? Maybe he *was* actually wrong about him.

He deleted it and locked the phone.

What would he even tell him after that? No words would ever erase what he said. The hurt he saw on Leo's face.

Keitaro's face overlapped with Leo's for a moment. Nausea instantly hit him, as he ran to the toilet and actually threw up.

The face Keitaro made when Taiga burned his journal. Then Leo's face right before he turned.
The same disappointment. The same hurt.

He retched again, feeling the bitter metallic taste of bile in his mouth.

Nothing had changed, afterall. He was still a bomb ready to explode whenever someone poked the wrong spot. And he was perfectly capable of bringing everyone with him. 
Even the ones closest to him.

His throat was burning from the acid. He welcomed the pain, though. Afterall, all of that was his own fault.

He sat on the floor, resting his back to the cold wall. 

Yeah. He brought this on himself. He let Leo get too close, when Taiga himself didn't know what he was doing.
The very moment he got exposed, he kicked him out.

He leaned his head onto his hand, resting his elbow on the knee.

*Take a wild guess*

Taiga almost laughed. It didn't take a prophet to understand what he meant. Yet he decided to stay oblivious.
Because that was comfortable. Because he didn't have to deal with it if he didn't acknowledge what the heck was actually going on.

He tilted his head backward until he hit the wall. He stayed like that, staring at the ceiling.

Thinking about it, it was pretty obvious what Leo was up to. And Taiga had been playing along all the fucking time.
Because he enjoyed that. The closeness. The laughs. The contacts. Even just a freaking knee touching his own was enough to make his stomach turn.

Then, when the comfortable grey area got exposed, he chose to flee. Like the freaking coward he was. He didn't even had the courage to tell Leo the truth. He made it sound like it was his fault.

*you're making it worse*

God, what a fucking mess he was.
