### Chapter 19 - Fallout

Taiga stood out there for what felt like an eternity. When his fingers began to hurt from the cold, he started towards his room.

The corridor was silent. Most people were probably still in the common lodge. Scott hadn't returned either.

He sat on his bed in the empty room. Now the only noise was the heater clicking from a distance.

Just an hour earlier, he'd been enjoying his hot chocolate with Leo and everyone else. Now that seemed like it was someone else's life.

He unlocked his phone and opened Leo's chat. Their last messages were from that very morning, about Enzo pouring a disgraceful amount of syrup on his pancakes.

**Leo:** it's disgusting even for me

Taiga caught himself smiling. He stopped immediately.
He tapped on the text box. His fingers lingered on the keyboard for a couple of seconds.

*sorry*

He deleted it. Sorry? Really?

*you didn't do anything wrong*

Of course he hadn't. But that was not the point. Leo already knew that.

*you weren't wrong ab*

Taiga stopped mid-sentence. Was that actually true? Like, Leo was obviously wrong about Taiga not wanting him around. But... what about Taiga himself? Maybe he *was* actually wrong about him.

He deleted it and locked the phone.

What would he even tell him after that? No words would ever erase what he'd said. The hurt he'd seen on Leo's face.

Keitaro's face overlapped with Leo's for a moment. Nausea hit him. He ran to the toilet and threw up.

Keitaro's face when Taiga had burned his journal. Then Leo's face right before he turned.
The same disappointment. The same hurt.

He retched again, feeling the bitter metallic taste of bile in his mouth.

Nothing had changed, after all. He was still a bomb ready to explode whenever someone poked the wrong spot. And he was perfectly capable of taking everyone down with him.
Even the ones closest to him.

His throat was burning from the acid. He welcomed the pain, though. After all, all of that was his own fault.

He sat on the floor, resting his back against the cold wall.

Yeah. He'd brought this on himself. He'd let Leo get too close when Taiga himself didn't know what he was doing.
The very moment he got exposed, he kicked him out.

He rested his head in his hand, his elbow on his knee.

*Take a wild guess*

Taiga almost laughed. It didn't take a prophet to understand what he meant. Yet he'd chosen to stay oblivious.
Because that was comfortable. Because he didn't have to deal with it if he didn't acknowledge what the heck was actually going on.

He tilted his head backward until he hit the wall. He stayed like that, staring at the ceiling.

Thinking about it, it was pretty obvious what Leo was up to. And Taiga had been playing along the whole fucking time.
Because he'd enjoyed it. The closeness. The laughs. The touches. Even just a freaking knee touching his own was enough to make his stomach turn.

Then, when the comfortable grey area got exposed, he chose to flee. Like the freaking coward he was. He didn't even have the courage to tell Leo the truth. He'd made it sound like it was Leo's fault.

*you're making it worse*

God, what a fucking mess he was.
