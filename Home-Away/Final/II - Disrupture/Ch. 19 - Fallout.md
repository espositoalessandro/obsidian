### Chapter 19 - Fallout

Taiga stood out there for what felt like an eternity. When his fingers began to hurt from the cold, he started towards his room.

The corridor was silent. Most people were probably still in the common lodge.

Taiga reached the door to Leo's room. He stopped with his hand raised in midair when he heard a sniff coming from inside. He felt sick.
He clenched his fist hard and stood there for a while, but he couldn't bring himself to knock.

He turned and went inside his room instead.

Scott hadn't returned yet.
Taiga sat on his bed in the empty room. Now the only noise was the heater clicking from a distance.

Just an hour earlier, he'd been enjoying his hot chocolate with Leo and everyone else. Now that seemed like it was someone else's life.

He unlocked his phone and opened Leo's chat. Their last messages were from that very morning, about Enzo pouring a disgraceful amount of syrup on his pancakes.

**Leo:** it's disgusting even for me

Taiga caught himself smiling. He stopped immediately.
He tapped on the text box. His fingers lingered on the keyboard for a couple of seconds.

*sorry*

He deleted it. Sorry? Really?

*you didn't do anything wrong*

Of course he hadn't. But that was not the point. Leo already knew that.

*you weren't wrong ab*

Taiga stopped mid-sentence. Was that actually true? Like, Leo was obviously wrong about Taiga not wanting him around. But... what about Taiga himself? Maybe he *was* actually wrong about him.

He deleted it and locked the phone.

What would he even tell him after that? No words would ever erase what he'd said. The hurt he'd seen on Leo's face.

Keitaro's face overlapped with Leo's for a moment. Nausea hit him.
He ran to the toilet and threw up.

Keitaro's face when Taiga had burned his journal. Then Leo's face right before he turned.
The same disappointment. The same hurt.

He retched again, feeling the bitter metallic taste of bile in his mouth.

Nothing had changed, after all. He was still a bomb ready to explode whenever someone poked the wrong spot. And he was perfectly capable of taking everyone down with him.
Even the ones closest to him.

His throat was burning from the acid. He welcomed the pain, though. After all, all of that was his own fault.

He sat on the floor, resting his back against the cold wall.

Yeah. He'd brought this on himself. He'd let Leo get too close when Taiga himself didn't know what he was doing.
The very moment he got exposed, he kicked him out.

He rested his head in his hand, his elbow on his knee.

*Take a wild guess*

Taiga almost laughed. It didn't take a prophet to understand what he meant. Yet he'd chosen to stay oblivious.
Because that was comfortable. Because he didn't have to deal with it if he didn't acknowledge what the heck was actually going on.

He tilted his head backward until he hit the wall. He stayed like that, staring at the ceiling.

Thinking about it, it was pretty obvious what Leo was up to. And Taiga had been playing along the whole fucking time.
Because he'd enjoyed it. The closeness. The laughs. The touches. Even just a freaking knee touching his own was enough to make his stomach turn.

Then, when the comfortable grey area got exposed, he chose to flee. Like the freaking coward he was. He didn't even have the courage to tell Leo the truth. He'd made it sound like it was Leo's fault.

*you're making it worse*

God, what a fucking mess he was.

***

"Taiga?"

He heard Scott's voice in the room. Right, enough sulking. Time to face the world.

"Yeah."

He stood up, flushed the toilet and rinsed his mouth out. Then he took a deep breath before going out.

Scott was sitting on his bed, untying his shoes. He looked up.

"Are you... feeling alright?"

Taiga nodded.

"You a-are extremely pale."

"I'm good."

"Okay... Anyway."

His tone suddenly shifted to his soccer voice. He straightened up.

"I know you're upset, but can we please talk for a second?"

Taiga exhaled and sat on his bed. Scott continued.

"Look. There's no way around this. Aaron was a dick and you pushed it further."

Taiga's jaw tightened, but he didn't answer.

"I'm not trying to say who was worse, but I can't let this destroy the team's morale, especially with a match tomorrow. Half of our teammates saw you two arguing."

"If you're trying to suggest that I go and talk to him..."

"No. You don't have to make up tonight. But tomorrow, you still have to play together. Everyone else is still counting on you two."

"You don't have to tell me. I'm not even playing—"

"Taiga."

He looked up. Scott was staring right into his eyes.

"This is about the team. Not about you."

That hit with extreme precision.
Now something heavy dropped into his stomach. Suddenly he realized that his bullshit might have done more damage than he'd thought. It was not just his relationship with Leo.
He tried very hard to suppress another retch.

After a while, Taiga nodded.

"Alright. Sorry, you're right."

Scott smiled. "Good. I'm really sorry about what happened too. I should have stepped in sooner, but I didn't expect things to escalate that badly."

"It's not your fault."

"I'm the captain, Taiga. And that comes with responsibilities."

Right. Scott too. How in the world had he been able to fuck up so many people in ten fucking minutes?

"About the rest... I can help you as a friend, but I can't fix it. That's up to you two."

Taiga thought about confronting Aaron again. Right now, it seemed like the worst idea in the world. He'd barely managed not to punch him a couple of hours earlier.
And right now, just imagining his face made his pulse race again.

"Last time I saw him, I was about to punch him. I don't..."

He took a deep breath.

"...I'm gonna need time."

Scott nodded. "I'm glad you didn't. By the way, do you know where Leo is? Last time I saw him, he was running after you."

Taiga looked away, trying hard to hold back his tears. "Don't know. Probably his room."

Scott stayed silent for a couple of seconds. "Okay."

He picked up his pajamas and went to the bathroom.

Taiga exhaled and got changed as well. He was already in bed when Scott came out.
Scott switched off the lights and tucked himself under his sheets.

"Night."

"'Night," Taiga muttered.

When the last light was out, he finally let the tears fall.

***

Next morning, Taiga seriously considered skipping breakfast.
Not because his stomach was still upset.

He still managed to drag himself downstairs with Scott. The cold morning air hit him all at once when they opened the lodge door.
Taiga felt better. Slightly.

They walked silently to the dining lodge. The corridor from the main lodge already smelled like coffee.
Normally, he would have loved that. Now, his stomach tightened at the thought of what was waiting beyond.

Olympus was spread across three big tables near the windows. Not everyone had arrived yet. The rest of the hall was almost empty, save for Pinetree's players.

He spotted Aaron sitting next to Jordan.
Taiga exhaled and went to pick up a tray. He took more food than he actually wanted, repeating to himself that he needed fuel for the match.

He took a seat at another table, between Scott and the window, with his shoulder toward the entrance.

Five minutes later, he heard Enzo's voice coming from behind him.
He stared at his plate, stopping with the fork halfway to his mouth. His stomach suddenly refused to accept anything else.

Enzo and Leo passed his table on their way to the food counter. He refused to look up.
Enzo was complaining about something involving waking up early on Sunday. Taiga noticed that he could barely hear Leo's voice. Which was unusual.

He glanced up. They were in line. Enzo was still talking while Leo stood holding his tray, looking down.

He couldn't even manage to look at him. How was he supposed to survive an entire match?

Ten minutes later, they were coming back with their trays. Enzo stopped at their table and sat beside Evan on the bench opposite Taiga. Taiga looked up automatically and saw Leo still standing, looking hesitantly at him.

Their eyes met for a second. Enough for Taiga to feel sick again. He looked down at his plate, fork still in his hand.

Leo sat beside Enzo, at the far end of the opposite bench.

Taiga couldn't eat anything else.

***

Pinetree gave Olympus a small meeting room in the training center beside the pitch.

Mercer arrived, and the briefing began.

"First of all, I can see you've all made lots of progress in your new positions. Remember what you learned. Now, regarding the match."

He pointed at his magnetic board.

"As you've seen for yourselves, Pinetree players are extremely versatile. They will use that to move people where you don't expect them. So, don't assume their fullback will always be where you expect him to be."

Pinetree's tags on the board showed a 4-3-3 formation.

"They organize around midfield. Don't expect much chasing near our goal. They will encourage you to play along the touchlines before closing you down. Don't give them what they want."

"Cole. Reyes. Mendez. Their fullbacks will run, and they will be dangerous. But they'll leave space behind them often. Use it."

They nodded. Nobody talked, though.

Mercer looked at all of them and narrowed his eyes for a second.

"Communication will be key," he continued. "Talk to each other."

Someone nodded. Mercer frowned.

"That's all."

***

Taiga pulled his training top over his head and put it in his bag. Across the changing room, Leo was sitting beside Enzo, winding tape around his ankle.

He looked away before Leo could look up.

Communication is key. Right.
For the first time since joining Apollo, Taiga was glad he was on the bench.
Outside, the sun had reached the pitch, but the benches were still in shadow. Snow lay in dirty banks beyond the fence. A few people had gathered along the opposite touchline, hands buried in their pockets.

They warmed up with the ball first.

Gav passed to Taiga and moved around the cone. Taiga gave it to Leo.

"Back," Leo said.

He returned it immediately. Taiga passed to Gav.

Again.

On the next turn, Taiga put too much into the pass. Leo stretched a foot out, stopped it and brought it back without saying anything.

"Easy, Akatora," Mercer called.

"Yeah."

Taiga adjusted his sleeves and waited for the ball to come around.

Scott was already shouting from the other end of the pitch. Carter had left somebody unmarked in the practice box, and apparently everyone in the valley needed to know.

Taiga tried to listen to that instead.

People began filling up the bleachers. He noticed that the bleachers beside Pinetree's main pitch were enclosed by wooden walls, with heaters spaced throughout. That looked cozy. They had about a tenth of the capacity of Olympus's stadium.

Hale and Mercer then whistled, and both teams started toward the locker rooms.

Milo approached Taiga as they reached the touchline.

"Hey."

Taiga looked up. "Hey."

"You... alright?"

"I'm trying."

Milo smiled uncertainly. "I'm really sorry for what happened yesterday."

"Thanks..."

"Well, for what it's worth, I'll do my best to kick his ass!"

Taiga snorted a laugh. "I'm sure you will."

Milo smiled properly. "Good luck, you guys."

"You too."

***

Fifteen minutes before kickoff, they were in the locker room, waiting for Mercer to call them out.

They were all sitting on their benches, waiting without talking much.

Scott stood up.

"Alright, guys."

He stepped into the middle of the room. Silence fell.

"There's no point in pretending nothing happened."

Taiga gulped. Aaron stopped tying his boot.
Scott pointed at the door.

"When we go out of this locker room, we leave whatever shit we're carrying in here, along with our belongings. When we put our shirts on—"

He pointed a finger at his chest.

"—one's problems become everyone's problems. And I don't want any unnecessary bullshit on the pitch."

He looked around the room without lingering on either Taiga or Aaron. Taiga appreciated that.

"Is that fucking clear?"

Everyone muttered, "Yes."

Mercer opened the door. "Come out."

Scott turned to his team. "Now let's go show them what Apollo is really capable of."

***

The referee whistled, and the game started.

For the first few minutes, Pinetree let Olympus have the ball near their own goal. Dennis stood well back, pointing people into position. Skylar moved between Carter and Walsh without chasing either of them.

Then Aaron received by the left touchline.

Milo came toward him from the inside, cutting off the pass to Gav. Aaron looked up. Evan had already started forward, but Pinetree's right-back was close enough to follow him.

"Back!" Carter called.

Aaron took another touch.

Milo got closer. One of the midfielders came across behind him.

Aaron finally played backward, hard enough that Carter had to turn and run after it.

Mercer stepped to the edge of his area.

"Earlier, Bennett!"

Aaron lifted a hand.

A couple of minutes later, the same thing happened on the other side. Enzo received, Jonah came forward to help, and suddenly both had a Pinetree player in front of them. Enzo tried to squeeze the ball between two pairs of legs.
Throw-in.

Taiga rubbed his hands together beneath his coat.

From here, he could see where they wanted Olympus to go. Every time the ball reached the side, green shirts moved toward it.
The space appeared somewhere else, too far away for the player with the ball to use without help.

Gav kept turning and pointing. Leo came short, then stopped when Isaac received instead. Nobody seemed sure who was supposed to move first.

"Turn!" Taiga muttered through clenched teeth when Leo got the next pass. He had room.

Leo checked over his shoulder, took the ball across his body and looked for Jordan. By the time he played it, Dennis had stepped in front.

On seventeen minutes, Milo moved away from the touchline toward Gav.

Aaron followed him a few steps, then checked when Pinetree's right-back ran outside.
Evan was coming back, pointing toward the runner.
Aaron turned. Milo went the other way.

For a second, both of them moved toward him.
Milo passed outside.

"BACK POST!" Scott shouted.

The fullback reached the ball before Evan and crossed low. Skylar got across Carter. Scott went down, but the ball was already past him.

The people on the opposite side of the pitch cheered.

Taiga stared at the goal while Skylar ran toward the corner, Milo catching him by the shoulders.

One-nil.

Aaron stood with his hands on his hips. Evan said something to him, pointing back toward where the move had started. Aaron nodded without looking up.

Scott collected the ball and threw it toward halfway.

"NEXT ONE! COME ON!"

***

At halftime, Taiga followed the others into the changing room.

Mercer shut the door.

"What's this?"

Nobody answered.

"Zero communication. You are all hesitating. How many times did we talk about the space behind their fullbacks?"

He looked at Enzo.

"Yeah, it's there."

"Yes. It is. So why do we keep waiting until they've covered it?"

Evan took a drink. Leo was looking at the floor.

Mercer pointed toward the door.

"They want you standing still by the line with the ball. We knew that before kickoff. Yet we keep giving them time to put us there."

Gav nodded. "We need the next pass sooner."

"And someone asking for it. Bennett, when Cole comes short, you can go past him. You don't need to wait until he's got the ball to start."

Aaron straightened.

"Okay."

"Mendez. You had Reyes running twice. Play him."

"Yeah."

"Ellis, keep going when Reyes comes inside. Foster will cover."

Gav nodded again.

Mercer looked around the room.

"You're all waiting for somebody else. Call. Move. If the pass is wrong, we'll deal with it. We can't do anything with a pass you never make. Also."

He paused for a second, looking at them.

"You have forty-five minutes to behave like adults."

Mercer checked the time.

"Get back out."

***

For a few minutes, Olympus moved faster.

Aaron went past Evan before the ball reached him. Leo found Enzo running behind the fullback. Jordan's header went over, but at least they were getting somewhere.

Taiga leaned forward, elbows on his knees.

Then the passes started coming late again.

"Go," he muttered when Evan received. "Fucking go."

Evan turned back. Taiga's heel struck the ground, over and over.

He stood when Leo broke through the middle, then watched him lose the ball while looking for a pass. Taiga sat down and immediately leaned forward again.

Near the hour mark, Milo won a corner.

Dennis came up from defense. Walsh stayed with him, but Dennis reached the cross first and headed it down. Carter's clearance struck another player. The loose ball rolled toward a Pinetree midfielder, who drove it through the crowd.

Scott went down too late.

Two-nil.

Taiga closed his eyes and leaned back.

"Akatora. Warm up."

He opened his eyes. Mercer was looking at him.

"Now."

Something heavy fell through his stomach. He stood anyway and began jogging along the touchline.

Four minutes later, Mercer called him back.

"Their right-back's still going inside. Stay wide and run behind him. Give Bennett a pass when he needs one."

Taiga nodded, pulling his bib off.

The fourth official lifted the board.

**11 OUT**
**41 IN**

Taiga gulped. His first conference appearance. He'd spent weeks waiting for this.

Evan came toward the touchline, breathing heavily.

"Go show them, tiger," he said.

Their hands met, and the referee waved Taiga on.

He felt Leo's gaze on him as he jogged across the field. Taiga didn't look back.
Instead, he stared at Aaron for a second.

His face looked scared for whatever sick reason. Taiga still wanted to punch it.
Instead, he took a deep breath and nodded briefly.

Aaron relaxed visibly and nodded back.

That could work for now.

He turned towards Pinetree's side. Milo was smiling at him. Taiga cracked his knuckles in response, putting on a challenging smile.

Two minutes later, Aaron gave him his first pass. Taiga returned it and ran forward. The next time, he turned, beat the fullback to the ball and had his cross blocked by Dennis.
Corner.

He jogged into the box, already breathing hard.

For a while, the running occupied him. He could follow someone, chase a ball, get into a space.

At seventy-four, Aaron found Taiga near the touchline.

The fullback stepped toward him. Taiga knocked the ball inside to Leo and immediately ran behind his man.

"Leo! Back!"

Leo returned it first time.

Taiga reached it before Dennis could come across. He took another touch toward the byline and saw Leo still running toward the penalty area.

Taiga drove the ball low back toward the penalty arc, finding Leo, who hit it first time.

He saw the goalkeeper leap for it, but the ball flew into the upper left corner.

Taiga blinked. Two-one.

Someone patted him on the shoulder while half the team piled onto Leo.

He just stood there. The realization that he'd just provided an assist came a few seconds later. Joy didn't follow, though.

Taiga bit his lip as Leo emerged from the crowd, laughing.

Their eyes finally met.

Leo's smile faded slowly. Taiga's mouth curved into a brief, hesitant smile.

"Nice pass," Leo said.

There was no warmth in his voice. Taiga lowered his head and nodded. When he looked up again, Leo's expression had changed. Sadness was now all Taiga could see in his eyes.

"N-Nice shot," he managed to say.

Leo nodded back, then jogged back into position.

Pinetree won the match two-one.

***

Back in the locker room, the atmosphere was slightly more relaxed.

"The second half was better," Mercer said. "Communication improved, and you had more chances to score. Mendez. Akatora."

They looked up.

"Good job. The exchange was almost flawless."

"Thanks, Coach," they muttered together.

"Now, shower and go eat. Bus leaves at four. We'll talk about this tomorrow."

Mercer left. Players started undressing, and chatter slowly filled the room.

Evan sat beside Taiga and put an arm around his shoulder.
Taiga looked at him. He was smiling brightly.

"That's what I'm talking about."

"Yeah, that was cool as fuck!" Enzo said from across the room.

Taiga forced a smile. "Thanks, guys."

"And Leo's sniper shot? Sick!" Gav added.

"Yeah, fuck! I can't wait to see that again in film tomorrow," Jordan said.

Taiga heard Leo laughing, but he didn't look up.

***

At lunch, he shared the table with Gav, Enzo, Leo, Skylar and Milo.

They discussed the match and congratulated each other. No one mentioned Aaron or what had happened yesterday.

Milo and Skylar exchanged numbers with everyone before leaving.

They packed their things afterward, and by 4:30, Olympus was outside the main lodge.

Coach Hale gathered both squads before the bus was loaded. Short speech: thank you, good work, see you later in the semester.
Photos happened. Olympus. Pinetree. Then mixed.

"Aw, I can't believe you guys are already leaving," Milo said.

"Yeah," Skylar added. "It was fun hanging with you."

Taiga smiled. "Pretty sure we'll be seeing each other again soon enough."

"Yeah," Enzo said. "It'll be our turn to show you our campus!"

Gav grinned. "I really hope you guys are ready for the city."

Milo made a face. "I hope so too! Well, have a safe trip back! Text us when you make it back to Olympus!"

Then hugs followed. Milo squeezed Taiga before he could react.

"You were on fire this morning! Your goal should have counted for two."

"T-Thanks. You weren't half bad either. A real pain in the ass."

Milo pulled back and grinned.

"Eheh, thankies! So, see you guys at your place then!"

Taiga nodded. "Yeah. Next time we will kick your asses."

"I'm sure you'll try."

"Fuck off."

Milo laughed. "Bye, Taiga!"

"See ya."

Taiga climbed aboard behind Enzo.

Halfway back, Leo was already sitting in the same row as on Thursday, in the aisle seat.

Before Taiga could think of anything, Enzo approached to sit beside Leo. Leo cast a brief, hesitant glance at Taiga before standing up to let Enzo in.

Taiga looked down and sat two rows back on the opposite side, in the window seat. Scott sat beside him shortly after.

The bus pulled away.
