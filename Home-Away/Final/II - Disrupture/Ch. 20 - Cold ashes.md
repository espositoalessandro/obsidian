### Chapter 20 - Cold ashes

Monday arrived and life continued at Olympus.

Taiga's legs felt heavy when he crossed the campus heading to the Lecture Hall for his Data Structures lecture.

He stepped through the doorway with his heart beating fast and spotted Leo almost immediately. Mostly because he was sitting in the same row, only two chairs farther over than usual.

Taiga stopped at the aisle as Leo looked up. For a second neither of them spoke.

Then Leo nodded once. "Hi."

"Hey."

Taiga took his usual seat, leaving two empty chairs between them. Leo returned to his laptop.

The professor arrived and the lesson started. He spoke, Taiga wrote notes, Leo did too.

There were no jokes. No glances. No smiles.
Usually, Leo was pretty distracting for him, yet now he could barely understand what the topic was.

He was, like, three meters away. It felt like there was an entire pitch in between them.

The lecture eventually ended. Taiga packed his things and stood up. His heart was heavy.
Should he try and say something now? But what could he possibly—

"Taiga."

His stomach dropped as he turned way too quickly.

Leo held out a pen. "You dropped this."

Taiga took it. "Thanks."

Leo turned without answering. Taiga stood there, holding his pen with his heart still beating fast, watching him pack up and head toward the other end of the row.

Fuck it.

"Leo."

He turned.

Taiga opened his mouth a couple of times without being able to get a single fucking word out.

"...Yes?"

He exhaled. Jesus.

"Could... do you—I mean..."

Taiga bit his lip. Come on, dumbass.

"C-Can we talk?"

Leo's expression was indecipherable. A year passed. Maybe two.
Then he slightly furrowed his eyebrows.

"Don't you have Calculus now?"

"Yeah, right. Uh—later? After film?"

Leo stared at him for a second. Then he finally nodded.

"Fine."

Relief hit Taiga so fast that he felt dizzy.

"Okay. Great. Then... later."

"Yeah. Later."

Great. Now there was no escaping it.

***

Taiga spent the whole Calculus class trying to put together something to say.
A blank page sat open on his screen while the professor kept talking.

Sorry was an obvious starting point. Then what? What could he even expect from him at this point?

Yes, giving him an explanation was part of it. Of course it was.

But... what would happen if Leo decided to walk away anyway?
Would Taiga really be okay with that? Obviously not. The memory of sitting through their previous class without any interaction still made his stomach clench.

Then again... how could he face him if he himself didn't know exactly what he fucking wanted?

How could words fix such a mess?

He rested his head in his hands, gripping his hair.

This was going nowhere.

Taiga exhaled and picked up his phone.

***

He arrived outside the cafeteria in the University Center shortly after and found Nora already waiting for him.

"Hey."

"Hey, Taiga!"

They sat down at an empty table inside.

"So, how did the exchange go?" she began.

Taiga hung his coat on the back of his chair.

"Cold. And... messy."

She raised an eyebrow.

"Did something happen? Is that why you asked me to talk?"

Taiga fiddled with his thumbs.

"...kinda."

"What did—"

"Sorry! I'm late!"

Zoe stormed in, panting heavily.

"Did I miss the fun?"

"We've just arrived."

"Hey, Zoe!" Nora said.

"Nora!"

They kissed each other on the cheeks, and Zoe sat beside Nora.

She glanced between them. "Both of us? That's serious, then."

Nora looked worried. "Apparently. Now I'm seriously concerned."

"Yeah, what happened? Oh—wait a second."

She narrowed her eyes, staring at Taiga.

"Does this, by any chance, have anything to do with Leo being incredibly boring throughout our Calculus class?"

Taiga gulped. He kept staring at his fingers.

Nora widened her eyes. "What? Did they fight?"

Zoe shrugged. "I dunno. Leo didn't want to say anything! He kept saying he was *just tired*. As if he was capable of something like that."

A waiter arrived and they ordered their food. Zoe asked for a coffee instead.

Nora turned toward him. "So?"

His pulse began to rise.

"...kinda."

"Yeah, you already said that! What happened, Taiga?"

"Yeah, we fought."

Zoe slammed her hand on the table. "I knew it!"

He told them about their last night at Pinetree. About the terrace and what happened there. How Aaron had talked shit about them and how he'd reacted.

"I would have punched him. Straight away," Zoe declared.

Nora shook her head. "Jesus, what a jerk."

"Yeah... well, I got pretty close to doing it. That's why I left."

Then he told them how Leo had followed him and how he'd pushed him away.

"I... I think I wasn't ready for that. Like—I didn't expect to even go there. I got caught off guard. So I ended up telling him to go away."

Nora and Zoe didn't react immediately.

"Taiga," Zoe said. "I'm not going to beat around the bush here. You like him. Don't even try to argue."

He didn't answer. Talking out loud about that was still incredibly hard.

"But still," Nora continued. "I don't get why. I mean—so what if you like him? Are you, like, just coming out of the closet or something?"

Zoe frowned. "Wait, what? You thought you were straight?"

Taiga turned red almost instantly. "Jesus. No. It's not that."

"Then why did you push him away like that?" Nora insisted.

"I... still don't know exactly. Mostly, really bad past experiences."

Zoe tilted her head. "You had a toxic boyfriend or something?"

He hesitated for a couple of seconds. "Not exactly... let's just say we didn't see eye to eye on a few things. But that's not the point. Look—"

Taiga took a deep breath.

"I asked him this morning if we could talk. Today, after film with the team. Thing is..."

"You don't know what to say," Nora finished for him.

He nodded. "Pretty much."

Zoe took a sip from her cup. "Well, what you say mostly depends on what you want from him."

"But I think you should give him an explanation. He deserves that, at the very least," Nora said.

"Yeah, that much I figured. About what I want... that's another story. I liked what we had. I don't want that to go."

Zoe spread her hands. "Then tell him! If that's the truth, what's wrong with that?"

"Because... what if that's not what he wants?"

Nora frowned. "What do you mean?"

"What if he wants something I'm still not ready for?"

"Like what?" she said. "Be your boyfriend? Go on a date?"

That word sent shivers down his spine.

"I don't know. Something like that."

"Then you tell him," Zoe answered. "You make it clear that you actually like him but need more time. Plenty of people do that. In the meantime, though, you work your ass off to figure yourself out."

"And if you end up realizing that's not what you want," Nora continued, "then you tell him. But, Taiga, let's be honest. You two have been at it for *months*."

"W-What?"

Zoe smiled. "You think we're stupid? Gosh, I was expecting you two to kiss at any moment."

"Yeah, the way you look at each other? Dead giveaway," Nora added with a smirk.

Now Taiga's face was on fire.

"Okay. Fuck you. Both of you."

Zoe picked up her cup and leaned back in her chair.

"Welcome back, Taiga."

***

Mercer paused the footage just before Pinetree's first goal.

On the screen, Aaron and Evan were both moving toward Milo. The fullback was already outside them.

"Who's going with him?"

"I should've stayed with the run," Evan said.

"And called it."

Mercer let the clip play. The cross came in low. Skylar got across Carter, and the ball went past Scott.

"Call the runner. Before he's gone."

Taiga wrote that down.

They worked through the rest of the match. Mercer kept stopping the footage a second before someone passed, showing them the space they'd already lost.

At seventy-nine minutes, Aaron's pass went out to the left.

On screen, he gave the ball to Leo and ran behind the fullback. The return came straight back. One touch toward the byline, then the cutback.

Leo met it at the edge of the box.

"Jesus," Jordan muttered as the ball flew into the upper corner.

Mercer rewound to Aaron's pass.

"Better. Bennett releases it early. Akatora keeps moving after the pass, and Mendez follows instead of standing there watching."

He played it again.

The goalkeeper stretched. Same result.

"One more?" Enzo asked.

"You can watch it on your own time."

A few people laughed. Mercer closed the video and turned the lights on.

"That's all."

Chairs scraped behind Taiga.

Taiga closed his notebook and looked up. Leo was sitting two rows in front of him, arguing with Enzo and Jordan whether the keeper could've reached it.

Come on. No chickening out now.

Easier said than done. Leo stood, still smiling while arguing with the others. 
Taiga gulped, even though his mouth stayed dry. 

He stood. Jordan patted Leo on his shoulder, then he and Enzo started toward the staircase.

Taiga felt his legs shaking when Leo approached him.

"Central Park?" He asked.

"Yeah. Sure."

They went outside.

***

They didn't talk throughout all the walk to the park.
The air was still biting, although the snow around the campus was barely surviving in dirty banks along the paths.

Taiga tried to rehearse what he wanted to say, but the closer they get to Central Park, the harder it became to remember any it.

By the time they found a bench and sat, his mind had gone totally blank.

For a minute, neither said anything. Campus moved around them: bikes on the path, somebody laughing near the central fountain, the mess hall doors opening and closing behind a stream of students.

Taiga had spent most of the day wanting this conversation. Now, every possible opening sounded wrong.
Leo did not help him. He just waited in silence.

Finally he took a deep breath.

“I’m... sorry.”

Taiga swallowed.

“For Saturday.”

Leo’s face stayed careful.
Taiga opened his mouth. Nothing came out. Fantastic.

He stared at his hands. Then he closed his eyes. There was no way around it.

"You weren't wrong. About me wanting you around."

Still nothing from Leo. God.

"I know I said otherwise. I was fucked up. Angry. Mostly scared."

"Scared?"

Taiga opened his eyes and looked at him for a second. Leo was frowning.

"I grew comfortable in staying around you without... you know, naming anything."

He stared at his fingers.

"I've been a coward. And when Aaron said that, I felt so exposed I had to run away."

"I thought it was because you were about to punch him."

Taiga laughed once from his nose. 

"That, also."

Leo didn't add anything else.

"I wanted to come back to you. The same night. I was about to knock at your door. When I heard-"

His throat closed up.

"Shit. I-I thought you were better off without me. I hurt you so fucking bad when you did nothing to deserve it."

His eyes were watering. Fuck, he wasn't going to cry now.
Taiga looked away from him. He must be looking so pathetic right now.

He took a couple of deep breaths, trying to calm himself down.

"I understand if you don't want to see me anymore. I j-just-"

His voice broke and Taiga couldn't say anything else.
For a second, he considered running off. Hide in a bush or something. He just wanted to disappear from the world right now.

"I don't," Leo said.

Taiga looked up.

"I don't want you to go away."

It took a couple of seconds for Taiga to realize what Leo had just said. Now he had no idea how to answer.

"But I don't want to pretend this didn't happen either," Leo continued

"Right."

"No, I mean..."

Leo took a deep breath.

“What Aaron said. About us.”

Taiga gulped.

“I don’t want it to change... wherever we were going.”

His's heart started beating hard.

"Do you?" Leo asked.

And here came the question Taiga had feared the most.
He wiped tears from his face and exhaled. 

"...No."

Leo relaxed visibly and smiled.

"I gotta say, I was really confused. I've spent all Sunday thinking I said something I shouldn't have. Then I started realizing that maybe I had been reading it wrong all along."

He took a breath.

"I really believed that you pushed me away because... you know... the others might think you were into me or something."

"...and it wasn't true," he added shortly after.

Taiga could still see the hurt on his face. Guilt still clawed his chest.

"It was actually the other way around," Taiga said.

Leo looked down and smiled "Yeah, I see it now."

They silence fell awkwardly for a minute.

"I still don't get one thing, though," Leo said.

"What?"

"Why did you react that way? I mean- why are so scared about..."

Leo gestured.

"This?"

"It's kind of a long story. But..."

Yeah. He deserved it.

"I think I owe you an explanation."

Taiga paused, recollecting his thoughts.

"When I first went to camp, two years ago, I didn't go there alone. My boyfriend... well, ex-boyfriend, came with me."

He hesitated for a second.

"Back then I was... different. I liked hanging out with lots people, talking, opening up and stuff."

"Hard to imagine."

"Yeah, I know. I found a place there pretty fast. Friends. Lots of it. People who actually liked having me around."

Taiga rubbed his hands together.

“My ex had come because he wanted more time with me. Instead, I started spending time with everyone else too. He got jealous and we fought about it. A lot. We were both stupid and no one was willing to acknowledge his own mistakes.”

Taiga took another breath.

“Then scoutmaster organized a SportsFest. At some point, a guy from the other team showed up beaten up, covered in bruises and blood, and said I did it.”

Leo’s expression now changed. "The fuck...?"

“I didn’t. But everybody believed him. Things blew up, and suddenly everyone turned against me. The same people that were cheering for me two minutes before, now accused me of sabotage.”

He kicked a stone on the pavement.

“After that, camp stopped feeling like mine. People avoided me. Some of them started fucking with me because they knew nobody would believe me. They started ambushing me in bathrooms and bullying me around.”

Leo was quiet for a moment.

“What about your boyfriend?”

Taiga looked down.

“He was the one who set me up.”

Leo went still.

“The guy beaten up confessed to me after seeing what he had caused. So I confronted my ex and I totally lost it. I shoved him. He punched me.”

Taiga pointed to the scar running across his nose.

“That’s where this came from.”

"Fuck."

“I broke up with him. He left the next day while I stayed. Even though it was hell for me.”

"Wh- didn't you speak to your scoutmaster?"

Taiga shook his head. “What was the point? Even if I told everyone the truth, it wouldn’t undo anything. Plus...”

"You wanted to cover for him."

"Yeah. Guess love makes you do stupid things."

Taiga went silent. A minute passed, then Leo said,

“So, when you told me to go away because of what Aaron said...”

“It felt like the same shape. Something between me and someone else suddenly becoming everybody’s business. Me losing my shit. Somebody close enough to get hurt by it.”

Understanding moved across Leo’s face.

"Taiga... "

"Last year, when I met Keitaro and the others... I had came back to camp looking for a new start. I wanted to turn page. Then I saw Keitaro making tons of friends in just a few days, while I was still struggling because of my reputation."

A pause followed. 

"I spent half of the term hating him while trying to make him miserable. Trying everything to prove that if you fuck up once, then everyone who loves you suddenly turns against you."

"Back at the rink... you said that last year you weren't in a good place."

"Yeah, I did horrible things to him, to the point where I burned down his journal. Just to make him hate me. Hit me. Anything. Instead... he forgave me."

Taiga shook his head. He laid back, resting his hands on the bench, looking up in the sky.

"I've been trying, times and times again, to get on with my past. And every time, it always comes back. Every fucking time, for whatever reason. I always end up hurting people next to me. And I have only myself to blame."

The stone bench was cold under his palm. He slightly curled his fingers.

"That's why I didn't came back to you."

He stopped.
A huge weight seemed to be lifting from his stomach. Yet, as he looked at the stars, he felt extremely alone.
A small point in an endless universe, burning too hot for anyone to get close.

Now he also felt incredibly selfish for having asked Leo to stay with him. He probably had no idea what Taiga was actually capable of. 
The explanation was all he needed, he had better moving on witho-

Taiga froze. He felt something warm touching his hand.
When he looked down, Leo had put his hand over the back of Taiga's. 

His heart started to rush against his ribs. Then he looked up: Leo was staring at him with a warm, compassionate smile.

His brain started screaming. Remove it. Now. Too dangerous.

As if Leo could read his mind, he squeezed tighter, brushing his thumb against Taiga's knuckles.

"That's not up to you to decide, Taiga," he said.

Taiga didn't say anything. Leo continued.

"Whatever mistake you think you make, I don't want you to choose whether it's too much for me."

He slightly moved his hand, interlocking his fingers with Taiga's.

"Can you promise me that?"

Taiga looked away. Jesus, Leo was offering him everything he could be hoping for. Why the heck was he being so stubborn anyways?

For once, he decided to ignore himself.

He gently clenched his fingers around Leo's and nodded.

They stayed like that for a while. Holding hands, without muttering a single word. Eventually, Taiga looked at him again.

"Thank you."

Leo smiled. "You owe me an emotional breakdown."

"You already got yours after the match."

"Come on, that wasn't even comparable."

"You didn't specify."

They laughed for a moment. Then Leo said, "Thanks for telling me."

Taiga looked down at their hands, still locked together. He felt the same weird warmth inside he had felt at Pinetree.

"Yeah."

"I don't know about you, but I'm freezing my ass over here and I'm kinda starving. Food?"

For once, food actually sounded good.

"Yeah, let's go."
