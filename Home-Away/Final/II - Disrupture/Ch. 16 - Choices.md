### Chapter 16 - Choices

"Okay. Before dismissing you, there's another thing."

They were in the film room, after Friday afternoon practice.

"We've confirmed the exchange with the Pinetree Academy. We leave Thursday, February twenty-sixth, and come back Sunday."

"Exchange?" Aaron asked.

"Yeah. Joint training. Match on Sunday."

"Their coach is Daniel Hale," Mercer continued. "He does things differently from me, but he's a good coach. You'll follow his instructions during the joint sessions."

Gav raised his hand. "How about our classes, though?"

"This is why you're hearing about it now. Talk to your professors and let me know as soon as possible if there is any problem."

"Are we staying on their campus?" Enzo asked.

"Yes. Pinetree is a bit different from regular colleges. Their campus is pretty high up. Pack warm clothes and be ready to adjust your pace."

"High up like... on a mountain?"

"Unless they found a way to make it float, then yes, Reyes. Which is the whole point of the exchange."

Enzo looked horrified.

"You'll receive the itinerary and the athletics letter tonight. That'll answer any question about logistics. Now."

He pointed at the magnetic board behind him.

"Pinetree is almost a month away. Northbridge is tomorrow. Stay focused."

Outside the film room, conversation immediately went back to Pinetree.

"I heard Pinetree's campus is, like, made entirely of wooden cabins and lodges," Leo said.

Evan shrugged. "I wouldn't be surprised."

Enzo turned towards him. "You've done exchanges before?"

"Nope. That's a first for me as well."

"Great."

Aaron was weirdly silent.

"You don't like this?" Taiga asked.

"No, it's—"

He made a face.

"Dunno, I'm only just getting used to how Mercer wants us to play. Now we have to train with another team, on their campus, at high altitude and with another coach?"

Taiga nodded. "Yeah."

"Well..." Evan exhaled. "Guess we'll deal with it when it comes."

Taiga adjusted his strap and followed the others to the volleyball arena.

***

Enzo had his sign rolled up in his bag. He still refused to reveal the content, although he clearly had issues with glitter.

"You got the skin of a killer," Leo said, giggling.

"Ugh, it won't come off," he answered, rubbing his face.

"So, who are we meeting?" Evan asked.

"Ah right, wait."

Enzo pulled out his phone and began typing. A couple of minutes later, he said:

"Okay, Yuuto is coming out."

Shortly after, a guy in an open padded coat emerged from the athletes' entrance of the arena. White dyed hair, bare knees and volleyball shorts.

"Enzo! My dude, you actually brought everybody!"

"Yuuto! Yeah, I also made something," Enzo said, smirking.

"Oooooh I like this. What is it?"

His grin widened. "You'll see."

"Apparently, it involves lots of glitter. Or is it just fashion?"

A guy emerged from behind Yuuto. Dark hair, glasses and a pale blue jacket zipped to his chin. Taiga recognized his face before remembering where he'd seen it.

"Oh! This is Avan, my roommate." Yuuto caught his sleeve and pulled him into the group. "Avan, this is Enzo from class, and all his soccer buddies. I said bring people, and he brought a whole party!"

"Yeah. I can tell. Nice to meet you, I'm Avan Geiserford."

The voice settled it.

"Aren't you in the student council?" Taiga asked.

"Yes. I'm the president."

Right. The speech. The one Taiga hadn't listened to.

"Woah, no shit. You're the president's roommate? That's extremely cool!" Leo said.

"I-It's no big deal. I'm just a regular student like you," Avan said.

"Yyyyeah." Yuuto patted his shoulder. "He's a bit stiff at first, but don't worry he eventually loosens up."

"That's not true. Both things."

"Yeah, yeah. Just like at the film club with Ace! Don't you remember when you—"

Avan glared at him. "Yuuto."

Yuuto put both hands over his mouth.

Someone called him from inside. He looked over his shoulder.

"Ah, sashimi. Okay, I gotta go! Avan knows where to sit. Thanks for coming, guys! Be loud!"

Avan watched him disappear in the corridor. Then he turned.

"Ehm. Sorry about that. Follow me inside, please."

Leo leaned toward Taiga. "Pffft, not stiff at all."

Taiga snorted silently and followed Avan inside.

The arena was warm and already noisy. Taiga took his coat off while they found seats. Avan sat at the end of the group; Scott and Gav settled beside him. Leo dropped into the seat next to Taiga.

Enzo finally pulled out his sign.

**YUUTO DO THE VOLLEYBALL GOOD!**

That made the whole group groan and hide their faces behind their hands.

Avan looked resigned. "He's definitely gonna want that in our room."

On court, Yuuto had taken his coat off. He looked up, found the sign and laughed hard until a teammate caught him by the shoulder and turned him toward the net.

Olympus lost the first set.

Taiga understood most of what had happened. Mostly. At one point the ball had come off Yuuto's hands and gone out, and somehow that had been a point for Olympus.

"They touched it," Avan explained when Leo asked. "The blockers. Yuuto hit it off their hands."

"Oh. That's allowed?"

"That's the point."

Leo leaned back toward Taiga. "We should start doing that."

"We have corners."

"Yeah, but we could give it a cooler name."

Olympus won the second set. In the third, every time they pulled ahead, the other team caught up again.

A bad pass sent one of their players diving toward the floor. He got underneath it. Someone else kept it up, and Yuuto was already jumping.

Leo caught Taiga's forearm with both hands.

"Come on, come on—"

Yuuto dropped the ball behind the block. The defender reached it too late.

Their whole row stood. Enzo's sign blocked half the court, and Leo let go to clap. Taiga pushed the cardboard away with one hand.

"Move that fucking thing!"

Enzo moved it into Aaron's face instead.

When they sat down again, Taiga glanced at his forearm. Leo was just celebrating. No big deal.

Olympus took the set, then the fourth. Avan folded his program and applauded with the rest of them. Leo was still arguing about the last point when they started gathering their things.

They waited for Yuuto near the entrance. He emerged carrying his bag and talking before he'd reached them.

"Holy cali-maki, you guys were loud! I could hear you from the back! Did you see when I almost ate the advertising board?"

"We saw," Avan said.

"My life flashed before my eyes. Mostly lunch. Speaking of! Are we getting food?"

They got burgers at a restaurant in the University Center.
They fitted around two tables, with Avan checking that the spare chair wasn't blocking the aisle and Yuuto offering his fries around before he'd tried one himself.

"I got the big portion so everybody could. Go on!"

For a while, Taiga ate and listened. Yuuto was explaining a move he called the Phantom Drop and Enzo immediately wanted a special move as well.

"Apologies. He's a bad influence," Avan said when Taiga sighed.

Taiga shook his head. "Enzo doesn't need a bad influence."

Leo chuckled beside him. "So, Avan, Yuuto said you two are in a film club?"

Avan suddenly stopped eating. "It's—Uh... It's not a club. It was just a movie night."

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "Yuuto said 'club' though."

"Y-Yes, because..."

Avan was going slightly red.

"Ehm. Because we used to host movie nights in our room. He called it that, but it's not a club."

"Aww, shame," Leo said. "I'd love to join a film club."

"You already do more than you can manage," Taiga pointed out.

"Hey! That's not true."

He gave him a look.

"...Most of the time," Leo added.

When they parted outside, Yuuto thanked them again and Enzo insisted he take the sign. Then he and Avan headed toward the dorms, already arguing about where it would go.

"What are you doing Sunday?" Leo asked as he and Taiga turned onto the path behind the others.

"The lab write-up."

"Right. We should finish that. Computing lab at two?"

"Fine."

Leo took his phone out to put it in his calendar. Taiga waited while he walked into a bollard.

"Ouch."

***

Tuesday's lab began with an announcement from Owen.

"For the next two weeks, you'll work with someone different. The assignments are individual submissions, but I want you discussing your implementation with somebody whose habits you haven't already memorized."

Taiga and Leo looked at each other.

"You've memorized my habits?" Taiga asked.

"Unfortunately, yes."

"In a professional environment, you won't always get to choose who you work with. Better get used to it," Owen continued.

The list appeared on the screen. Taiga found his name beside Dylan's. Leo was with Rachel, two rows over.

Dylan arrived while Taiga was unplugging his charger. He had seen him in lectures, usually near the front with headphones around his neck.

"You want to use yours?" Dylan asked.

"Already got it open."

"Works for me."

The task was a small hash table. Insert, search, delete. Taiga read the requirements, Dylan asked which collision method he preferred, and they started.

For the first half hour, it was a bit weird.

Dylan was good, but he didn't really challenge Taiga's implementation. When Taiga proposed a helper function, he agreed straightaway.
It was... too easy, almost.

Across the room, Leo was explaining something with both hands. Rachel interrupted him, pointed at the screen and made him read it again.

Taiga returned to the tests.
They finished twelve minutes before the end. Dylan submitted, packed and left for work. Taiga pulled out his phone.

**Taiga:** you done?

**Leo:** almost
**Leo:** i hope 🥲
**Leo:** don't leave

He looked over. Leo was staring at his laptop with his chin in one hand. Rachel was typing.

Taiga opened the notes for the follow-up task.
When Leo finally arrived, he dropped his bag onto the empty chair.

"We were checking the wrong slot. For eleven minutes."

"Impressive."

"We were very thorough about it."

He leaned over Taiga's laptop to see what he was reading. His leg pressed against Taiga's as he leaned closer.

"Oh, don't look at next week yet. I've only just survived this one."

Taiga closed the page.
Why the hell was he recording every accidental contact now? Yet, Leo's leg remained there until they stood.

"Coffee?"

"Please."

***

By the second week of February, Olympus was on a roll. Four wins out of four matches. 
Match after match, the air in the locker before the starting whistle was getting lighter and lighter, until it became almost routine.

Then St. Vincent match came, and a cold shower of reality hit the whole team.

St. Vincent scored in the first half from a corner.
Taiga saw the header, saw Scott get a hand to it, and saw the ball cross the line anyway. For a moment he thought there had to be somebody on the post who could still clear it.

There wasn't.

After that, St. Vincent kept coming at them. Every pass backward brought another white shirt running. Olympus got through a few times, found space and then wasted it before the defenders could recover.

From the bench, Taiga could see the space behind them. Reaching it was apparently the difficult part. Evan made a run; the pass went backward. Enzo received wide; two players closed him down. Leo came deeper to get the ball, then looked up to find the space he'd left occupied by somebody from St. Vincent.

Mercer shouted at him to stay higher and he did. Gav found him there. Leo turned, put Jordan through, and the goalkeeper saved the shot with his leg.

Taiga was halfway up before he sat down again. 
The second half, though, was worse to sit through.

Taiga warmed up twice. Both times he came back to the bench still wearing his bib. Mercer used Lucas when Carter's calf tightened and made another change in midfield. Evan stayed on the left.

He knew that he probably wouldn't have changed the situation. Yet, sitting there while his team was clearly struggling made everything worse.

And apparently, on the pitch the situation wasn't better.
On Olympus's next attack, Aaron played a safe pass backward. Then another. Evan turned and asked him to come outside.

"I'm fucking waiting," Aaron shouted.

Taiga heard that from the bench.

With ten minutes left, Leo tried to slide a pass through three pairs of legs. It hit the first. He got it back, went around one player and lost it to the next.

Taiga saw him burrowing his face in his hands.

The whistle went while Scott was telling everyone to get up for one more ball.
Taiga stayed on his feet. Beside him, someone swore and kicked an empty bottle under the bench.

On the pitch, Leo stood with his hands on his hips. Aaron had already started walking toward the tunnel. Evan was patting Enzo's shoulder.

***

The atmosphere in the locker room changed dramatically. 
People still spoke. They asked for tape, moved bags and told someone to get out of the doorway. Enzo's boots hit the floor hard enough to make Taiga look over.

Mercer waited until everyone was inside.

"We'll look at it Monday. They pressed well. We rushed when we had time and waited when we needed to move it. Recover tomorrow. I want you ready to work."

He left them to change.
Aaron was still talking to Evan.

"You asked me to wait. Then you asked me to go."

"At different points in the game," Evan said.

"I know they were different points."

"Guys," Scott said, gloves still on. "Cut it. This doesn't help. We lost and it's nobody's fault. It's gonna happen again, so learn to deal with it."

Aaron opened his mouth, stopped and pulled his shirt over his head.

On the other side of Taiga, Leo was taking the tape off one ankle in small, unnecessary steps.
He caught Taiga looking and gave him an uncertain smile.
Very unconvincing.

After the shower, Leo was already gone by the time Taiga zipped his bag.
He found him sitting on a bench outside the stadium, with an unopened bottle of water resting between his hands and his bag laying on the ground.

Taiga stopped in front of him.

"Hey."

Leo looked up.

"Oh, hey. Sorry I ran off, I needed some water."

He held out the bottle of water.

"Right. Mind if I sit?"

"Not at all."

Leo shifted to make space.

People passed on their way toward the campus paths. A maintenance cart reversed near the gate, beeping at nobody.

For a while, neither said anything.
Then Leo turned the bottle in his hands.

"Welp, that was one shitty game."

"Yeah."

"Anyway. First loss out of the way." He gave a small shrug. "Had to happen eventually."

"I guess."

"How do you feel about that?"

Taiga looked at the pathway.

"I didn't even play."

"Nope, but you can still feel bad about it."

"Yeah, and what about you?"

"Like I said, it had to happen. No big deal."

"Leo."

"What?"

Taiga looked at him.

Leo tried a smile. "What? It's a game. Just one game."

"That's what you keep saying."

"Because it is. There's no reason to dramatize over a loss."

"I know, then why-"

"Taiga, I'm fine. Alright?"

Taiga stopped with his mouth half open.
The tone caught him off-guard. Sharper than usual, even though he still had that weird half-smile on his face.

"Alright."

Leo's face changed instantly. Worry washed away the fake smile.

"Shit- sorry! I didn't mean to say it like that."

"You're good."

Leo looked away and opened his bottle.

"...I'm not. I'm pissed."

Taiga snorted lightly. "Yeah, no shit."

"It's just..." Leo took a deep breath. "It's frustrating. We lost because of one stupid corner. We chased all the time, tried every space and they won for a stupid freaking corner."

He shook his head.

"And every time I got it, I thought, okay, this time. I'll get past him. I'll put Jordan through. Something. Then I've lost it again, and now everybody has to run back because I couldn't just make the easy pass."

"You're making it sound like the whole match depended on you."

"Yeah, I know that we all somehow fucked up. I just..."

He twitched his mouth.

"I wish I had done more. I know I could've done more and I feel stupid because I didn't."

Leo took a drink and wiped his mouth with the back of his hand. He went silent again.
Taiga didn't really know what to say. He understood that feeling enough to know that words couldn't really soften it.

"Sorry, I started blathering nonsense."

"You always do it. Why apologize now?"

Leo bumped his shoulder into Taiga's. 

"Fuck off"

Taiga smiled. "I'm hungry. Food?"

Leo hesitated. "I don't know, I mean... I may not be of much company right now and-"

Taiga rolled his eyes and stood up, grabbing both his and Leo's bags.

"Hey!"

He had already started towards the University Center.
