### Chapter 18 - Thin air

Somehow, Pinetree's training pitch seemed longer and longer after every sprint.
Taiga was fairly sure that Olympus had a bigger one, yet this one was way more hostile.

Hale had started Friday morning's training with something he called "physical adaptation".

"Adaptation to what, exactly?" Enzo had asked.

"Have you ever run at high altitude, Mr. Reyes?"

Enzo had lowered his hands, still looking confused.

"You'll see," Evan had said. "Away matches at Pinetree have always been a pain."

Taiga had known what he was signing up for, yet he hadn't expected the effects to be so drastic.

"Okay, five-minute break. Catch your breath."

He rested his hands on his knees. Barely twenty-five minutes into training, and he was already struggling to catch his breath during the breaks.
He slowly walked towards the benches and picked up his bottle. The water was almost ice-cold, which didn't exactly help. He put his coat around his shoulders.

Enzo dropped onto a bench. "I think I've seen God at least three times."

Leo laughed, but even he didn't want to waste oxygen on a reply.

Hale had divided them into small groups: one group passed the ball around while the other tried to take it away.
The Pinetree players were passing while Olympus chased.

Hale clapped once. "Okay, everyone, gather here, please."

Taiga put his bottle down, took off his coat and went back onto the pitch.

Aaron was looking at the Pinetree players, who were already talking to each other.

"How long before we get used to it?" he asked.

"It depends on the person, Mr. Bennett. But don't worry, your bodies have been acclimatizing since the moment you stepped off the bus. Now."

He turned to everyone.

"We'll switch to more technical training. Olympus and Pinetree players will be mixed together and we—yes, Mr. Price?"

Jordan lowered his hand. "Uh, won't training together give everything away before Sunday?"

A smile appeared on Hale's face. "Don't worry. This will not be your usual training, and I promise you'll still have plenty to find out about each other."

Mercer stood beside him. "Your match preparation stays with me. Nothing we do here changes our usual approach. Now, just focus."

"Thanks, Daniel," Hale continued. "Now, as I was saying, we're mixing teams and changing your jobs."

A couple of Pinetree players sighed and groaned, as if they already knew what awaited them. Taiga exchanged a worried look with his teammates.

"If you're an attacker, you will now play as your direct opponent in defense. So, Mr. Akatora, for example, what's your usual position?"

"Uh- left wing."

"Then you'll play as a right fullback."

Taiga was horrified.

"I've never played in defense."

Hale smiled. "That's exactly why we are doing this exercise. Knowing your opponent's role is as important as knowing your own. Now, take your positions. Goalkeepers will keep their roles, for obvious reasons."

Taiga saw Scott visibly relaxing his shoulders.

Mercer gave Hale a list of the Olympus players and their positions. Hale called them one by one and assigned them their opponents' roles.

Taiga took his position behind Aaron, who was now playing as a right winger. Leo stepped awkwardly into a position farther back in midfield.

Milo was on the other team, playing as a left fullback. "Looks like we're still against each other!"

Aaron smiled nervously. "Yeah, apparently."

When the game started, Taiga felt the same as when he first started playing organized soccer. Positions made sense until suddenly they didn't anymore.

Taiga received the ball, passed it to Aaron and immediately ran toward him so Aaron could pass back.
Instead, Aaron tried to beat Milo but failed.

Scott shouted, "TAIGA, INSIDE!"

Taiga turned and sprinted back. Fuck, too late. Milo had already passed to Dennis, who was running into the space Taiga had left behind.

Taiga chased him toward the goal, but Dennis shot before he could catch up. Luckily, Scott blocked it.

Hale whistled. "Akatora, your goal is behind you. Before trying to support Bennett here, make sure you don't leave empty spaces."

Taiga exhaled. "Right." And he jogged back into position.

"Okay, again."

Shortly after, he had the ball again. He looked up and saw Aaron running farther ahead along the right side, so he prepared to kick the ball into that space.

Right before he made the pass, he saw Aaron stopping and coming closer instead. Too late. He sent the ball ahead of Aaron, and Milo intercepted it.

Aaron pointed at his feet with both hands. "Why?"

"You were running!"

"I stopped!"

Taiga spread his hands. "You changed direction at the last second."

"Then look up first!"

He was about to answer when Hale called for another reset.
For the rest of the game, Taiga kept catching himself watching the ball while someone moved behind him. Scott's voice followed him everywhere.
Once, he managed to steal it cleanly and immediately started forward. Then he checked himself and looked for someone to pass to. Leo was raising his hand a little behind him.

"Back," Leo called.

Taiga gave it to him. Leo sent the ball toward the other side, and they managed to score shortly after.

Recovering between plays took longer than it used to at Olympus. Often, Taiga was still panting heavily when they restarted.

Aaron did better when he had room to run. He beat Milo once with a feint Taiga recognized from training at home, then drove a cross straight into Skylar's legs.

"Earlier," Hale said. "Skylar was still turning when you got past Milo."

"I wanted a better angle."

"But that extra touch gave him time to get in your way."

Aaron pulled his bib away from his chest and reset.

The next cross came early. Dennis had come back to help defend and cleared it with his head.

Aaron swore.

They finished before noon with a slower passing exercise and a walk around the pitch. When Hale dismissed them, Taiga sat on the bench and drank some water before heading to the locker room.

Leo stood beside him, holding his coat out.

"Still like the mountains?"

Taiga took it.

"Ask me after lunch."

***

They met Milo and Skylar on the upper road after eating. Luckily, Enzo had actually brought proper boots this time.
He still complained.

"Are there many stairs?" he asked.

"It's a trail," Skylar said.

"That doesn't answer the question."

Milo turned around, walking backward for a few steps.

"It's easy! Twenty minutes, maybe a little more."

"Take your time," Evan said. "We're not doing the morning again."

Aaron made a noise at that.

"Was that actually hard for everyone, or does the mountain hate me personally?"

"Both can be true," Enzo said.

"Fuck you."

"I thought I was gonna die."

Leo laughed. "I kept thinking the next break would fix it. Then we'd start again and, nope, still dying."

Taiga stepped around a patch of ice.

"The first few were fine."

"Exactly!" Aaron pointed at him. "Then suddenly I couldn't get enough air. Same fucking run."

"Yep," Evan said. "Had to slow down."

Aaron tugged his gloves on properly.

"And the positions?"

"Oh, that was terrible," Leo said cheerfully. "I kept following people too far. Hale had to come and put me back where I belonged like... four times, I think?"

"Heh... G-Gav nearly scored twice," Scott said.

Gav glanced over. "Nearly. You got in my way."

"You looked extremely happy about it," Enzo said.

"I was!"

Taiga could believe that. Gav had been demanding the ball with an enthusiasm he usually reserved for explaining things nobody had asked about.

Aaron walked beside them for a little while, boots crunching through the packed snow.

"I kept looking for someone behind me. Like I'd forgotten where I'd left myself."

Taiga laughed. "I was there."

"Well, that explains why I couldn't find you."

"You spent half the time running away."

"I'm supposed to! I'm a winger now."

"Is that what it looks like from where you usually are?"

Aaron considered. "Pretty much, yeah."

Taiga laughed and shoved him from behind. "Fuck off."

That got Leo started on the space people left when they moved. He tried to explain one passage of play with both hands, then had to stop when the path narrowed.

Aaron listened. "So you found that confusing too?"

"Yeah! It was like... you know when you take the same road for years, then one day you take it in the opposite direction and it doesn't look like the same road anymore? That!"

"Yeah... I still don't see the point of that, though."

"I guess it's too early," Evan said. "I believe tomorrow will be easier once we've had time to digest everything."

"...I hope."

Aaron's shoulders had come down a little.

Shortly after, another slope appeared in front of them.

Enzo groaned. "No fucking way..."

"Want me to carry you?" Aaron said.

"Bridal style only," Enzo specified.

"Changed my mind."

The conversation stretched out along the trail. Gav stopped at a junction to ask Milo about the longer route marked on the sign. Skylar claimed that Milo had photographed every sign on campus and still couldn't give directions.

"I know where things are!"

"You called me from behind the gym."

"Because the door was locked!"

They caught up with Evan and Scott at the next bend. The trees opened ahead of them, and a wooden railing appeared beyond the last trunks.

The valley lay below in long strips of white and dark green. He could pick out their lodge, the library roof, the two pitches on the lower terrace. A car moved along the road so slowly that he watched it until it disappeared behind the trees.

"Okay," Enzo said behind him. "Worth it."

"Could you put that in writing?" Milo said.

"No. If you ask me later back on campus, I'll deny everything."

For a while, everyone took pictures. Taiga sent one of the valley to the Camp Buddy chat. Leo came to stand beside him and held his phone over the railing.

The wind caught his hair. He gave up trying to get it out of his face and turned toward Taiga.

"Could you take one of me?"

Taiga took Leo's phone and stepped back. Leo leaned against the railing, still trying to fix his hair, and laughed when it blew loose again.

"God, it's hopeless, isn't i—"

Taiga took the picture then.

"Hey, wait! I wasn't ready."

"That makes it better."

Leo came to check. He came out looking pretty natural, smiling and adjusting his hair without looking at the camera.

"Woah, I look dope here! Did Keitaro teach you that?"

"Maybe."

"Okay, now step in. Come on!"

Taiga made a face and stood beside him by the railing. Leo held out his own phone, and Taiga leaned in far enough to fit. The photograph caught Enzo directly behind them, looking into the camera with his mouth half open.

Leo checked the screen and started laughing.

Enzo came over. "Send me that immediately."

They took a proper group one before leaving. Milo set the timer and hurried back, squeezing between Skylar and Gav at the last second.

On the way down, Aaron walked ahead with Evan. Taiga could hear them arguing about whether the first exercise or the last game had been worse.

Leo stayed beside him. "Oh no, Enzo made it his profile picture."

"Of course he did."

"It captured my essence," Enzo said from behind them.

Taiga laughed once. "I can totally agree with that."

***

On their way back to the common lodge, Leo stopped at his room to bring the laptop.

"Someone up for a movie?" He asked. 

They had nothing better to do and they would have spent the whole evening in the common lodge anyways, so Taiga agreed.

"Nah, I want to destroy Aaron's ass at pool again," Enzo said.

Aaron made a face. "I bet that sounded better in your head. Anyway, last time we drew."

"Cool. So this is gonna be the defining match."

Gav, Scott and Evan agreed to the movie as well.

They suddenly realized that watching a movie in the common room proved basically impossible. People were talking everywhere, someone turned a TV on and the players at the nearest pool table began disputing a shot.

Leo closed the laptop.

"Okay. My room. This is impossible."

They argued about which movie to watch all the way back to the dorm. Gav kept proposing intricate movies, while Taiga wanted something easy, Leo tried proposing a musical and Gav immediately ruled the category out. 
Leo picked up his phone and browsed through his watchlist. 

"Horror?"

"P-Please no," Scott said.

"Uhh... a comedy?"

Taiga rolled his eyes. "Come on."

"How about an anime? Something from Ghibli?" Gav proposed.

Leo considered. "I don't think I'm in the mood for Ghibli right now."

He kept scrolling. 

"Oh! I know one! How do you feel about a post apocalyptic world with crazy cars, tons of guns and a very nice plot?"

Taiga squinted his eyes. "Continue."

"Well, I haven't seen it yet so I don't know much. There's a desert and uh- cars?"

"You already said that," Gav said. "Anyway, I like the premise."

They agreed right before entering the room. 

Enzo's pillow occupied most of the bed, with a sweatshirt thrown across it. Leo took a chair and moved in front of his bed, putting the laptop on it. 
They say with their backs against the wall on the bed. Leo plugged the charger and started the movie, then he climbed up beside Taiga.

*My name is Max. My world is fire and blood.*

"I already like this," Taiga said.

Leo snorted.
Shortly after he picked up the biscuits left from the walk and passed them around before putting them between him and Taiga.

Later on, Gav opened his hands. "What in the name of...?"

Leo's mouth was open. "That's sick. Insanely sick."

"Why would they bring a g-guitarist with them?" Scott asked.

"And why does it spit fire?" Gav said.

Taiga chuckled. "At this point, why not?"

Without looking he reached into the packet between them and found Leo's hand already there. 
He looked down and pulled his hand automatically. Leo slightly arched his mouth and pushed the last whole biscuit toward Taiga and took the broke pieces for himself. 

Taiga held the biscuit for a couple of seconds while still looking at him. 
Again. He felt that strange, warm sensation that somehow put a stupid smile on his face.

"Thanks," he whispered.

Leo just winked in response. He leaned over the other side of the bed to bin the empty packet and sat back cross-legged.

Taiga glanced at Leo's hand, resting on his thigh merely centimeters away from his own.
With his heart beating hard, he shifted his hand a centimeters towards Leo's.
For a moment, the world reduced at the distance between his little finger and Leo's index. 
Ten centimeters, top. He moved it another bit. Then another. Did Leo just moved his own as well? Taiga moved his hand closer. His heart was beating so hard that Taiga feared Leo could hear it.
He moved it again. Now he could feel the heat irradiating from Leo's own hand.

"NO FUCKING WAY!" Gav shouted.

Taiga snapped out of it. Leo was laughing at the movie. He turned toward the screen.
His heart was still rushing, to the point where he almost had short breath. He pulled his hands back curled his fingers against his thighs.

What had gotten into him?

Now the movie was showing porcupine-like cars with steel spikes all around them attacking the convoy.
Taiga had missed the transition.

Gav stretched in the bed as the credits started. 

"That was insane."

"B-but it was actually good. D-despite the trash," Scott said.

"I'd say it was good *because* of the trash. It was good trash."

Leo nodded. "Totally agree."

Taiga rubbed his eyes. 

"Did you like it?" Leo asked.

"Mh? Yeah. By the premise, I didn't expect it to be that good."

Gav stood. "We'd better get going though, we're already late for dinner."

Leo looked at the clock. "Shit- right. Go ahead, I'll just tidy up a little and meet you downstairs."

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "Can't you do that later?"

"There's crumbs all over my bed!"

Fair. "Okay. I'll help you, or you'll miss the entire evening."

"Ah-ah."

"Okay, then we'll wait you downstairs," Gav said before closing the door behind him.

Taiga helped Leo removing all the crumbs from the bed and fix the sheets.

"You good?" Leo suddenly asked.

"Me? Yeah, why?"

"Dunno. You were weirdly silent."

"I was... watching the movie?"

"No I mean- well, nevermind."

Then he closed his laptop and put it in the desk while Taiga put on his coat.
He was halfway through the doorway when Leo called him.

"Taiga, wait."

He turned. "What?"

Leo was still standing in the middle of the room, holding his coat. He looked hesitant.

"I- uh..."

He scratched the back of his head.

"No, nothing. Let's go before we miss the entire dinner."

Taiga frowned, but said nothing and followed along.
