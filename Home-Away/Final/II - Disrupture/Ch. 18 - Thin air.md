### Chapter 18 - Thin air

Somehow, Pinetree's training pitch seemed longer and longer after every sprint.
Taiga was fairly sure that Olympus had a bigger one, yet this one was way more hostile.

Hale had started Friday's morning training with something he called "physical adaptation".

"Adaptation to what, exactly?" Enzo had asked.

"Have you ever run at high altitude, mr. Reyes?"

Enzo had lowered his hands, still looking confused.

"You'll see," Evan had said. "Away matches at Pinetree have always been a pain."

Taiga already knew what he was signing for, yet he didn't expect the effects to be so drastic.

"Okay, 5 minutes break. Catch your breath."

He rested his hands over his knees. Barely twenty-five minutes of training and he was already out of breath.
He slowly walked towards the benches and took his bottle. The water was almost ice-cold, which didn't exactly help. He put his coat around his shoulders.

Enzo dropped on a bench. "I think I've seen God at least three times."

Leo laughed, but even he didn't want to waste oxygen to reply.

Hale had divided them into small groups: one group passes the ball around while the other tries to take it away.
Pinetree players were passing, while Olympus chased. 

Hale clapped once. "Okay, everyone gather here please."

Taiga dropped his bottle, took off his coat and went on the pitch.

Aaron was looking at Pinetree players, who were already talking to each other.

"How long before we get used to it?" He asked.

"It depends on the person, mr. Bennett. But don't worry, your bodies have already started acclimatizing the moment you stepped out of the bus. Now."

He turned to everyone.

"We'll switch to a more technical training. Olympus and Pinetree players will be mixed up and we- yes, mr. Price?"

Jordan lowered his hand. "Uh, won't training together give everything away before Sunday?"

A smile appeared on Hale's face. "Don't worry. This will not be your usual training and I promise, you'll still have plenty to find out about each other."

Mercer stood beside him. "Your match preparation stays with me. Nothing we do here changes our usual approach. Now, just focus."

"Thanks Daniel," Hale continued. "Now, as I was saying, we're mixing teams and changing your jobs."

A couple of Pinetree players sighed and groaned, as if they already knew what was awaiting. Taiga exchanged a worried look with his teammates.

"If you're an attacker, you will now play as your direct opponent in defense. So, mr. Akatora for example, what's your usual position?"

"Uh- left wing."

"Then now you'll play as a right fullback."

Taiga was horrified.

"I've never played in defense."

Hale smiled. "That's exactly why we are doing this exercise. Knowing your opponent's role is as important as knowing your own. Now, take positions. Goalkeepers will keep their roles, for obvious reasons."

Taiga saw Scott visibly relaxing his shoulders.

Mercer gave Hale a list with Olympus player and their position. Hale called them one by one and assigned to their opponent's role.

He took his position behind Aaron, who was now playing as a right wing. Leo stepped awkwardly in the midfield.
