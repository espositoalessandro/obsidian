### Chapter 14

Taiga opened the room 317 door at 12:36 and found Caleb kneeling beside the mini-fridge with a thermometer in one hand.
Obviously.

Caleb looked over. “Hi.”

“Hi. What happened?”

“Nothing.”

“Then why are you measuring the refrigerator?”

“I unplugged it before leaving.”

“So?”

“It needs to stabilize before I put food back.”

Taiga looked at the three containers sitting on Caleb's desk. “You brought food from home.”

“Yes.”

“How much?”

Caleb looked at the containers. “Too much.”

Taiga smiled and dropped his duffel and the luggage beside his bed. 

“You had a good break?”

Caleb stood. “Mostly. Maya came for New Year's. My mother asked about graduate school four times.”

"Did you tell that you're a freshman?"

"Yes."

"And it didn't work."

"No."

“Excellent.”

Caleb put the thermometer on the shelf, then looked at Taiga's suitcase.

“How was Camp Buddy?”

“Still there.”

Five minutes later, Caleb asked whether Taiga had used the last of the printer paper before break. 

Normal life resumed, and Taiga unpacked.

At seven, Taiga left his room with most of his clothes still folded on top of the bed.
The cafeteria in the University Center was louder than it had been before break.

Not packed, exactly. A lot of people still weren't back. But enough tables were occupied that the room had started sounding like itself again, chairs scraping against the floor and trays knocking against counters.

Taiga heard Enzo before he found them.

“I'm saying there is no reason to put raisins in anything.”

“That is not what you were saying,” Gav replied.

“It was the central argument.”

“No. Your central argument was that raisins are an insult to grapes.”

“Which they are.”

Two tables had been pushed together near the windows. Almost everyone was already there.

Taiga immediately spotted the only blond head in the group. Leo spotted him too and beamed.

“There he is!”

For some stupid reason, Taiga's stomach decided to give a quick turn.
He shook his head.
Whatever.

"Hey."

Enzo pointed a fry at Taiga. “Akatora, settle something. Raisins.”

“No.”

He slapped the table once. “Thank you.”

“You didn't even ask the question yet,” Gav objected.

"That's still an answer."

Taiga hooked his jacket over the empty chair beside Gav.

"Did you manage to find the camp under the snow?" Enzo asked.

"Then you were lucky."

Leo reached across the table and stole one of Enzo's fries.

“Hey!”

Taiga got up to get food before Enzo could turn the theft into a legal dispute.

By the time he came back, Gav had apparently decided the raisin argument required evidence.

“Raisins have a higher sugar concentration by weight because most of the water is removed.”

“See? They're even bad for your teeth,” Enzo said.

Scott raised his hand. "I like raisins, though..."

They both stared at him.

Taiga sat and opened his drink. The conversation moved on to their breaks.

Evan had spent most of the break with family. Gav had apparently received a new chess set and then complained that the pieces were weighted badly. Aaron had trained twice during the break because, according to him, sitting still for three weeks would have killed him.

“What about you, Scott?” Evan asked.

Scott paused with his fork halfway to his mouth. “Uh—good. It was good.”

Across the table, Leo was telling Aaron something about a delayed connection on the way back. He moved his hands when he talked, nearly hit his cup, caught it at the last moment and kept going.

"And as soon as we landed, Enzo was immediately complaining about the climate."

"You've been here before," Taiga pointed out.

Enzo looked offended. "I was mourning the warmth of our homeland."

Leo looked at Taiga. “He complained from the airport to campus.”

"And you didn't?"

"YES HE DID!" Enzo shouted.

"I simply said 'holy shit' outside the airport. That's not a complaint."

Taiga smiled over the rim of his cup. "Debatable."

Leo turned with a betrayed expression. "Whose side are you on?"

He didn't answer.

Soccer resumed Tuesday. Training was training: cold, wet, and two hours of Taiga discovering that three weeks away is not good for sports.

Mercer kept his word: he resumed training as if last session was merely days before. Obviously, almost no one had followed his program to the letter. 
The cold air didn't help as well. Gloves and thermal shirts could only do so much.

The hot shower felt like a blessing. He could slowly feel the finger of his feet again.
The locker room afterward was nearly empty by the time Taiga came out. He had stayed under the hot water longer than necessary because the walk back to Residence was going to be miserable and heat was currently free.

When he came out, towel around his waist, most of the team had already gone. Scott was closing his locker, Aaron was arguing with someone on the phone about train tickets, and Leo stood three lockers down from Taiga wearing just a pair of tracksuit pants, his hair still wet from the shower.

"Holy fuck-"

Taiga looked over. Leo was fiddling with something around his neck and loudly cursing.
He caught Taiga staring and said:

"Can you help me with this shitty clasp? I never take my necklace off, but with the cold it's kinda ugly against my skin."

He was holding a red lace with golden clasps at the end. The lace was holding a golden pendant resembling the outline of a sun.

"Uh, sure."

Leo came over and lended him both ends.

"Alright, turn."

He smiled and turned his back to him.

“See, this is why I keep you around.”

"Shut up."

Taiga put the necklace around his neck and held the two ends in his hands. They were nastier than he expected.
Leo's damp hair kept making things harder.

"Hold your hair."

Leo gathered it forward with one hand. That movement made the muscles of his lean back flex a bit. The area around the shoulder blade was extremely well toned, even if he wasn't that bulky.

Taiga blinked. Clasps.

He tried again.
Whoever had made these things was a sadist.

His fingers kept brushing against the back of Leo's neck. He suddenly became very aware of his bare skin standing like 30 centimeters away from his own. 
Oh, right. He was also wearing just a towel around his waist.

"You good?" Leo asked.

"Why did they make them so fucking small?"

"Yeah, I know. That's why I never take it off."

Taiga exhaled and tried once more. Again, he couldn't help but touch Leo with his knuckles. He was... warm.
Something dropped lightly from his stomach. 
It didn't stop, though.
The sensation reached the area behind his towel. 
Taiga felt a light twitch.

Panic immediately flooded his system. His ears began raising in temperature, and his heart started beating harder.

Shit. Holy fucking shit.

He gave everything he had to focus on these freaking clasps and finally managed to snap them together.

"Okay. Done."

Taiga turned immediately toward his locker and pulled a pair of underwear from the bag.

"Yay! Thank you!" Leo said.

He didn't dare to look at him. 

"...Yeah."

Taiga kept dressing as fast as he could.
