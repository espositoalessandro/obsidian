# II - Disrupture

***

### Chapter 13

The first month of university had stretched itself into approximately a year and a half. Every day had contained a new building, a new person, a new rule, some new way to embarrass himself in front of Coach Mercer, Professor Hall or whichever random stranger happened to be standing nearby when Taiga forgot how normal people behaved.

And yet, as soon as he arrived on the gravel path, no time at all seemed to have passed.

Taiga ended up arriving earlier than everyone else. 
He stopped under the familiar entrance arc. A smile automatically appeared on his face. 

"Taiga!"

He looked down. Yoshinori was waving at him outside the office cabin.
Taiga raised a hand, his smile didn't leave.

As if summoned by hearing that name, Yuri popped out one of the cabin.

"Did you called- TAIGAA!"

She immediately rushed toward him and hugged him tight before he could even realize what was happening. 

"Oh my God, look at you! Three months of college and you're already barely recognizable!"

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "I don't grow up in three months."

"No, but..." She pinched his cheek. "You definitely look different."

"Yeah. Whatever."

"Come on, Yuri. Let him settle a second before attacking him."

Yoshinori appeared by her side.

"It's good to see you! You look good."

"Yeah, you too."

He smiled. "Come, give me your bag, I'll leave it in your cabin. You must be hungry after the trip. Aiden's already cooking and the others are in the mess hall."

Taiga lended him his duffel and followed Yuri in the mess hall.

"Hey guys! Look who's come back!", she announced.

Three heads turned toward him. Lloyd smiled first and waved at him with both hands.

"Taiga! Welcome back! It's so good to see you."

Darius raised a hand. "Hello, welcome back."

"Took your sweet time, Dynamite," Yoichi said with his arms crossed.

Taiga couldn't help but smile again. "Hey."

He sat at the table with the rest of them. The familiarity of the mess hall hit him all at once. Everything was still as he left it, except for all the Christmas decorations put everywhere. 

Lloyd immediately wanted to know every detail of Olympus architecture, since he had seen picture and was astonished by the grandiosity of the structure. Darius was curious as well, but let Lloyd ask the questions. 
Yoichi didn't see how a couple of stones could be that interesting. 
Yuri was evidently holding back with her questions. Probably saving the worst for later.
How merciful.

Ten minutes later, Aiden came out from the kitchen with his usual apron and a still holding a spatula.

"Taiga! I thought I heard your voice! How's it going buddy?"

"Hey Aiden. I'm fine, it's good to be back."

Aiden smiled widely. "Glad to hear it! Well, I won't keep you waiting for long, you must be starving! Be back in a jiffy!", and disappeared back in the kitchen.

Yoshinori joined them as well. "So, how was the trip?"

Taiga shrugged. "Normal. Boring, I guess."

"I can imagine."

They kept catching up for the next fifteen minutes, then the door opened and Goro came in. His expression was stern as usual, but when he saw Taiga it somehow softened a bit.

"Ah, hello Taiga. Welcome back."

"'Morning, Goro."

He hanged his leather jacket and took his place at the table.

"I hope your trip was alright."

"Pretty much, yeah."

"Good. So, how was college?"

"Dad!", Yuri said.

Goro blinked. "What?"

"You're already doing the parent thing."

"The parent... thing?"

"Yeah. The parent thing of asking children how was school as soon as they came back home."

Taiga looked between the two. Seeing Yuri roasting Goro never got old.

Goro switched from confused to slightly embarrassed.

Taiga smiled. 
Yeah, he was home.
