### Chapter 15 - Default

And trouble arrived.

Data Structures was the first class Taiga had on Monday. He arrived ten minutes early and had already sat down, taking a look at the slides.

"Is this sit taken?"

The voice came from his back and was recognizable enough.

"What if I say yes?" Taiga said without turning.

"I'd be in trouble because apparently I know no one here."

Leo put his bag on the desk and sat down anyway.

"That would be actually new."

A girl two lines down turned and waved at Leo, who smiled and waved back.
Taiga raised an eyebrow.

"Well... mostly no one. Not properly, at least."

Taiga sighed.

The professor began with the syllabus, moved through assessment and reached the first actual example with twelve minutes left.
They spent most of the lesson taking notes and occasionally asking the other one to repeat a passage they had missed.

Overall, that didn't went as bad as he had expected.
The class was actually interesting and Leo proved to be more tolerable during lessons. 
Slightly.

He started talking immediately after professor announced class was over.
Leo was still talking about the project requirements when Taiga turned toward the other staircase.

"And I don't mind a group project, but when they say 'shared responsibility,' what they usually mean is that one person is about to become extremely responsible for—"

"Where are you going?"

Leo stopped beside him. "Uh- Coffee."

"That's the other way."

"Oh. Ups, my feet went in autopilot."

"Evidently."

Leo laughed. "Want coffe?"

"I have Calculus."

"Right. I already suffered through that."

"Congratulations. Get out of the way."

Leo moved aside with an unnecessarily formal gesture. Taiga passed him and joined the people heading toward the next classroom.

"Lab tomorrow!" Leo called after him.

Taiga raised a hand without turning.

***

Tuesday brought the first Data Structure Lab. Professor's assistant Owen had been fiddling with the projector for about five minutes. 

"Bear with me."

Beside him, Leo was unpacking a charger that had somehow tied itself around his headphones.
Finally, Owen managed to show his desktop on the projector.
He opened the assignment page.

"Alright. Welcome to lab. We'll be working in groups of two or three people for most of the semester."

Taiga groaned. Leo looked delighted instead.

"Pair?" he asked.

"Provided you can untie that before we graduate."
 The assignment was a singly linked list: insert, remove, search, reverse. Tests and a short explanation of the cost of each operation.

"Requirements are on the course page," Owen said. "Read all of them. Including the bit under the heading that says 'Read this before asking me.'"

Taiga opened the starter repository. They started working without much disagreement for the first fifteen minutes.

Then Taiga wrote a helper function for searching the list, because writing two times the same line of code sounded criminal.

Leo didn't look as convinced.

"What's that?"

"It's a search function."

"Yeah, that returns the previous node, the current and the index."

"Because we need them in different place. So we don't have to write the same loop every time."

Leo read it again. He scrolled up, checked one of the callers and came back down.

"Okay, but search doesn't need the previous node. Insert doesn't need the current one. Now I have to remember which bit of the result I'm supposed to use."

Taiga turned toward him. "You prefer copying things?"

"You built a government department where a loop would've worked."

Taiga laughed despite himself. "It's one function."
Leo pulled his own laptop over and showed him the version he'd started. It was shorter. It also repeated most of the traversal in two places, which Taiga immediately pointed out.

They argued quietly over both screens until Owen stopped beside their table.

"Is there a problem?"

"Design disagreement," Taiga said.

"We're negotiating governance," Leo added.

Owen waited, apparently decided he did not need to understand that, and looked at the code.

"Run the tests and see if it works before assessing."

Leo's reverse failed on an empty list. Taiga's insert failed when the new item belonged at the very end.

"Your government has closed the border," Leo said.

"Your list doesn't fucking exist."

Leo put his face in one hand. "I know. I assumed the head was there."

They fixed the failures. Taiga narrowed the helper to something with an actual specific purpose, and Leo conceded that one of his loops could probably disappear. By the time Owen came back, the tests were green and they had started disagreeing over the explanation instead.

"This is fine. Submit the code today. You'll get a follow-up task for next week."

At ten to four, Taiga closed the repository and reached under the desk for his charger. Leo stayed seated, winding his own cable around his hand.

"Coffee before training?" He asked.

Taiga looked at the time. Doable.

"Alright."

The cafeteria had a line, but it moved quickly. Leo's drink had  so much drink that coffee barely contributed for color. Taiga ordered his black and they found a plance at the standing counter by the window.
Leo opened the assignment on his phone and tried to show him something.

"We're done with that."

"I know, but look."

He had renamed the helper.

`ministryOfTraversal()`

"Clear responsibility. Better documentation."

Taiga laughed and kicked him under the chair.

"Change it back before you submit."

***

The day was already grey and sad, but somehow Mercer's announcement made it even more depressing.

"Conference season begins next week on Saturday with St. Vincent. We host."

Players exchanged concerned looks across the field. Aaron lost his smug face for several minutes. Even Leo's attention snapped forward.

"It's gonna be nothing different from what we've done in fall. Same sport, same rules. Bigger crowd."

Taiga gulped. The last words sounded a bit contradictory with the premise.

In the lockers, everyone was talking about the upcoming season. Every freshman was bombarding older players with questions.
Aaron was untying his boots more silently than usual. Enzo was reacting by being even more loud. Leo was listening to something Evan was saying, while pulling his shirt off.

Taiga automatically looked up from his bench. Leo was turned with his back to him, speaking with Evan. His back was still glistening with sweat, which made every muscle even more defined. He also spotted the bloody necklace.
Something shifted in his stomach as he remembered his fingers touching his skin.

Okay, time to shower.

He undressed quickly and rushed to the stall. 

***

Taiga was in his room that evening. His phone buzzed beside the Discrete reading.

**Leo:** question about the lab

**Taiga:** what broke

**Leo:** nothing yet
**Leo:** do you think the helper needs its own test

Taiga wasn't sure whether he was joking or serious. Apparently he was both, because he asked two useful follow-up question when Taiga answered. Plus a stupid joke, obviously.
A photo followed shortly after: a sandwich whose contents were sliding out of one side while Leo apparently attempted to hold the rest together with his thumb.

**Leo:** unrelated emergency

**Taiga:** put the phone down and use both hands

**Leo:** i'm documenting the situation

**Taiga:** you're sacrificing your dinner for that

Six minutes later, a second photograph arrived.
The sandwich was now on a plate in several pieces.

**Leo:** update

Taiga laughed loudly enough that Caleb turned over.

**Taiga:** congrats, now you have a salad

**Leo:** 💀

***

Wednesday brought the second Data Structure lesson. Leo arrived and immediately dropped his head on the desk.

"Calculus at nine in the morning should be illegal."

"Nine it's not that early."

Leo turned towards him without raising his head. His face loudly disagreed.
He managed to recover during the lesson enough to take notes. 

"Coffee? I absolutely need one." Leo said, while starting toward the exit.

"Can't. I have my Calculus section in ten minutes."

"Ah, right. Tomorrow?"

"We have separate classes tomorrow."

Leo paused a second. "Fair. Well, good suffering then. See you at training!"

He went downstairs, while Taiga entered another class. By the time he had sat down and pulled out his notes, his phone buzzed.

**Leo:** do you think pigeons know when semester starts

Taiga looked around the class. There was no context.

**Taiga:** what

**Leo:** there are like 40 outside apollo
**Leo:** where were they in december

**Taiga:** somewhere warmer because they're not idiots

**Leo:** so they DO know

**Taiga:** that's not what i said

**Leo:** i'll take it

Taiga put the phone face down and returned to his notes.

***

Thursday arrived, and training got rougher. With the first conference match one week away, Mercer had to make sure they had fully recovered from the break by then.

"Rest this weekend. From Monday, we'll resume with our three days per week schedule."

Aaron complained the whole session. Apparently, training twice during the break hadn't made thing easier.

In the lockers, he was still arguing about whether Mercer's training plan was actually adequate.

"You did it twice in three weeks!" Enzo pointed out.

"And you never did it."

"And I'm accepting the consequences of my decisions."

"You can barely walk."

Taiga was pulling out one of his socks when Leo passed with just a towel around the waist and a bottle of shampoo in one hand.

His stomach twitched. Again. What the fuck?
He's been seeing Leo shirtless the whole fucking semester. Why did his brain decide to short-circuit now?

He removed one of his shin guards. One of the straps had twisted, so he straightened it. He checked the other one too.
Around him, people were heading for the showers. Leo's voice moved toward the doorway with Enzo's.
 
Taiga took his phone out. Caleb had asked whether they needed detergent.

**Taiga:** yeah

That took approximately two seconds. He continued looking at the screen anyway.

"You good?" Evan asked while headed to the shower.

"Yeah, I'm coming."

Taiga put the phone back in the bag.
This was ridiculous. There were men changing all around him. There had been men changing all around him for months. Leo had not acquired a new body since last Tuesday.

He spent the whole time in the shower studying every detail of the tiles of the floor. Still, he was perfectly aware of which stall Leo was in.
Fuck.

***

By the end of the next week, Taiga had developed a plan. Spend as much time as possible on the field before going in the locker room. Then play for time near the locker until Leo had spent at least five minutes in the shower. 
No watching him going in, no watching him coming out.
Perfect.

Taiga lasted two sessions, before considering it absurd.

Friday, the day before the first conference match, there was enough tension in the air to distract Taiga.

He could see it everywhere with the other freshmen. When Mercer corrected them, Aaron got snappier. Leo laughed too much. Enzo didn't joke.
Taiga knew it was too soon for him to play in a conference match, but he wasn't unscathed by the looming thought either.

"Okay, that's it for today. See you in twenty at Apollo for film."

Mercer dismissed them.

This time, Taiga decided to change approach. He rushed to the showers as soon as Mercer dismissed them. That was way better than wait outside like an idiot.

He was already putting his towel around the waist and pulling down his jock when Leo sat at his bench and began untying his boots. Good.

He still see him pulling his shirt off with the corner of his eye.
Okay, this was getting ridiculous. He picked the farthest shower stall and swore when the water came out hotter than expected.

A few minutes later, the door beside his stall opened. Taiga recognized the humming immediately. He closed his eyes.

Leo must've been doing this on purpose at this point.
An image of a naked Leo with an evil grin chasing him around the locker room flashed in his mind.

Yeah, definitely not something normal people usually do.

"You good? You rushed into shower like you have a meeting in five minutes."

"I-"

He cursed the universe.

"I was getting cold."

"Yeah, fair enough. I hate being all sweaty in cold air. Can't bodies understand that it's cool enough outside that they don't actually need to sweat?"

Taiga forced his gaze on the floor tiles. He was barely listening what Leo was saying.
There was just a frosted panel in between them. And it did a questionable job in keeping privacy. He could see Leo's blurred silhouette moving. In fact he could tell exactly what he was doing. 
No. Floor. Tiles. Shampoo.

"...it's freaking snowing outside! Makes no sense. Don't you agree?"

Taiga realized he had asked a question a second later.

"Uh- come again?"

Leo paused. "Were you spacing out while I was talking to you?"

"Possibly."

"Ugh. I said, it makes no sense for the university not to put heaters in the locker room while there's snow outside."

"Yeah. Criminal."

"I know right?"

Come on, he only had to rinse his hair. One last effort.
Mercifully Leo stopped talking. For exactly thirteen seconds.

"You thinking about tomorrow's match?" he asked.

"I'm not gonna play."

"That's not what I asked, though."

No, he wasn't thinking about the fucking match. Goddammit. 

Normally, yes, he would've been freaking out just to be on the bench of an actual match. Normally. When his brain used to work properly.

"Whatever."

"I'm a bit scared, truth be told. It's my first official match and I might be feeling the slightest hint of pressure, eheh."

"Didn't you say you played at school?"

"Yeah, but this is an official conference match between universities. That's a whole other tier!"

He thought for a couple of seconds.

"First five minutes. Keep it boring as hell."

Leo laughed. "You cannot recycle my wisdom."

Taiga washed the last bit of the shampoo, but didn't leave yet.

"It worked for me, should work for you."

"Of course it worked! It was *my* advice."

"Fuck off."

They kept talking for a few minutes. Somehow, Taiga forgot to be freaked out for a while.
