### Chapter 17 - Thawing

"You are forgetting your gloves."

Taiga glanced at his desk.

"Right, thanks."

"Is it gonna be that cold?" Caleb asked.

"It's like 1,400 meters up. In February. What do you think?"

"Fair enough."

Wednesday evening, the day before Pinetree.
The bag had been open on Taiga's bed since dinner, and he had somehow spent twenty minutes packing for three nights. His boots and training kit were already in the team equipment bags.

His phone buzzed.

**Leo:** how many thermal shirts have you packed?

**Taiga:** three

**Leo:** shit
**Leo:** i only got two

**Taiga:** let's hope they have a laundry room then

**Leo:** dunno
**Leo:** have washing machines made it to the mountains?

**Taiga:** no, you must scrub clothes with snow

**Leo:** thrilling
**Leo:** you want window or aisle?

**Taiga:** what?

**Leo:** on the bus

Taiga thought about it way too much.

**Taiga:** window

**Leo:** alrighty

**Taiga:** don't be late

**Leo:** hey
**Leo:** i'm excellent at important mornings

He wasn't.

Taiga was standing beside the bus with his backpack over one shoulder. Behind him, bags thumped into the luggage compartment. The practice fields were still dark beyond the parking lot.

Leo walked past him and tossed his bag into the luggage compartment.

"Morn'n."

Taiga looked at him.

"What?"

"Morning."

Taiga blinked. "Are you actually awake?"

"I dunno... prob'ly not."

Behind Leo, Enzo emerged from the other side of the bus hugging a pillow almost as wide as his body.

"He gets worse if you ask questions."

Leo turned toward him. "Cállate."

Taiga chuckled. "Yeah, I can see that."

Leo gave him the finger, climbed the steps and stopped beside a row of seats. He waited for Taiga to take the window seat, then sat and tried untangling his headphones from his scarf.

"Really excellent at important mornings," Taiga said.

"Fuck off, I'm here."

"Barely."

Leo finally got the scarf off.

Around them, the squad was settling. Scott sat near the front with his headphones already on. Aaron came down the aisle holding his phone to his ear, telling Sofia that yes, he'd brought the charger. Gav was trying to fit his backpack beneath the seat while Enzo explained why the pillow could not possibly be made smaller.

"Just squash it," Gav said.

"It needs room."

"So do I."

Mercer counted them, checked with the driver and sat down. The bus pulled out at seven.
Taiga watched the Sports Zone slipping past the window.
Beside him, Leo had already collapsed against the seat with his eyes closed.

The bus was silent for the first hour of the trip. Most of the team was sleeping, while others had their headphones on. Nobody was in the mood to talk.

The service stop restored most of Leo's vocabulary. He and Taiga bought tremendously overpriced coffee at the shop and spent most of the stop complaining about it.

"That's why they keep charging that much," Gav said.

"Coffee is survival," Leo answered.

The road began climbing not long afterward. At first there were only strips of snow beneath the hedges. Then the fields turned white, the trees thickened and the bus slowed for bends that took them farther up the slope.
Beyond the pines, a ridge appeared through the low cloud. There were houses along the lower road, smoke coming from a few chimneys, a narrow stream running black between snow-covered banks.

Leo leaned forward.

"Oh, wow."

He was looking past Taiga. Taiga moved his shoulder back to give him a better view.

"That's so much snow."

"You were standing in snow last week."

"That was dirty campus snow. This is proper snow."

Taiga looked out again. Most of the branches still held it.

"Yeah, it's pretty cool."

"Does your camp get like this in winter?"

"Pretty much, yeah."

Leo sat back. "Now I get why you like this."

"You're gonna complain immediately anyway."

"I can still appreciate it from a safe distance."

Taiga shook his head and snapped a photo of the view.

***

Pinetree Academy was built on a slope near the top of a mountain.
The buildings were mostly timber, with large windows covering much of the walls.

The bus stopped outside a long lodge with a stone ground floor and balconies facing the trees. Other buildings climbed behind it, connected by paths with the snow cleared into uneven banks.
Below the road, Taiga could see the training fields on a wide, level terrace.

Leo stepped off the bus and immediately pulled his scarf over his mouth.

"Holy fuck."

Taiga followed, stepping into a familiar cold breeze. He couldn't help but smile at the view.

Mercer was shaking hands with a man in a dark green tracksuit. Gray at the temples, sleeves pushed up despite the weather.

"Daniel Hale," the man said when the last few players came around the bus. "Welcome to Pinetree Academy! We'll get you inside in a moment."

He gave Mercer an envelope and pointed out the entrance.

"Room list and keys. Your equipment can go in the storage room on the ground floor. Lunch is open until one. The dining hall—"

He pointed to a smaller lodge attached to the side of the main one.

"—is that one. Now, settle in. Today is mostly a chance to get the journey out of your legs and find your way around. Tomorrow we start training properly."

A guy beside him stepped forward. Tall, broad through the shoulders, with green hair showing beneath his hood. He wore the same Pinetree jacket as Hale, over a loose sweatshirt.

"Hi, guys. I'm Dennis Warrington. Captain. You'll probably see me around more than you want to."

Scott shifted beside Gav.

"Uh... Scott. Stirling."

Dennis offered his hand. "Good to meet you, Scott."

Scott shook it.
Mercer handed Gav the room list.

"Choose your rooms and roommates, then go register at the reception."

Pairs formed. Evan grabbed Aaron before he could protest. Gav was already walking with Jordan, while Enzo took a key and nudged Leo.

"Come on, I'm freezing my ass off."

Leo turned for half a second toward Taiga, giving him a hesitant look. Then he followed Enzo.

"Yeah, coming."

Taiga's stomach sank a little.

He shook his head. Whatever.

He looked around and found Scott holding a key and looking slightly terrified.

Taiga approached him. "Hey. Want to share with me?"

Scott's shoulders relaxed a bit. "Oh, yeah, s-sure!"

They followed the others into the main lodge, where they told the receptionist their rooms and their names.

"Okay, perfect," Hale said. "Go settle in. See you in the dining hall, then. Dennis, can you guide them to the dorms?"

"No problem."

Dennis guided them out of the main lodge and up the road toward the residential area.
There were several smaller buildings placed along the road.
Pinetree had three lodges for male students, three for female students and one large lodge with a common room.

"That's where we usually spend the evenings," Dennis said, pointing at the common lodge.

"Not much to do outside in the evening, especially in winter. So most of us gather here."

"Does it have a fireplace?" Gav asked.

"Of course."

"I'm sold."

***

Dennis took them into the nearest of the men's lodges and up to the second floor. The rooms in this wing were kept for visiting teams. Most of the doors stood open, showing beds already made and empty desks beneath the windows.

Room 214 had two beds, two desks and a radiator that knocked when Scott turned the valve. Almost everything was made of wood, giving the room a real mountain-cabin feel. Taiga liked it immediately.

"Can I take this one?" Scott asked, pointing at the bed farther from the window.

"Yeah."

Taiga put his duffel on the other bed. The window faced a bank of pines, their lower branches buried in snow.

For a while, the only noise came from the corridor. Someone was trying a key in the wrong door. Enzo was explaining something about his pillow.

Scott glanced toward the sound, then went back to unpacking.

Taiga took his phone out. He still had the photograph from the bus open. He sent it to the Camp Buddy group, followed by another through the bedroom window.

A knock came at the open door a few minutes later.

Leo leaned in. "Oh, here you are. We're two doors down."

"Congrats. Did Enzo's pillow fit in the room?" Taiga said.

"Barely. It takes up half his bed."

"How did he even bring it from home?"

"He didn't. He bought the exact same one online the very first day we arrived at Olympus."

Taiga blinked.

"That's disturbing."

"Can't he sleep without it?" Scott asked.

Leo shrugged. "Apparently not."

He came over to look through the window. Beyond the nearest trunks, a narrow path curved out of sight toward the common lodge.

"You've got a better view than ours. All we can see is trees."

"We see a path. Very interesting view."

"Still better than just trees. Anyway, you two ready to go?"

Taiga glanced at Scott, who simply nodded.

"Yeah."

***

They walked back down to the main lodge and through the corridor to the dining hall. Windows ran along one side, looking over the lower campus. Two rows of tables with fixed benches filled the hall. A few Pinetree players sat at the far end with laptops and half-finished drinks.

They got food and took a table near the windows. Taiga sat between Aaron and Evan, with Leo opposite him.

The food was good. Hot rice, chicken, vegetables that hadn't been boiled into surrender. Enzo tried a mouthful and visibly relaxed.

"Hey! You guys from Olympus?"

A guy had stopped beside Leo, tray in hand. His short hair was white, with sky blue underneath, and he had a pale jacket hanging open over his shirt.

Leo nodded. "Yep."

"Awesome! Mind if I join you? I'm Milo. Milo Kristensen."

"Yeah, sure!" Leo said, shifting toward the window.

Milo put his tray down.

"So, how was the drive? Did it take long to get here?"

"Yeah, around four hours," Aaron said.

"Dang. How're you liking it so far?"

"It's... pretty intense. I've never seen a campus on a mountain."

"I know, right? It's pretty cool. Except during storms. You really don't want to wander outside then."

"And what do you do around here?" Leo asked.

Milo shrugged lightly. "Mostly hang around campus. There are some sick hiking trails. In the evening, pretty much everyone goes to the common lodge. There are buses to the city if you want something different."

Leo turned to Taiga. "Sounds like your paradise."

Taiga kicked him under the table.

"What about Olympus, though?" Milo asked.

They talked about Olympus and showed him some photos.

Milo was holding Leo's phone, looking at a photo of the stadium. Another guy walked past him with a tray. Milo glanced up.

"Hey, Skip! Check this out!"

Brown skin, cropped black hair, small black earrings. A yellow hood lay over the collar of his black jacket.
When Taiga looked at him, something about his face looked familiar.

He glanced at the screen.

"That your campus?"

"Yeah," Evan said. "It gets a bit extreme sometimes when it comes to sports."

"Do you host the World Cup in there? It's fucking huge."

"Heh, I know."

Milo's eyes were sparkling. "Ohh, I can't wait to see it! I bet playing away at Olympus gets pretty intimidating."

"It's gonna take some time for the away match," the guy said.

Milo waved him in. "Come on, sit with us!"

The guy put his tray on the table and sat next to Milo. "Skylar Pascual. Nice to meet you."

"That's a freaking cool name," Aaron said.

Skylar blinked. "Uh... thanks?"

Milo giggled. "That's not what we call him, though—OUCH!"

Skylar had kicked him under the table. "Shut up."

"What position do you guys play?" Evan asked.

"I'm usually the right winger. Skylar is our beloved striker. You should've already met our captain, Dennis. He's our center-back," Milo answered.

Aaron frowned. "Usually?"

"Well, our coach loves versatility. He makes us play in other roles from time to time."

"Hale put me at left-back last week," Skylar said.

"Yeah, and you spent the whole game trying to get back up front!"

"You were assigned striker and suddenly we found you against the fullback."

"What can I say? We are natural enemies."

That, Taiga could agree with.

Conversation spread along the tables. Skylar and Evan were talking about the pitches; Aaron asked which Pinetree players were in their year. Milo wanted to know how far away Olympus's dorms were from everything else.

"Here, look," he said when Leo asked about their campus. "It's easier if I show you. The map makes it look flat, but it absolutely isn't."

He opened it on his phone and pointed out the training building, the library and a road running above the residences.

"Woah, look at this!" Leo turned the screen toward Taiga.

Several trails continued beyond the buildings. One ended at an overlook.

"How far's that?" Taiga asked.

"From here? Twenty minutes, maybe. Depends how fast you walk."

Skylar glanced over. "Longer if he comes."

"Hey, I like stopping for pictures! That's allowed."

"Still, longer."

Milo stuck his tongue out at him. Then he turned to the rest.

"We can take you there tomorrow, if you want. Sadly, we've still got classes today."

Leo grinned. "Yeah!"

Milo smiled back. "Ookay, deal! Now I think..."

He checked the clock.

"Yeah, we gotta skedaddle. We finish at four. If you guys wanna meet up, we can show you around before it gets dark!"

"That'd be really nice of you, thanks," Evan said.

"At the library?" Gav asked.

"Yeah, perfect! There's a coffee stall right inside, too" Milo answered.

"That's all you had to say," Leo said.

Milo took out his phone. He and Leo exchanged numbers, and a moment later the Apollo chat had a photograph of the library entrance.

Skylar handed Milo the backpack he'd left on the bench.

"Come on."

"Right! See you guys later!"

They left through the other door, Milo still talking as he hurried to catch up.

***

The library occupied the ground floor of one of the classroom buildings. It was warm inside, with plenty of people sitting with laptops, books and notebooks. Large windows lined most of the walls.
From here, they could see the main lodge below them and the sports terrace farther down. Snow lay against the fences and behind the goals, but the green surface was clear.

Leo spotted the coffee stall first, just to the left of the entrance.

"This is so cool! Why don't we have a proper coffee shop in our library?"

"Yeah," Gav said. "The coffee from the vending machines is hideous."

Taiga approached the stall. "One black coffee, please."

The others followed. They sat at a table near the windows, discussing whether sweat would freeze on their faces during training.

Shortly after, Milo and Skylar entered.

"Hey, guys! I see you've already settled in. That's great. Come on, let's go before it gets too dark."

Outside, the light had turned pale gold on the upper roofs. The road beyond the library climbed toward another group of buildings, while a narrower path curved between two lecture halls.

Milo took the path.

"This way's quicker when it's not frozen. Unless you want to see the administration building, which... I don't know why you would."

"You're really selling it," Evan said.

"I've only been in there when something's gone wrong."

Skylar put his phone away. "That's every week."

"It was one stupid form!"

Leo laughed beside Taiga. He asked which office, then somehow they were comparing the registration systems at both colleges.

They reached the road again. Milo pointed at the biggest building.

"That's our common lodge! It has, like, three rooms with a fireplace in the center and couches all around. It's hygge as hell!"

Aaron frowned. "Hygge?"

"It's a Danish word for 'cozy'," Skylar said. "This campus is pretty much inspired by Danish architecture."

"What do you usually do there?" Leo asked.

"Well, there's a pool room with, like, three or four tables, a few table-tennis tables and other stuff like darts and so on."

"No fucking way, you have pool tables here?"

"Yup! We're gonna have lots of time to play, don't worry."

Leo covered his mouth with his hands. "I'm transferring here."

"You'll last, like, three days," Taiga said.

"But those would be three glorious days."

"Sometimes the weather can be pretty rough here," Skylar said. "If there's an oncoming storm, they don't allow people outside until it's passed."

Milo made a face. "Ugh, yeah. Once I got stuck in the dining hall for like two hours."

"Okay," Leo said. "Transfer withdrawn."

Taiga snorted. "That was quick."

"Well, anyway," Milo continued, "if you keep following the road uphill, the trail to the overlook starts just beyond the last building."

Leo nodded. "Noted."

They took a different path back around the library. Enzo discovered that walking downhill on snow in sneakers wasn't a very good idea.

"Ouch."

Evan gave him a hand.

"I hope you've packed better shoes," Skylar said.

"Ugh, I hope so too."

They reached a wide lawn beside the library, on a terrace a couple of meters above the main lodge.

The lawn was circular, surrounded by a low stone wall, with benches all around.

"And this is our park. We usually hold events here in summer. At Christmas, we even had a few markets."

Taiga looked around. Here at Pinetree, there were far fewer people wandering around or sitting on benches.
It was definitely quieter.
The lawn was mostly empty, with just a few people watching the sun set behind the pitches.
He pulled out his phone to take a few pictures of the valley below.

"...Oh, and you should have seen our Halloween party! Here we—SHIT!"

Taiga turned and saw Skylar brushing the remaining snow off his gloves.
Milo had one hand inside the neck of his jacket.

"Dude!"

"What?"

"That went down my back!"

"Did it?"

Milo bent to scoop up snow. Skylar waited until his arm came forward, then stepped aside.

The snowball hit Enzo, who was still holding his coffee.
He stopped for a second and looked down at his coat.

Taiga put his phone away. He smelled trouble.

"Oh, shit." Milo straightened. "Sorry! That was for him!"

Enzo brushed the snow off his coat. Then he finished his coffee and dumped the cup in the trash can beside him.

"Alright."

He bent and scooped a handful of snow.

Milo backed away, laughing. "Wait, wait! I said sorry!"

"And I accept your apologies."

The snowball caught Milo in the chest. Leo laughed, which drew Enzo's attention to him.

"Oh, come on—"

Leo barely turned before the next one broke across the back of his coat.

"Okay, you'll regret that," he said.

Aaron pulled his gloves on.

"Finally. Something interesting."

Taiga sat on the wall, enjoying the warfare. Enzo hit Leo right in the back of the head. Leo immediately shoved both hands inside the collar of his puffer jacket.

"SHIT, IT'S COLD!"

Taiga laughed hard. Until something hit him on the shoulder. He turned and saw Aaron grinning.

"Fine."

And so it began.

At first, Taiga teamed up with Enzo and Skylar. Aaron joined Milo and Leo behind a bench.

"COWARDS!" Enzo shouted.

"We're exploiting the environment!"

Gav, Evan and Scott joined a minute later as a separate team. Taiga's eyes were still on those cowards behind the bench.

"I'll bait them," Taiga said.

Skylar nodded and bent to prepare ammunition.
Taiga made two snowballs and ran around to their flank. When his line of sight was clear, he hit Leo on the side.

Leo and Milo immediately stood up to shoot back, but that was a big mistake. At least three snowballs hit them in the back.

Leo was laughing. "YOU BASTARD!"

Taiga chuckled and flipped him off as he ran back to his allies.
Aaron hit him on his way back. Fair enough.

The teams lasted less than ten minutes before everyone got separated. At that point, it became pure carnage.

Skylar threw at anyone who turned away from him. Evan intercepted one meant for Scott, looked down at his sleeve and spent a few seconds making a snowball considerably larger than the others.

Skylar started backing away.

"Don't."

Evan advanced.

"That'll kill someone."

"You'll manage."

Skylar ran. Evan's throw hit him between the shoulders and nearly knocked him to the ground.

Milo cheered from behind the bench, then got hit by Aaron while he was standing up.

Taiga's next handful fell apart before he could throw it. He took more, packed it harder and looked for Leo.

Gone.

He moved around the end of the bench.

Leo was crouching there with a small pile of snowballs laid out beside his knee.

He looked up.

"No, no, don't—"

Taiga kicked loose snow over his ammunition.

"HEY!"

Taiga was still laughing when Leo jumped straight at him, grabbing him around the waist and knocking him down.

They fell into the soft snow and immediately began wrestling, trying to shove snow inside each other's jackets.
Taiga managed to pin Leo down, holding his hands against the ground while kneeling over him.

"Yield!"

Leo was still chuckling while trying to wriggle free, but Taiga held him tight.
Then he settled.

"Alright, alright."

Taiga let go of his hands. They were both still smiling, catching their breath, when something hit Taiga directly beneath the collar.

"HOLY FUCKING—"

Not far away, Aaron was brushing snow off his gloves.

"You were such an easy target."

Leo was rolling in the snow with both hands on his stomach. Taiga could hear him trying to breathe between laughs. He turned to Aaron.

"You're so dead."

"That was perfect!"

"I'm killing you!"

"You have to catch me!"

Taiga got most of the snow out. The rest had become cold water against the back of his shirt.

Then he heard Milo shouting, "Alright, guys, it's getting dark. Truce?"

The sun had sunk behind the mountains and the lights had gone on around the campus.

Aaron came over with his coat open.

"Truce?"

Taiga looked at him.

"Watch your back."

"Alright, fair enough."

He brushed snow off Taiga's shoulder before heading off with Evan.

Leo stood up, still breathing hard. Taiga realized his face hurt from laughing.

"Oh my god," Leo said between breaths. "That was so fucking fun."

Taiga glanced at him. "You're soaked."

"So are you."

Taiga looked down. The front of his coat was covered in white smears, and one pant leg was wet almost to the knee.
Slowly, the cold started creeping in.

"Okay, now I'm starting to freeze," Leo said.

"Yeah. Let's go dry off."

"Hold on."

He pulled out his phone and framed the lawn. There were trenches where they'd run and broken snowballs across the benches. Leo took a photograph, then stepped backward to get more of the damage into it.

"Come on," he said, putting the phone away. "I need something hot."

They walked to the common lodge together. The entrance was warm, with a rack of wet boots beside the door. Milo pointed out the kitchen, and they got hot tea before heading back to their rooms to change.

***

At dinner, Milo had his phone out between mouthfuls.

"Wait, there's another one. Look at Aaron."

He turned the screen toward them.

Aaron stood halfway across the lawn, one arm still extended after a throw. Behind him, Skylar was trying to run and get snow out of his collar at the same time.

"Send me that," Aaron said.

"Already did! Leo's got all of them."

"Delete it," Skylar said.

Milo smiled and put his phone safely on the other side of his plate.

The dining hall was busier now. Players had spread between the tables instead of keeping to their own teams. Someone near the windows was showing Scott a save on his phone. He leaned closer, hair falling across his face, then pointed at something on the screen.

"You graduating this year too?" Dennis, their captain, asked Evan.

They started comparing what they still had left to finish.
Milo wanted to know whether there was snow where Leo came from.

"Nope. Santo Domingo."

"Oh, wow! So this is still new for you?"

"New enough that I keep forgetting it goes inside your clothes."

Skylar reached for the bread basket.

"My cousins in Cebu keep asking me for snow pictures. Should send them that."

"The one of you?" Milo asked.

"Of him."

He pointed at Taiga.

"Don't," Taiga said.

Skylar grinned.

"Oh, so you're a tropical guy too!" Enzo said.

"Yeah, I'm from Bacolod, in the Philippines."

"How did you end up on a mountain?" Leo asked.

"My mum's from here and they had transferred here for work when I was a child."

"You go back often?"

Skylar shrugged. "Usually once per year during summer break."

"And you, Milo?" Taiga asked.

"Me? Denmark in Europe! Came here last year."

"You got snow in there?" Leo asked.

"Oh, you have no idea. We barely get any sun in winter. Not that I tan much anyway." He pushed up his sleeve. "Look at this!"

"That's why we call him Milk," Skylar added.

"Oh, fuck off. Plus, didn't you say it was because of my hair?"

"They can both work."

They kept talking for a while about their homelands. Skylar, Leo and Enzo started comparing their islands and their customs, which were surprisingly different even though they were both tropical places. Milo talked about how actually cold his place could get, which made Pinetree look like a tropical resort.

After dinner, they went to the common room.
They left their boots on the rack by the entrance and hung their coats on the hooks above it.

The common room was busier now. People had spread between the low couches facing the stone fireplace and the two long tables farther from the heat. Someone had taken a stack of board games off the shelf and left them on a chair.

The fire was lit. 
Taiga took the right-hand end of a couch and stretched his feet toward it. The warmth reached his socks almost immediately. He hadn't realized how much he still wanted that.

Leo sat beside him.

"Okay. This is nice."

"Yeah."

Skylar found a deck of cards on the shelf. Milo pulled a low table between the couch and two armchairs, while Enzo set a bowl of pretzels on the end table beyond Taiga's elbow.
Scott sat beside Enzo.

"What are we playing?" Leo asked.

"Bullshit?" Skylar said.

Gav sat in an armchair. "What's that?"

"You need to put down all your cards to win," Milo said. "The card rank you can put down increases from ace to king for each turn we take. On your turn, you can put down as many card as you like, face down, provided they are the same rank."

Gav frowned. "If they are faced down, how can you tell they are the same rank?"

Milo smiled. "That's the trick. You can lie and someone can call your bullshit. If they are right and you lied, you take the whole central pile. Otherwise, they take it."

"Skipping allowed?" Leo asked.

"No skipping," Dennis said, taking the other chair. "And Jokers out."

They all sat down on the floor over cushions. Taiga leaned his back against the couch.
Milo started dealing.

"I like how that's the only rule everyone agrees on."

"We haven't agreed yet," Skylar said.

Evan joined them halfway through the discussion, listened for a minute and asked whether they could start before breakfast.

They settled it.

For the first few turns, everyone behaved reasonably. Then Enzo put down three cards.

"Two fours."

Taiga blinked. "That's three cards."

"Right. Three fours!"

"Bullshit," Evan called.

Enzo turned them over and started laughing. Milo leaned forward to look. Queen, ace and a seven.

"Dude! None of those are right!"

Taiga pushed the pile toward him.

"Take your shit."

Skylar played quickly. Barely glanced at his hand, put something down and looked straight at whoever seemed most likely to challenge him.

Milo called him twice.

Wrong both times.

"The grin," Milo said, collecting the second pile. "I thought you were trying not to laugh!"

"I was."

"Oh, you're such an ass."

"That's called bluff, dummy."

Dennis was harder to read. He kept talking to Evan through his turns, putting cards down without breaking off the conversation. Taiga called him once and immediately regretted it.

Across the table, Gav had started counting under his breath. Obviously.

Scott suddenly froze on his turn.

"I- Uh..."

They waited.

"O-One eight."

Taiga raised an eyebrow. "Bullshit."

Scott lowered his head and turned his card. A three. 

"I-I had no eights..."

"You need to work on your bluff skill."

Leo set his cards on the cushion and leaned across him to reach the pretzels. Their knees touched and his left palm came down on Taiga's thigh, just above the knee.

Taiga went still.

Leo took the bowl with his other hand and sat back. He shifted the hand back on his thigh, but half of it stayed loosely on Taiga's leg.

Taiga glanced down. Then at Leo, who simply held the bowl toward him.

For a second, neither said anything.

Taiga took a pretzel.

"Thanks."

Leo smiled a little and put the bowl on the table.

"Bullshit," Enzo said.

Milo turned them over: both wrong.
He dropped his head back against the chair.

"Aww, come on! I thought I had that one!"

"You announce it with your whole face," Aaron said from behind him.

"I do not!"

Aaron imitated him. Badly, but recognizably enough that Milo started laughing too.

Taiga's stomach dropped a little when he felt Leo's little finger moving slightly on his thigh. He tried very hard not to look down.

When Leo's turn came, he lifted his hand to pick up his cards.

Taiga let out a slow breath through his nose and reached for another pretzel.
He could still feel the warmth of Leo's hand on his leg.

Milo got quieter as his hand grew smaller. 
Dennis put down two cards.

"Queens."

Milo looked up.

"Bullshit!"

Dennis paused. Milo fanned out three queens for everyone to see.
For a second, everyone just looked at them.

Dennis turned over a four and a nine.

"Fair enough."

"Oh, I got you!" Milo pushed the pile toward him. "I finally got you!"

Skylar leaned back and laughed while Dennis collected the cards. Milo remained delighted through the next three turns, including the one where Skylar made him pick up half of them again.

Leo played more slowly during the second game. Once, Taiga had to point at the table before he realized it was his turn.

"Mh? Yeah, sorry, I was thinking."

"Think faster."

He put down one card, got called and took the pile. Taiga laughed.

Leo bumped his elbow. "Don't enjoy it so much."

After that round, Leo handed his cards to Aaron.
