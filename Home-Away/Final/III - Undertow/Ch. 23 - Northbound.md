### Chapter 23 - Northbound

At 9:28, Taiga was nervously waiting in the common room. No one else was there, which made the whole place feel wrong.

He had already typed *you awake?* in Leo's chat, when the elevator doors opened and a very excited Leo appeared.

Taiga deleted the text and put his phone away. "Morning."

"Good morning!" he said with a smile.

"You're weirdly early."

"Yeah, well, I woke up at seven and couldn't get back to sleep." Leo wrestled the strap back onto his shoulder. "Shall we?"

The sky outside was grey and an annoying wind made the walk to the station not a pleasant stroll.

"Come on," Taiga said. "Platform seven."

They got their duffels up onto the rack and pushed the smaller bags under the seats in front. Leo sat by the window. The train had barely cleared the platform when he pulled out his phone to take a picture.

"Of what?" Taiga asked. They were passing a wall with a drainpipe every ten feet.

"My mom asked me to let her know when we'd left."

"You could tell her in words."

"Ugh. Pics are quicker."

Leo locked his phone and put it away. "I don't even know where we're heading."

"North."

"How long?"

"A couple of hours by train, another by bus."

"Right. By the way, how many scoutmasters are there now?"

Taiga counted on his fingers. "Five, plus camp president. Most of them don't stay for the offseason, they prefer to work part-time."

"Right. And... where am I going to sleep?"

"They'll set up a couple of beds in a cabin. When we visit, we usually sleep there."

"We're sharing a cabin?"

Taiga opened his mouth, then closed again when he realized. 

"Yeah," he said after a second.

Leo looked down. "Right. Cool. How about Yoichi?"

"Since Yoichi's living there, they built him a small lodge after the last term."

"No way! He's got his private house now?"

"Yeah. They actually wanted to build me one as well. That's when I started thinking about college. It felt too... I dunno..."

"Definitive?"

"Kinda, yeah."

Leo nodded. "Yep, makes sense. Do you regret it so far?"

Taiga shook his head. "Not yet."

"Wow, optimistic."

"Well, I have to share a campus with you."

Leo shoved his shoulder. "You should be thanking me, moron."

...Yeah, probably. 
Although, Taiga didn't say it.

***

The bus left them beside a gas station with two pumps and a bait shop with plywood over the windows. Taiga put his bags down by the bench. The last time he'd been there, the snow had reached the seat. Now it was dry everywhere, and new leaves were starting popping out of the trees.

Leo looked back at Taiga. "This is it?"

"No."

"Good, because I was about to have questions."

"Someone should be coming to pick us up."

Five minutes later, a tan pickup with the camp buddy logo on the front doors came around the bend, rattling a little over the broken asphalt. 

Yoshinori pulled up beside the bench and welcomed him with his usual proud, insufferable smile.

"Hello guys!"

"Hey, Yoshi."

He got out of the pickup and gently hugged Taiga. "Glad to have you back, Taiga."

"Yeah, same."

Then he turned to Leo. "Hello, you must be Julio, right?"

Leo's head swung toward Taiga. "Why did you do that?"

"What? It's your name."

"Only my mom calls me that!" Leo sighed and straightened up to shake Yoshinori's hand. "Yes, sir. Just Leo."

"I'm Scoutmaster Yoshinori. You do not need to call me sir, though."

"Give him twenty minutes," Taiga said.

Leo ignored him. "Nice to meet you, Yoshinori."

"And you, Leo. Thank you for coming to help. Here, give me."

Yoshinori took both their duffels and put them in the back of the pickup.

The drive took less than five minutes. When the car turned and took the gravel path ending with the big Camp Buddy arch, Leo leaned over the window. Taiga saw him smiling.

"That sounds very official," Leo said.

Taiga snorted. "Were you expecting a scam?"

"One never knows."

Yoshinori pulled into the newly constructed parking lot beside the entrance arch. Taiga opened his door. Yuki hit him from the side before he'd straightened up. He caught himself on the car and got a hand under her collar.

"Hi. Yeah, hi. Hang on, my foot's still in the car."

She whined and put both paws on his leg. There was already mud on his jeans and he hadn't even made it off the gravel.

"Tch. Look what crawled back."

Yoichi was approaching with his hands in his jacket pockets. Taiga rubbed both sides of Yuki's head and waited for her to get down.

"Missed you too, dog."

"Tch. As if."

Leo came around the back of the car with Taiga's canvas bag in one hand. Yoichi gave him a slow look. "Who's this?"

"Hi! I'm Leo." He put the bag down and offered his hand. 

Yoichi glanced at it, then back at him. "You were the white guy from Halloween. The one in toilet paper."

Leo blinked and lowered his hand. "I- Yes?"

Yuki finally noticed Leo. She lifted her head, and he crouched down with his hand low and waited for her to come to him.

"Hey."

"Don't baby her," Yoichi said.

"I haven't touched her."

"Your voice is annoying."

Yuki sniffed his hand, then his jeans. By the time he'd sat back on his heels she had her chin on his wrist.

"Traitor," Yoichi muttered.

Yuki immediately tried to climb into his lap. Taiga lifted both duffels from the ground and nudged Leo.

"Come, let's go inside."

They had barely crossed the arch, when Yuri pushed the door of one of the lodges open with her elbow, holding a basket of laundry. When she saw Taiga she exploded immediately.

"KYAAA! Taiga! You really came! Come here!"

Taiga took one step back. "Yuri."

She set the basket down on the step and hugged him anyway. "Aww~ look at you! You look wonderful! College agrees with you."

"It doesn't."

"It absolutely does. Look at those shoulders of yours!"

Leo turned away. Taiga pointed at him from inside the hug. "I can see you."

That only made him laugh harder. Yuri finally released Taiga and caught sight of Leo.

"Hello! You must be Taiga's friend!"

"Yep! Hi, Leo."

"I'm Yuri," she said, taking his hand with both of hers. "Welcome to Camp Buddy!"

"Thanks, ma'am! It's a pleasure."

"Just Yuri, please! How was the journey? Did you two already eat something?"

"Just travel snacks," Taiga said.

Yoshinori stepped beside them. "Then you can drop your things in cabin two. Aiden has already prepared lunch, we'll be waiting for you two."

"Right." Then he turned to Leo and nodded towards the cabins. "Come."

As they approached the cabins, Leo's smile became more and more stupid. Outside the cabin, Taiga was about to say something about it, but he let it be. They left their boots outside and stepped in.

Cabin two smelled of cold wood and floor cleaner. Two lower bunks had been made up across from each other.

Leo looked around. "This is much bigger than I expected! Did you sleep here?"

Taiga dropped his duffel beside one bed. "Yeah, I've been sleeping in this one since my first term. Last summer, this was my bed."

He put a hand on the post of the bunk above him. The three marks he'd made with his pocketknife were still there. One was longer than the others because he'd slipped.

Leo came over to see what he was looking at. "Those your claw marks?"

"Maybe."

"Did somebody keep stealing your bed?"

"No. It was a rainy day and I was bored."

"Aw, shame. I came up with a whole reason for you."

"One does not exclude the others."

"Heh, fair. Where are the bathrooms?"

"There." Taiga pointed at a door at the back of the cabin.

"Oh. I didn't expect those to be inside the cabins." Leo stuck his head in the bathroom. "I had a whole speech ready about being brave in an outhouse."

"I can find you one if you're disappointed."

"No, no. I think I can live with a bathroom."

***

"Taiga! Welcome back, buddy!"

"Hey."

Aiden was carrying a big pot when they entered the mess hall. He dropped it on a table where the others were already sitting, and hugged him.

"Look at you! First semester survivor."

"I'm the same one you saw four months ago..."

"You're definitely not!"

He let Taiga go and turned to Leo. "Hey there! I'm Aiden!"

"Hello, I'm Leo! Nice to meet you!"

Aiden shook his hand vigorously.

"Always a pleasure to have friends brought here! This place can become pretty boring and silent during offseason, y'know. Come, I was just about to serve the stew."

Most of the tables were pushed against the walls for the off-season. At the long one nearest the kitchen, Goro had a folder open beside his bowl. He closed it when Taiga came over.

"Taiga, welcome back. It is good to see you here again."

"Good to see you too," Taiga said.

Goro gave him a small nod and turned to Leo. "Mendez, I presume."

Leo stood a little straighter. "Yes, sir."

"Ehem. Yoshinori informed me that you chose to spend your university recess assisting us with preseason preparations. Your help is much appreciated."

"T-Thank you, sir!"

Aiden set a bowl down in front of Leo. "Eat first. Goro can turn anything into an orientation speech if you give him enough time."

Goro looked offended. "It was not supposed to be a speech!"

Yuri sat beside her father. "Dad has been in preseason mode since February."

"I have been *planning* since February."

"That's exactly what I said."

Leo took a bite, then reached for his water. "Oh, wow. This is really good."

"That's what I like to hear, buddy!" Aiden got him another piece of bread. 

"This is preseason food. Hot, filling, and there's enough of it that nobody gets to complain they're hungry later."

Yoichi, already eating, looked into his bowl. "It's stew."

"Correct. Good job."

"Psh."

"Keep talking and I'll assume you don't want seconds."

Yoichi pulled his bowl closer. "Didn't say that."

Lloyd and Darius came in while Taiga was wiping stew off his thumb. Lloyd had a roll of drawings under his arm.

"TAIGA! There he is! You came back during actual work season. I knew college hadn't ruined you."

"Hey, Taiga," Darius said.

"Hey, guys."

"Oh! New volunteer." Lloyd put the roll down and shook Leo's hand with enough enthusiasm for both of them. "Lloyd Sirius. Architect, scoutmaster, occasional victim of unrealistic deadlines."

Darius held out his own hand. "Darius Najjar. Foreman. I build things. I'll be with you on the cabin row tomorrow."

"I'm Leo! Nice to meet you," Leo answered.

Lloyd pointed at Taiga. "We taught this one most of what he knows about construction. By the way, important question. Birthday?"

Leo blinked. "What?"

"Birthday."

"M-May twenty-fourth?"

Lloyd slammed the table as if that explained everything. "Gemini! I knew it."

Darius slid the drawings clear of the bowls and sat down beside Lloyd. Leo leaned toward Taiga to ask what he'd done that was Gemini. Taiga just shook his head as to say *don't think about it*.

Yuri lasted about three minutes before she began the interrogation she'd promised was innocent.

"So, Leo! How's life at Olympus? Taiga's explanations are usually about six words long."

Leo laughed. "Well, it's... busy for sure. Between classes, friends and soccer we usually have our hands full."

"Oh! How's soccer with Taiga? You know, that's something he practically never talks about."

Leo glanced at Taiga, who narrowed his eyes in response.
He already knew where Yuri was going.

"Uh... I'd say he looks like he's constantly pissed at the ball."

Yoichi barked a laugh, while everyone else silently chuckled. Taiga kicked Leo under the table.

"But in a good way!" Leo immediately added while laughing as well. "Like, he's pretty stubborn. At first, he angrily chased everything that moved in front of him. Now, he angrily chases selected things, but doesn't let go."

"Pffff. Progress," Yoichi said.

"Fuck off. You're making me sound like a dog running at things.  That's Yoichi's job."

Yoichi flipped him off in response.

"Hihi~ Yeah, we know our Taiga can be pretty... resolute sometimes," Yuri said.

Leo chuckled. "Yeah, then he can't take a compliment. Like, once we were in the showers and I told him-"

Yuri's eyes widened. "Ohhhh, so you guys shower together? All of the team?"

Leo blinked. "W-Well..."

"Yuri..." Goro said glancing at her.

"What? It's a perfectly legit team bonding activity. We could learn a thing or two from their coach!"

Taiga sighed. "Locker rooms have shower stalls, you know. Unlike our old cabins' bathrooms. And our team has, like, twenty or something players."

That apparently was the wrong thing to say. He caught a dark shine in her eyes.

"Twenty??!? All in the same locker room?!?"

Goro put down his spoon. "Yuri."

"Yes, Dad?"

"Allow our guest to eat without interrogating him."

"I am getting to know him!"

"You are embarrassing Taiga."

"That's part of getting to know him~"

Yoichi snorted into his bowl. "Dynamite's gonna fucking explode before dessert."

Taiga buried his face in his hands. "I hate all of you."

By the time the bowls were empty, the sun had begun to descend behind the ridge.

"So, what are the plans for the week?" Taiga asked.

"We'll start on Monday. Most of the work will be clearing paths, inspecting cabins and fixing any damage, sorting equipment, and cleaning up the sports ground," Yoshinori said.

"Meanwhile, we'll assess the all camp facilities and report to Mr. Clermont by the end of the week," Lloyd added.

Taiga nodded.

"That means," Yuri continued, "you guys have the weekend all for yourselves! You definitely should show Leo around."

Leo's face brightened up. "Oh, yeah! That'd be amazing!"

"Taiga, dear, why don't you two go to Buddy Falls tomorrow? It's beautiful in spring." 

Taiga considered. "Yeah, that could be a good idea."

"Yesss! It's also incredibly romantic~"

"YURI."

Yoichi snorted. "Dumbass walked straight into that one."

Taiga exhaled and picked up his bowl and Leo's, but Aiden met him halfway to the kitchen and took them.

"Nope. You two traveled all afternoon. Go."

"That's not a rule," Yoichi said. He left his bowl by the sink and took a step toward the door.

"It is tonight," Aiden said grabbing him back by the hoodie.

"You make shit up constantly, Buttcheeks."

"That's one of the privileges of being a scoutmaster." Aiden tossed him a towel. "Come on, you dry."

"UGH."
