### Chapter 22

"Hey, Yoshi."

"Taiga? Hello! It's good to hear from you. How are you doing?"

"Everything's fine. Is this a bad time? I can call you later if you're busy."

"Not at all. What can I do for you?"

Taiga leaned back in his chair. The golden light of sunset shone through the window.

"Well, campus is going on spring break next week and... I was wondering if you guys needed any help with preseason."

"Ah, I see. Well, you're always welcome here, Taiga, regardless of whether we need help or not. In any case, any help is obviously always appreciated."

"Right. Thanks, then."

"When are you coming?"

"Uh... is next Sunday alright?"

"Of course. Anyway, I don't expect this preseason to be too laborious. The new scoutmasters will arrive for the summer term, but everything's in good shape here."

"Winter didn't break anything?"

"Luckily, not much. I've noticed a few leaks in some of the cabins, and we obviously need to take inventory in all the storage sheds. We still haven't assessed everything, but I don't expect major problems like last year."

"Heh, Yoichi's gonna be happy."

"I expect so, even though I doubt that more work would make much of a difference to him."

"Tch. Lazy dog."

"Anyway, how are things on your end?"

"Not bad. The new classes are a bit more interesting than last semester's. Soccer is going well. Coach has been letting me play in official matches sometimes."

"I know. Keitaro sent me some footage of your matches. I'm really impressed, Taiga. I understand nothing about soccer, but seeing you go from zero to playing official matches... I cannot help but feel really proud of you."

Taiga smiled, despite himself. "Yeah, thanks."

"And Hyunjin tells me you're getting pretty interested in computer science. Am I right?"

"It's... alright. I mean, it's nothing like the stuff Jin showed me last summer. But I don't hate it."

"Ahah, I can imagine. University has a way of making things more... interesting than they look initially."

"Yeah, you could say that."

"Aiden tells me Hiro has the same complaint. Apparently, he sometimes says he liked cooking here better. But I'm assuming that's part of the process of actually learning things."

"Pfft. Hiro complained even in the camp kitchen."

"Well, who knows, maybe next time he'll complain less. By the way, I'm assuming you'll be coming on your own since I haven't heard from the rest."

"Yeah, our spring breaks are scheduled differently. Keitaro told me he was considering coming, but his break is in three weeks or something."

"I see. Oh, speaking of which, how are your classmates and teammates? I haven't heard much about them from you."

"Some of them are annoying. Most of them. Others borderline insufferable. A few are..." Taiga paused and gulped. "...Alright."

Yoshinori laughed. "Looks like you've made lots of friends there."

He glanced at Caleb's empty chair. "...Yeah, a few."

"I'm so glad to hear that. Well, if you want to bring someone with you, just tell me."

"W-what?"

"Here, I mean. At camp. If you want to bring a friend for the break, we've got plenty of space. Tell me before Sunday, so we can prepare accommodations."

"...Right. No, I think I'll come on my own."

"As you wish. For now, I'll plan for just you until you tell me otherwise."

"Alright."

"So, see you on Sunday, then!"

"Yeah, see you."

***

Somebody had brought a gaming console to Apollo. Taiga found Gav and Enzo glued to the TV when he arrived on Wednesday evening, with Leo, Evan and Scott cheering from behind the couch.

"Hi, guys."

Evan and Scott waved from a distance. Leo turned, smiling. "Hey!"

Gav and Enzo didn't even flinch. He hung up his coat and approached them.

"Hey," Taiga said more softly, gently patting Leo's lower back as he stood beside him. "Who's winning?"

"It's a tie for now. This is the last match. They've bet a pizza on it."

"My bet's on Enzo," Evan said.

"I-I think Gav's got this," Scott replied.

Taiga watched them play. Gav looked like he knew what he was doing. Enzo, though, was unpredictable enough to make all his tactics useless.

Leo bumped his shoulder. "Wanna bet a pizza as well?"

"You wish. Isn't that *your* console? Plus, I have never played this game."

"All I hear are excuses."

"You wouldn't be so cocky if this was a fair challenge."

Leo shrugged. "You can always chicken out."

"Fuck off. It's not gonna work this time."

"Alright, alright. And here I thought you were always up for a challenge."

Taiga looked at the bastard. "The fuck does that mean?"

"Maybe... you only take the easy ones?"

A vein started pulsing at his temple. He narrowed his eyes, which only made Leo's grin grow wider.

"Five matches," Taiga said. "First two so I can learn the game. Then, best of three."

Leo put on a smug face and held out his hand. "Deal."

Taiga shook it, staring right at him.

"So, what's the goal?"

Leo pointed at the screen. "You either kick your opponent out of the platform or beat him enough to knock him out. Three lives each."

"Looks simple enough."

Taiga quickly learned that it was easier said than done. Leo let him basically do whatever he wanted for the first two games. Near the end of the second one, Taiga asked for a 'real world demonstration of an actual fight', but Leo had evidently restrained himself.

He showed no mercy when the first actual game started. The rest of the guys tried cheering for him, but Taiga got knocked out two times before making Leo's damage reach 50%. He managed to recover a bit toward the end, but he still lost the game while Leo still had two stocks.

Unsurprisingly, he ended up owing Leo a pizza. Second game was better, but definitely not enough.

When the game ended, Taiga dropped his joycon on the couch and looked at Leo in resignation. 

"Fine. But this doesn't end here."

"I'm up for a rematch whenever you want."

"Later, now I'm hungry."

"Alrighty. A pizza would be nice right now, wouldn't it?"

Taiga squinted his eyes. "Just you wait for it."

***

The pizza guy arrived half an hour later. Taiga and Enzo sighed as they payed double the price, while Gav and Leo fist-bumped behind them with a grin.

"I swear," Gav said, "free pizza tastes different."

Leo answered with a mouthful. "Totally agreed."

Enzo glared at them but, incredibly, he said nothing. Instead, he turned to Evan.

"So, have you figured out if you're in? Break starts in four days and we still haven't book a thing!"

Evan scratched his head. "I don't think, dude. Finals are just a bit more than a month away and I got too much work to do. I can't slack off for the entire break, graduation is not free."

Enzo made a disappointed face. "That leaves the three of us basically."

"At that point, honestly I'd save those money to do something in summer. Maybe there'll be more of us," Gav said.

Leo nodded. "Yeah, I agree."

"So you're just staying here?" Taiga asked.

Enzo shrugged. "Apparently. Maybe we can spend a day at Santomarino, but I dunno how worth that is in spring."

"It's not bad," Evan said, "But yeah, it's way better in summer. Worth a day out, that's for sure."

"And you, Taiga?" Gav asked.

"I'll leave Sunday for camp."

"It's official then," Leo said.

"Yeah. I called them yesterday."

Enzo leaned back on his chair. "Ugh, I almost envy you. At least you can get out of there."

"It's gonna be mostly work, you know. We need to fix what broke in winter."

"I take that back. I don't envy you anymore."

Taiga smiled. "Figured."

"Oh, I dunno," Leo said, "I'd still consider it better than campus, honestly."

Taiga looked at him but Gav replied before he could answer.

"Well, at least' there's still the Symposion. You guys coming, right?"

"When is that again?" Evan asked.

"This Friday."

"Shit, already? Of course, though. Symposions are sick, you'll see."

"Will the really supply us the uniforms?" Leo asked.

"Yep. Everyone can choose theirs, obviously, but athletes are required to wear the color of their House. I believe Mercer will deliver them after Friday's training."

Taiga frowned. "Uniforms?"

"Yes, it's a themed party. Everyone will be wearing a toga."

"Of course."

Evan smiled. "It's one of the oldest Olympus tradition, from what I've heard. Even professors attend, although they have their own section."

Taiga imagined Mercer in a toga dancing with Rao. He felt a mixture of horror and amuse.
