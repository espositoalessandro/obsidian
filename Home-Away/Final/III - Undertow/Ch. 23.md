### Chapter 23

At 9:28, Taiga was nervously waiting in the common room. No one else was there, which felt the whole place feel wrong.

He had already typed *you awake?* in Leo's chat, when the elevator doors opened and a very excited Leo appeared.

Taiga deleted the text and put his phone away. "Morning."

"Good morning!" he said with a smile.

"You're weirdly early."

"Yeah, well, I woke up at seven and couldn't get back to sleep." Leo wrestled the strap back onto his shoulder. "Shall we?"

The sky outside was grey and an annoying wind made the walk to the station not a pleasant stroll.

"Come on," Taiga said. "Platform seven."

They got their duffels up onto the rack and pushed the smaller bags under the seats in front. Leo sat by the window. The train had barely cleared the platform when he pulled out his phone to take a picture.

"Of what?" Taiga asked. They were passing a wall with a drainpipe every ten feet.

"My mom asked me to let her know when we'd left."

"You could tell her in words."

"Ugh. Pics are quicker."

Leo locked his phone and put it away. "I don't even know where we're heading."

"North."

"How long?"

"A couple of hours by train, another by bus."

"Right. By the way, how many scoutmaster are there now?"

Taiga counted on his fingers. "Five, plus camp president. Most of them don't stay for the offseason, they prefer to work part-time."

"Right. And... where am I going to sleep?"

"They'll set up a couple of beds in a cabin. When we visit, we usually sleep there."

"We're sharing a cabin?"

Taiga opened his mouth, then closed again when he realized. 

"Yeah," he said after a second.

Leo looked down. "Right. Cool. How about Yoichi?"

"Since Yoichi's living there, they built him a small lodge after the last term."

"No way! He's got his private house now?"

"Yeah. They actually wanted to build me one as well. That's when I started thinking about college. If felt too... I dunno..."

"Definitive?"

"Kinda, yeah."

Leo nodded. "Yep, makes sense. Do you regret it so far?"

Taiga shook his head. "Not yet."

"Wow, optimistic."

"Well, I have to share a campus with you."

Leo shoved his shoulder. "You should be thanking me, moron."

...Yeah, probably. 
Although, Taiga didn't say it.

***

The bus left them beside a gas station with two pumps and a bait shop with plywood over the windows. Taiga put his bags down by the bench. The last time he'd been there, the snow had reached the seat. Now it was dry everywhere, and new leaves were starting popping out of the trees.

Leo looked back at Taiga. "This is it?"

"No."

"Good, because I was about to have questions."

"Someone's should be coming to pick us up."

Five minutes later, a tan pickup with the camp buddy logo on the front doors came around the bend, rattling a little over the broken asphalt. 

Yoshinori pulled up beside the bench and welcome him with his usual proud, insufferable smile.

"Hello guys!"

"Hey, Yoshi."

He got out of the pickup and gently hugged Taiga. "Glad to have you back, Taiga."

"Yeah, same."

Then he turned to Leo. "Hello, you must be Julio, right?"

Leo's head swung toward Taiga. "Why did you do that?"

"What? It's your name."

"Only my mom calls me that!" Leo sighed and straightened up to shake Yoshinori's hand. "Yes, sir. Just Leo."

"I'm Scoutmaster Yoshinori. You do not need to call me sir, though."

"Give him twenty minutes," Taiga said.

Leo ignored him. "Nice to meet you, Yoshinori."

"And you, Leo. Thank you for coming to help. Here, give me."

Yoshinori took both their duffels and put them in the back of the pickup.

The drive took less then five minutes. When the car turned and took the gravel path ending with the big Camp Buddy arch, Leo leaned over the window. Taiga saw him smiling.
