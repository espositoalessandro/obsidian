Taiga Akatora meets Olympus University!

Behind the white-stone walls of Olympus, something new awaits for him. Something he definitely wasn't prepared for.

Somewhere between practices, late nights, bad decisions, old wounds, and the ordinary business of figuring out who the fuck he's supposed to become, Taiga will discover that Camp Buddy isn't the only place he can belong!

He'll relive old memories, face past mistakes, explore new places, and get the hell out of his comfort zone. New and old friends will help Taiga through all of it.
 
Especially a certain blond, annoying guy from the Apollo Soccer Team who will prove increasingly difficult to ignore!

### Author's note

Hey! Thanks for being here!

<em>Home/Away</em> is a crossover between <em>Camp Buddy</em> and <em>Jock Studio</em> that takes place after the events of <em>Camp Buddy: Scoutmaster Season</em> and follows Taiga's decision to go to college.

I wrote this story a while ago mainly for self-indulgence, but I ended up getting pretty invested and turned it into a proper novel ^^"

It still needs a chapter-by-chapter rework/rewrite, but most of it is already laid out.

The current draft is 47 chapters long and sits at around 268k words. I don't expect the final version to match it exactly, but it's gonna land somewhere nearby.

For now, I'm planning to post two chapters per week, on Tuesdays and Fridays. Occasionally, I might drop a third one on Sunday if the pacing allows it. Some chapters need a little more time to breathe than others ;)

Familiarity with the original games is recommended, but I won't assume it. So, you don't need to have played all of them to enjoy the story!

All <em>Jock Studio</em> references and settings are based on BLits' development diaries up to October 2026, and the novel doesn't follow any of the events of the demos.<br />
So, bear in mind that some details may become outdated once the full game is released.

<strong>NOTE: Leo's backstory is entirely made up by me, so it may be dramatically different from the official one.</strong>

And lastly, yes, the novel is explicit. Obviously. But sex isn't the main focus of the story, and since this is a slow-burn romance, you'll have to be patient if that's what you're here for!

Have fun and don't hesitate to roast me in the comments :*
