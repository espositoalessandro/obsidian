*Time behaves funnily. People always say that it stretches when you don't want it to and it shrinks when you need more of it.*
*Why?* 
*Who decided that is a fucking sadist.*

That was what Taiga thought while he was unpacking his clothes in his bedroom.

Two weeks.
Two weeks and he and Leo will be together again.
Two weeks before Camp Buddy.

"Two weeks is stupid."

"Yeah, you already said me this."

"It doesn't change that it is."

Leo was lying on his bed, behind the small screen of Taiga's phone. 

"Yeah. It is."

"You still have to show me your room."

"You'll see it when you'll come here."

"COME ON! Why can't I have a peek?"

"Because you want to."

"How big is your bed?"

"Not big enough for the two of us."

Leo grinned.

"Has this ever stopped us before?"

Taiga smiled and sat on the edge of it.

"I guess not."

"Are you sure that it won't be a problem for your family?"

"Dude, you're staying here for one night. They'll survive."

Leo stayed silent for a second.

"What if they don't like me?"

"They'll still let you sleep here for one night."

"TAIGA."

Taiga laughed.

"This is a very serious question and it demands a very serious answer."

"Why wouldn't they like you?"

"I don't know? You tell me. Brief me on what should I tell them."

Taiga rested his head on his fingers and sighed.

"Don't tell them you hate cheese."

"Okay- WAIT."

"Yep, you'll have to endure one day of cheese."

"I'll throw up."

"Don't do it in front of them."

"Fuuuuuuck, why had it be the cheese."

"You asked it."

*But I guess the trick to survive when time stretches, is to fill it up anyways with things you like. Afterall, longing for something is already something worth looking for.* 

---
Leo froze. His face showed confusion. Then disdain. Then amusement. 

"You didn't really do it."

Taiga was laughing at his face. 

"Oh my god, you should see your face."

"Hilarious. Very."

"Come on, I've always dreamed to do it."

Leo finally surrendered to a smile. He rushed towards Taiga and jumped on him, hugging him tight. His trolley laying flat on the ground.
After Leo let go of him, Taiga took it. They left the "Arrivals" gate behind.

"Give it to me, I want to keep it."

Still laughing, Taiga handed him the sheet he was holding.
Leo looked at him.

"You could've at very least written 'Mr.' rather than 'señor'."

"It would have lost half of its meaning."

"Fair."

They reached the car. Leo took exactly one hour to conquer his mother's heart. He expected even less, but good enough.

---
Finally Leo, opened the door. He stood in the doorway, eyes closed.

"Are you coming in or not?"

"Shut up. This is a solemn moment."

Taiga shook his head and pushed Leo's trolley in his bedroom.

"Okay, I'm ready."

He took a dramatic deep breath and opened his eyes.

"Oh. My. God."

He stepped in. 
Taiga mocked him: "A small step for a man..."

"Shush. I'm in my boyfriend's sacred temple. Have some respect."

"That was borderline cringe."

"It sounded right in my head."

"That makes it even worse."

Leo looked around. Then smiled.

"I like it. It's nowhere near how I imagined it."

Taiga frowned.

"And how exactly had you pictured it?"

"I won't tell you."

"Say it."

Leo looked at him and raised an eyebrow.

"Make me."

Taiga looked at him. 

"No. You stink of airport."

"You're the worst."

*And when that something finally arrives, then all you have to do is finding something else to look for. If you long for nothing, what are you living for?*

---

Taiga and Leo went off on the familiar bus stop. 

"Guess this time no one is gonna fetch us, right?"

"Privileges are lost during the term."

"Sigh."

The walk to the entrance took almost twenty minutes. Before arriving, they could already hear the noise coming from the camp.

"It really does get lively in summer."

"You have no idea. I almost miss the camp when it was on the brink of failure. Much quieter."

Leo shook his head.

"The others are already waiting outside the arch?"

"Yeah."

After a couple of minutes, the familiar arch finally appeared.

"TAIGA!!!"

Taiga looked down. There he was.
Keitaro and Hiro were running towards him. Taiga couldn't help but smile.

"Took you long enough!"

Keitaro hugged him tight.

"Bus was late."

"Yeah, yeah."

Taiga pulled out. Hiro went straight to Leo.

"DUDE! I'm so happy to see you here! We're gonna have so much fun!"

Leo grinned and hugged him.

"Yeah, me too."

Yoichi, Natsumi and Hunter were waiting right under the arch.

"It was about time, Dynamite. Thought you and Sunball had bailed us."

"Don't get your hopes high, dogboy."

"Tch."

Leo smiled and waved.

"It's good to see you, Yoichi."

"Wait until tonight before saying it."

"Taiga, Leo!"

Hunter and Natsumi were approaching. After they both hugged them, Natsumi said:

"Scoutmaster Yoshinori told me we can all stay in cabin three!"

"It's gonna be pretty crowded this year," said Taiga.

Hiro pointed Yoichi.

"We can always let Wolfboy sleep with Yuki."

"I'd consider that an improvement."

Hunter added:

"Cabins have four bunks, they can fit eight people."

"Yeah but Wolfboy snores too loudly."

"You snore as well Torch-head."

Keitaro laughed,

"Yeah, sometimes you do."

"HEY!"

___
"It's good to see you, scouts."

Yoshinori welcomed them with the warmest smile.

"Alright, we'll talk more later, now get settled. Gathering is in an hour. You'll find your uniforms in the cabin."

The group went in. Leo immediately grabbed his new shirt. 

"I'm about to have a mental breakdown. I can't believe this is happening."

Taiga went beside him. 

"Come on, put it on."

Leo immediately changed in his new uniform. 
Dark green shorts, light grey shirt. A yellow scarf and a golden patch representing a golden retriever.

"What do you think?"

Taiga looked at him. Words didn't come out of his mouth. He just smiled. 
Keitaro said:

"Oh, you look amazing in it! It fits you so well!"

Hunter added:

"Yeah, yellow is really a beautiful color!"

Yoichi glanced at the animal patch.

"Tch. At least someone as good taste in animals."

Natsumi said:

"Welcome officially to Camp Buddy!"

Hiro zoomed around him.

"Oh my freaking God. It's perfect!"

Leo was simply overwhelmed by all this.

"Thanks guys."

He glanced at Taiga, who simply smiled back.

He took his hand.

"Let's go."

They crossed the cabin's door, and went to the gathering.

A new adventure was about to start.
