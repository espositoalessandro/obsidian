Crowds, heat, luggages being dragged. People talking out loud everywhere. 
These were the first things that hit Taiga when he got out of the bus. 
Great start.
He looked up at the entrance arch of Olympus University: statues, a fountain, gardens, white walls. Everything was fucking huge and pointlessly fancy. 
He adjusted the duffel on his shoulder and went on the side of the bus to recover his suitcase.

Things didn't improve when he started walking towards the entrance. Mothers almost crying, cringing students trying to shove them away, some fancy-asses that had personal valets carrying their stuff. 
At the arch, a girl behind a small stall with pamphlets gave him a bright smile and waved a hand towards him.

"Hi! Welcome to Olympus University!"

The warmth in her voice sounded so fake that Taiga almost turned around. Instead he nodded and gave her a quick "Thanks". Then, he turned before she could trap him.

A student rushed towards the bus and bumped Taiga.

"What the fuck, man?"

The guy was about to fall face-first onto the floor. He recovered and immediately turned towards Taiga.

"Oh shit, sorry dude, you alright?"

Taiga could already feel the familiar heat climbing to his head. 
No.
He took a small breath.

"Wathever."

Then he turned and continued. 
There. Day one and he almost lost it to a random guy who just bumped him.
Great start.
Except, he didn't. That should count, right?

He had swore to leave that part of him at Camp Buddy, buried at that fucking pier. 
He just didn't expect Olympus University to bomb all his senses straight away.

He moved on.
