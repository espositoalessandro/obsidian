Training had been irritating for twenty minutes before Taiga actually became angry.

Mercer had split them into four-on-four groups inside a narrow rectangle of pitch. Two small goals at either end. No goalkeepers. Two touches maximum.

“Move it before they can settle,” Mercer said. “And when you lose it, don't all become fucking heroes at once.”

Taiga understood that part.

Unfortunately, understanding instructions before the whistle and understanding them while eight people moved at the same time were apparently different skills.

Romero received the ball with his back to goal.

Taiga saw it and went.

Too easy.

He closed the space fast, reached for the ball—

Romero shifted it sideways.

Taiga's foot found grass.

By the time he turned, Romero was already past him.

“Akatora.”

Taiga stopped.

Mercer pointed between him and the goal.

“You don't need the ball.”

Taiga frowned.

“He had his back turned.”

“Exactly. Where was he going?”

Taiga looked toward Romero.

“Inside.”

“And you gave him inside.”

Right.

Mercer moved two steps onto the pitch and positioned himself where Taiga had been.

“Get close. Don't commit. If you stay on your feet, he has to make a decision.”

“So just hold him there.”

“Until you have a reason not to.”

Fine.

That was clear enough.

They reset.

This time Cole received.

Taiga closed down, then stopped himself before getting too close.

Cole turned.

Taiga stayed in front of him.

Good.

Cole took another step.

Taiga backed away.

Another.

Still nothing.

Cole kept coming until he was almost at the small goal, then slipped the ball sideways. Price tapped it in.

Mercer's whistle went.

Taiga stared at the ball.

“What?”

“You're escorting him.”

Taiga looked at Mercer.

“You said don't commit.”

“I said don't dive in. I didn't say give him a guided tour.”

Someone laughed.

Taiga didn't look to see who.

Mercer pointed toward Cole.

“You're still defending. Make him uncomfortable.”

Taiga pressed his lips together.

Fine.

Don't go too early.

Don't back off too much.

Apparently there was a correct distance measured in fucking centimeters that everyone else had been issued during childhood.

They reset.

Again.

This time Cole received near the line.

Taiga closed.

Not too fast.

Cole turned toward him.

Taiga stayed square.

Cole shifted the ball right.

Taiga followed but didn't reach.

Left.

Taiga held.

For one second neither of them moved.

Then Cole passed backward.

Mercer shouted from outside the grid.

“Good, Akatora.”

There.

Taiga jogged back into position.

That made sense.

He hadn't won the ball. Didn't matter. Cole had gone backward.

Fine.

He could do that.

The next few minutes were better.

Not good. Better.

Taiga forced one pass backward. Intercepted another because Bennett had closed the lane beside him. Lost the ball once, recovered and managed not to immediately sprint at whoever had taken it.

Then Price received on the turn.

Taiga saw the touch get away from him.

There.

He stepped in.

Price got there half a second first, knocked the ball beyond him and ran.

“Fuck.”

Taiga turned and chased.

Price passed.

Goal.

Mercer's whistle.

“Reset.”

Taiga walked back.

Evan Cole passed beside him.

“Don't chase the bad touch.”

Taiga looked over.

“It was loose.”

“Yeah. Doesn't mean it's yours.”

Taiga didn't answer.

They reset.

Ball moved.

Bennett to Romero. Romero across. Price dropped short.

Taiga checked the man in front of him, then the ball, then behind him.

Too much.

By the time he looked forward again, his player had moved.

Pass through.

Another goal.

Taiga kicked the grass once.

“Akatora,” Mercer called.

“I know.”

Mercer didn't answer.

That was somehow worse.

Again.

Taiga received this time.

One touch.

Cole came toward him.

Taiga played backward.

Safe.

Then moved forward for the return.

It didn't come.

Fine.

They lost possession on the other side.

Taiga turned.

His man was already running.

He sprinted after him.

Caught up.

Got beside him.

Shoulder to shoulder.

The attacker slowed.

Taiga saw the ball exposed.

He reached.

The attacker pulled it away.

Taiga's momentum carried him past.

“Fuck!”

Goal.

Mercer blew the whistle harder this time.

“Akatora. Stop trying to fix the last mistake with the next one.”

Taiga looked over.

“What?”

“You lose him once, then the next time you try to win everything immediately. Stop punishing the game.”

Taiga said nothing.

His face felt hot.

Mercer pointed back onto the pitch.

“Play the situation you're in. Not the one from thirty seconds ago.”

Easy for him to say.

Taiga reset.

His breathing was too fast for the amount of running they'd done.

He knew that.

He tried to slow it.

Next rep.

Simple.

Close.

Stay up.

Watch the ball.

Watch the player.

Check the passing lane.

Don't back off.

Don't dive in.

The attacker received.

Taiga moved.

The attacker turned.

Taiga hesitated.

That was enough.

One touch around him.

Gone.

Evan intercepted the pass before it reached the goal.

“Hold him there,” Evan said as he returned the ball. “You don't have to win it.”

“I know.”

It came out sharper than Taiga intended.

Evan glanced at him.

“Okay.”

They reset.

Good.

Done.

Taiga flexed his fingers.

Next ball.

Same side.

Same attacker.

Taiga closed.

Stayed in front.

The attacker shifted left.

Taiga followed.

Right.

Still there.

Then Bennett shouted something behind him.

Taiga looked.

Half a second.

The attacker went past.

Evan covered again.

“Don't turn your head when you're that close,” he said.

Taiga clenched his jaw.

“I know.”

Evan paused.

Then nodded.

“Alright.”

Again.

Taiga could feel Mercer watching now.

Maybe he wasn't.

Didn't matter.

It felt like it.

Ball came across.

Taiga's man checked toward it.

Taiga followed.

Too close.

The player rolled him and escaped behind.

Evan stepped across and killed the move.

Whistle.

Taiga stopped moving.

Evan passed the ball back toward the restart.

“You're still diving in.”

Something snapped.

“I fucking know.”

The words came out louder than he meant.

The group went quiet.

Evan looked at him.

Not angry.

Which made it worse.

“Then stop trying to win it,” Evan said. “Make him choose.”

Taiga could hear his pulse.

“I said I know.”

“Okay, but you're still—”

“Can you worry about your own fucking game?”

Silence.

Not complete. Another group was still playing twenty yards away. Somebody shouted. A ball hit a post.

But their square had stopped.

Evan stared at him.

Taiga knew immediately.

Not later.

Not after someone explained it.

Immediately.

Evan hadn't done anything.

Mercer's whistle cut through the pitch.

“Akatora.”

Taiga looked over.

Mercer pointed outside the grid.

“Out.”

Taiga's stomach dropped.

“Coach—”

“Out.”

Taiga walked.

He could feel everyone not looking at him.

That was worse than if they had.

He reached the sideline and stopped.

Mercer didn't come over.

He restarted the drill.

“Play.”

And just like that everyone moved again.

Taiga stood there with his hands on his hips, breathing like he'd run twice as far as everyone else.

On the field, Evan delayed the same player near the touchline.

Didn't tackle.

Didn't reach.

Just stayed there.

The attacker looked inside.

Evan took that away.

Looked backward.

Passed.

Taiga watched the whole thing.

Fuck.
