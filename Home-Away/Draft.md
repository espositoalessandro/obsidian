### Chapter 18 - Fault Line

At breakfast on Saturday, Taiga's phone buzzed.

Leo had sent him a photograph from the other side of the table.

**Leo:** enzo is eating syrup with pancakes attached

Taiga checked. Enzo was adding more.

**Taiga:** efficient

**Leo:** disgusting

Taiga had to lower his cup before laughing. Leo caught his eye above the phone and grinned.

"What?" Enzo asked.

Leo shook his head and took a mouthful of coffee.

By nine, they were on the pitch again.

Hale mixed the teams again but kept everyone in yesterday's unfamiliar positions. There was no repeat of the running. They worked through short games, stopping to change players or try a move again.

Taiga went back to right-back. Skylar, in the same bib as him, stood beside Jordan in the middle of their defense. Aaron was ahead of Taiga. Across from them, Milo pulled his bib straight while Gav and Jonah argued about where to stand.

Scott rolled the first ball to Jordan.

Jordan took it forward, saw Dennis coming toward him and tried to turn past him. Dennis stuck a foot in. The ball ran loose, and Scott reached it before either of them.

"Price. You've got a pass either side."

Jordan glanced at Taiga, then across at Enzo.

"Right."

The next time, he gave it to Taiga immediately.

Taiga wanted to send Aaron running, but Milo was already standing where the pass needed to go. Leo came closer and called. Taiga gave it to him, watched Aaron move and got the ball back from a different angle.

This time the pass went through.

Aaron reached it and crossed. Theo got a foot to it, but the shot went wide.

He stopped beside the goal, looking at the mark his boot had left.

"Thought that was going in," Aaron called.

"So did I."

Taiga jogged back. At least he wasn't the only one discovering new ways to miss something obvious.

On the next attack against them, Gav received in front of Leo. He turned, took another touch and looked toward Dennis.

Leo followed him.

Dennis went the other way.

Scott shouted, and Jordan got between Dennis and the ball. It struck his shin and went out.

Hale called Leo over while someone retrieved it.

"You don't have to follow Foster all the way out there. Look what opens behind you."

Leo looked back toward Jordan.

"Oh. Yeah."

Gav came over to ask about the run Dennis had made. By the time Hale sent them back, they were both pointing at different parts of the pitch.

Taiga pulled his sleeves over his wrists and waited for the next ball.

The passing was cleaner than yesterday. Skylar called Taiga's name and sent him the ball. When Jonah came toward him, Taiga gave it back. Skylar passed to Leo and moved again, making himself available while Leo turned.

Taiga still had to think about where to stand, but sometimes he got there before Scott needed to shout. Once, he saw Jonah start running behind him and moved across early enough to take the pass himself.

Then he tried to send it straight across to Enzo.

Too soft.

Gav reached it first and shot.

Scott tipped it around the post.

Taiga stopped with his hands on his hips.

"Sorry."

Scott pointed toward the other side.

"Make sure it gets there."

From beside the goal, Gav called for another ball. Apparently one attempt had only encouraged him.

They changed a few players and started again.

Aaron got past Milo with a quick change of direction. Gav came across to meet him, and Aaron tried to push the ball between his feet.

Gav closed his legs, took the ball and passed it toward Jonah.

"Help me out!" Aaron called.

Taiga had been moving up behind him.

"I was there."

"Then say something!"

"I did."

"GET BACK!" Scott shouted.

Taiga turned. Jonah was already coming down his side.

They recovered the ball near the corner. When Taiga looked across, Enzo was standing much farther back than usual, one hand out, demanding the pass. Taiga sent it to him.

Enzo waited for someone to approach, knocked the ball past him and ran onto it. A few seconds later, he sent it up the field to Aaron.

Aaron brought it down cleanly and found Theo.

This time Theo scored.

Enzo raised both arms from the other end.

"You're welcome!"

They stopped for water after the next game. Aaron stood beside Taiga, watching Milo and Dennis collect dry bibs.

"Do they do this every day?"

"Some version," Gav said, coming past for his bottle.

Aaron took a drink.

"Must be nice."

They resumed with the ball on Milo's side. Hale had Aaron try closing him down from different places. On one attempt, Milo passed around him. On another, Aaron stopped the pass but gave Milo enough room to run past instead.

"Watch his touch," Hale said. "When it gets away from him, go."

Aaron nodded.

Milo rolled the ball back to the starting point.

"If you come a little more this way, you've got both—"

"I know."

Milo paused.

"Okay."

"I heard him."

Taiga glanced over. Milo had already gone back to his place.

The next game started with Skylar losing the ball as he tried to turn. Jonah took it and went straight toward Scott, who blocked his shot. Skylar reached the loose ball first and sent it out toward Enzo.

On another attack, Gav kept asking for it through the middle until Dennis finally gave him a pass he could reach.

He shot without stopping it first.

Over.

Gav put both hands on his head. Leo was laughing as he went to retrieve the ball.

After another rotation, Taiga sat on the bench with Aaron and Jordan. Jordan pulled his coat on and asked Scott about the run Dennis had made earlier. Scott stood in front of them, moving his hands to show where he'd wanted him.

Aaron kept turning the cap on his bottle.

Taiga leaned toward him.

"You good?"

"Are you asking or grading me?"

Taiga sat back.

"What?"

"I'm fine."

"Okay. I was asking."

"Well, you asked."

Jordan was still listening to Scott. Taiga put his bottle away and left it there.

In the last game, Aaron got past Milo twice. The first pass reached Theo; the second was blocked. When Hale brought them in, Aaron went over to ask him about the difference.

Taiga only caught the start of the answer. Enzo was beside him, trying to work out how he'd ended up with someone else's gloves.

They finished before lunch. Mercer called them together while Hale's assistants collected the balls.

"Tomorrow, usual positions. Briefing at eight. Until then, eat, rest and keep yourselves available."

Aaron looked up.

"So we're done for today?"

"Yes."

On the walk to the changing rooms, Gav was still talking about the shot he'd missed. Jordan wanted another go up front tomorrow. Enzo wanted to know whether there was time to shower before lunch.

Aaron opened the door for them, then caught up with Evan.

"They look like they could do that in their sleep."

"They've done it more."

"Yeah, I got that."

Taiga went inside behind Leo, who was trying to explain to Scott where Gav should have aimed.
***

Scott caught Leo's cue before he could take another shot.

"Your hand's moving."

Leo looked down. "Which one?"

"The one on the table. Here."

They'd come to the common lodge after dinner. Enzo and Evan occupied the nearest couch, Aaron was choosing a cue, and Taiga had already missed a ball sitting close enough to the pocket that he could have blown it in.

Scott put his own cue down and spread his hand on the cloth.

"Keep this flat. Then lift your thumb a little."

Leo copied him. Scott showed him where the cue should rest, then stepped aside.

"Try moving it without hitting anything."

"Oh. Okay, that's much better."

Taiga watched the cue slide back and forth. He'd been gripping his own so tightly that his fingers were starting to hurt.

Scott came around to him next.

"Can I see?"

Taiga bent over the table.

"Relax this hand a bit."

"If I relax it any more, I'll drop it."

Scott reached for a spare cue and showed him. Taiga loosened his grip.

"Like that. Just keep it straight."

He hit the white. It struck the blue ball, which rolled toward the side pocket and dropped.

Oh.

Leo straightened immediately. "Wait, I want another try."

"You've been trying."

"Yeah, and now I know what I'm doing."

Scott retrieved the blue and put it back. Leo leaned over, took his time and missed.

Enzo covered his mouth, badly hiding a smile.

"Shut up. I'm learning."

"I can see that."

Taiga took the cue Leo held out to him. He tried the same shot again and missed too.

This time Enzo actually laughed.

They practiced until Scott suggested starting a game. He gathered the balls into the triangle while Leo chalked his cue with considerably more attention than he'd given it before.

"Solids or stripes," Scott explained. "Once you've got yours in, you go for the black. Leave that alone until then."

"And if the white goes in?" Taiga asked.

"That's a foul. Your opponent gets to put it where they want."

Leo looked at the table. "Anywhere?"

"Anywhere on the table."

"That's evil. Okay."

Taiga broke. Nothing went in.

Leo walked around the table twice before choosing a shot. He bent down, stood up again and checked from the other side.

"Are you playing?" Taiga asked.

"I'm deciding. There's a difference."

He finally potted a striped ball and looked so pleased that Taiga almost laughed.

"Stripes, then," Scott said.

Leo nodded solemnly, then missed the next one.

Taiga's first solid went in. The second hit the edge of the pocket and stopped there.

"Fuck."

"That's good for later," Scott said.

Taiga glanced at him. "It was supposed to be good now."

Scott's mouth twitched.

Aaron and Evan took over the neighboring table when it became free. Enzo carried his drink across to watch them, leaving Gav in his place on the couch.

Their own game took considerably longer than Taiga had expected. He could see the shot he wanted, but the white kept ending up somewhere useless afterward. Scott came over once and pointed out where it would probably stop.

Taiga followed the line with his eyes.

"So I need to hit it less hard."

"For that one, yeah."

He tried. The ball went in, and the white stayed close enough to his next solid that he didn't have to stretch across the table.

Leo leaned against his cue.

"Can I borrow you when it's my turn?"

Scott looked between them. "I can help both of you."

"No, you're helping him now," Taiga said.

Leo laughed. "Listen to you! Two balls in and suddenly we have transfer negotiations."

At the other table, Aaron potted his second ball and gave Enzo a little bow.

Then he missed one near the corner.

Scott glanced across.

"You're lifting up before you—"

"Can I take one fucking shot without somebody teaching me?"

Scott stopped.

Aaron stayed bent over the table for a moment, looking at the ball he'd missed. Then he straightened.

Evan picked up his cue. "My turn."

Taiga looked across. Enzo had put his drink down and was watching Aaron, who moved aside without answering.

Beside Taiga, Leo lowered his voice.

"Is that one mine?"

He pointed at the striped ball near the cushion.

Taiga turned back. "Yeah."

"Right. Almost forgot what I was doing."

Scott stayed to watch. When Leo asked how to reach it without knocking another ball, he came around and showed him where to stand.

By the time they reached the black, Taiga's lower back hurt from bending over. Leo still had one stripe left. Taiga lined up the black, changed his mind, tried from the other side and missed.

Leo sucked in a breath.

"Okay. Okay, I can do this."

He potted the stripe.

Gav sat forward on the couch.

Leo walked around to the black, bent down and paused.

"That corner," he said, pointing.

He hit it.

The black dropped. The white kept rolling toward another pocket.

Leo's mouth fell open.

It caught the edge, rattled and stayed out.

For a second, neither of them moved.

Then Leo raised both arms.

"YES! Holy shit, did you see that?"

Taiga stared at the white.

"That was luck."

"Some of it! The important bit was skill."

Scott was laughing behind his hand.

Leo came around the table and held his palm up. Taiga slapped it harder than necessary.

"Rematch."

"Absolutely."

They were still arguing about the last shot when Milo and Skylar arrived to find out whether anyone wanted coffee.

Scott took the next game against Skylar while Taiga and Leo watched from the couch, knees touching whenever one of them shifted. Gav left the couch halfway through and came over to watch the finish.

Scott won with a shot off the cushion that Taiga wouldn't even have considered trying.

Skylar put his cue away and opened the back door a crack.

"Anyone coming out? They've got the fire going."

Taiga reached for his coat.

Cold air reached Taiga's face as soon as they opened the back door.

The terrace was quieter than the lodge. A few chairs and a low bench stood around the firepit, with snow banked beyond the cleared paving. Evan and Enzo followed them out. Scott came a minute later, carrying his coat over one arm.

Taiga sat beside Gav on the bench. Leo pulled a chair closer on his other side. Once the heat reached him, Taiga took his coat off and draped it over the back.

Milo held his hands toward the flames. Skylar was still complaining about the game.

"I left you nothing. There was no shot."

Scott sat down opposite him.

"There was off the side."

"Yeah, I noticed."

Taiga looked at Scott. "How did you know where to hit it?"

Scott leaned forward a little. He pointed toward the lodge, where the table was just visible through the windows.

"Same as a straight shot. You work backward from the pocket. It's just harder to see."

Gav nodded. "I was trying to work it out when you were lining up. I thought you were going for the other corner."

"So did I," Leo said.

Aaron was near the far corner of the terrace, phone against his ear. Taiga caught a few words when the wind shifted, then Aaron said goodnight and put it away.

He came over and took the empty chair beside Skylar.

"Who won?"

"Scott," Skylar said.

Aaron smiled a little. "Should've warned you."

"Could've used that before I played him."

Someone passed Aaron a cup. Enzo began recounting his own game against Evan, who disagreed with almost every detail. Taiga listened, smiling into his drink while Enzo insisted that a ball had moved by itself.

Later, Gav came back to Scott's last shot.

"Wait, I think I get it."

He set his cup down and used the space between it and Taiga's boot to show them where the balls had been.

Leo leaned across Taiga to look, resting a hand on his shoulder.

"Wasn't the white farther over?"

"A little," Scott said. "Nearer the side."

Gav moved his finger.

"Right. So if you imagine the pocket on the other side of the cushion—"

Aaron exhaled.

"Are we still doing this?"

Gav looked up. "What?"

"The fucking pool lesson."

"I was asking Scott."

"Great. We can all get coached on something else."

Skylar turned toward him. "You don't have to listen."

"Bit difficult sitting right here."

Gav sat back. "I was just trying to understand the shot."

"It went in. I think we all got that."

Taiga set his cup down.

"What's your problem?"

Aaron glanced at him.

"Nothing."

"Then why've you been acting like a dick all day?"

Leo's hand left his shoulder.

Aaron sat back.

"Because I don't want another fucking lesson?"

"Because you keep snapping at people for talking to you."

"I'm talking now."

"Yeah. Like that."

Gav moved beside Taiga.

"It's fine."

"No, apparently it's not," Aaron said. "Apparently he's been keeping track."

Taiga felt his jaw tighten.

"You had a go at Milo this morning. Scott was trying to help you with one shot. Now Gav can't even ask him a question. Nobody's doing anything to you."

"Did they ask you to speak for them?"

"No. I was there."

"Then you heard me say I didn't want help."

"That's not the fucking problem."

"What's the problem, then? Come on. Explain that to me too."

Evan put his cup on the paving.

"Guys."

Taiga barely glanced at him.

"I asked if you were alright earlier. You bit my head off."

"You came over to tell me what I'd done wrong."

"I didn't."

"You've been doing it since yesterday. You were running. I was there. I'm there, I'm fucking there. I get it, Taiga."

"Then play the pass."

"There it is."

Heat rose into Taiga's face.

"You said I wasn't helping. I was right behind you."

"And you gave it away plenty of times yourself."

"Yeah. I did."

"But when I do it, you have to tell me."

"Because you blame someone else every fucking time!"

Aaron's mouth tightened.

"You're unbelievable."

"Am I?"

"Yes. You sit there correcting everybody, and the second somebody tells you to shut up, suddenly they're the problem."

"Taiga," Gav said quietly.

Taiga leaned forward.

"We all found it hard. We all had to do the same thing. You're the only one still taking it out on everyone."

"Right. Sorry I didn't work it out as fast as you."

"That's not what I said."

"It's what you've spent five minutes telling me."

"You want to do it the way you already know. Hale asks you for one different thing and you spend the rest of the session arguing about it."

"Because I'm trying to understand what he wants!"

"He wants you to look at what's in front of you!"

"I did!"

"Then fucking use it! He can't come and hold your hand every time!"

Aaron stared at him.

Taiga heard his own words a second too late.

"You're such a fucking arrogant prick."

"At least I can think for myself."

Gav's hand touched his forearm. Taiga pulled away and stood.

Aaron was still looking up at him.

"Yeah. Everybody's stupid except you."

"I didn't say—"

"You don't have to. We can all hear it."

"Okay, Aaron," Leo said. "Enough."

Aaron turned toward him.

"Why me?"

Leo paused.

"Because this is getting—"

"He's standing over me telling me I can't think for myself, and you're telling me to stop?"

"I'm telling you both to stop."

"You said my name."

"Then I'm saying his too. Taiga, sit down."

Taiga stayed where he was.

Aaron gave a short, ugly laugh.

"Bit late."

"Leave him out of it," Taiga said.

"Of course."

"What?"

"Every fucking time. I say something to you and suddenly there's two of you."

Leo straightened.

"I'm sitting right here. I'm not going to pretend I can't hear you."

"Then hear him as well."

"I did."

"And?"

"He shouldn't have said it."

Aaron spread his hands.

"There. Wasn't that hard."

"Don't talk to him like he's fucking stupid," Taiga snapped.

Scott stood.

"Both of you. Stop."

Aaron pushed himself out of his chair.

"You know what? Fuck it. You and your fucking boyfriend."

The word seemed louder than the rest.

Taiga couldn't hear whatever Scott said next.

Leo had gone still beside his chair. Gav's hand was no longer on Taiga's arm. Across the fire, Milo was staring at Aaron.

All of them.

Aaron's mouth opened.

"Shit. Taiga, I—"

Taiga took a step around the end of the bench.

For a second he wanted to go straight across the space between them.

Then he grabbed his coat.

"Wait. I didn't mean—"

"Don't."

He couldn't look at Leo. He couldn't look at anyone.

"I'm fucking done with you."

Taiga left before Aaron could answer.

***

The sleeve of his coat caught at his wrist. He pulled until it hurt, shoved his arm through and kept walking.

He could still see Leo beside the chair. Everybody looking, waiting for something to happen. For someone to laugh, or explain, or say that of course it wasn't like that.

He knew that wait.

His stomach had gone tight and cold.

Not again.

Taiga turned down the service path behind the lodge, away from the lights and the windows. His boots slipped on the packed snow, and he caught himself against the wall.

Footsteps followed.

"Taiga."

Of course.

He pushed away from the wall.

"Go back."

"Wait a second."

Leo caught up near the turn toward the training building. His coat was open, his scarf hanging loose around his neck. He stopped a few feet away.

"Jesus. You walk fast."

"What?"

Leo's breathing slowed. He glanced back toward the lodge, then at Taiga.

"Are you okay?"

"Fine."

"Okay. You didn't look fine."

"Then why ask?"

Leo pressed his lips together.

The cold was getting through Taiga's clothes now. He wanted to keep walking. He wanted Leo to stop standing there, looking at him like something had happened.

"You looked like you were going to hit him," Leo said.

"That's why I left."

"Yeah. Good."

"So go back."

"Can we just take a minute?"

"There's nothing to talk about."

"There obviously is."

Leo took a step closer. His hand came up toward Taiga's arm.

Taiga moved back.

Leo stopped.

For a moment, the only sound was someone shutting a door farther up the slope.

"I wasn't going to drag you anywhere."

"I know."

"Then can you stop acting like I'm trying to?"

Taiga looked toward the lodge. Through the trees, one window glowed above the path.

They'd seen Leo leave too. Of course they had.

"Why did you have to follow me?"

"Because you left upset."

"Exactly."

Leo's brow tightened.

"What?"

"He says that shit in front of everyone and you come running after me."

Leo lowered his hand.

"I didn't come because of what he said."

"Doesn't matter."

"It matters to me."

Taiga's jaw ached. He forced it loose.

"Go inside."

"Taiga, I'm not worried about what Aaron thinks."

"Well, good for you."

Leo went quiet.

Taiga could feel how badly that had landed. He rubbed his palms against the sides of his coat, looking for something else to say.

Nothing came.

"I was worried about you," Leo said.

"I didn't ask you to be."

"No. You didn't."

The change in his voice made Taiga look up.

Leo was pulling his coat closed. His hands moved slowly, fastening the zip where he'd left it halfway down.

"Fine. I'll leave you alone."

He turned.

Taiga's chest tightened.

"Why do you always do that?"

Leo stopped and faced him again.

"Do what?"

"Follow me around."

Leo's mouth opened a little.

"Because you're my friend."

Taiga looked away.

"You leave like that, I want to know you're alright. What was I supposed to do?"

"Nothing."

"Okay."

"Just leave me alone."

Leo nodded.

He stood there another second, then stepped back.

"Fine."

Taiga watched the space open between them. It had been cold before. Now he couldn't seem to get a full breath.

"Leo."

Leo stopped.

He was still willing to stop.

Taiga had to say something. Tell him to wait. Ask him to stay here until he could look at him without seeing the terrace, all those faces, the moment before somebody said something that made it worse.

Leo waited.

Taiga rubbed a hand over his mouth.

"Whatever. Fuck it."

"No."

He looked up.

Leo had turned back fully.

"You don't get to do that."

Taiga's hand fell.

"What?"

"You tell me to leave, then call me back, and when I stop, you do that."

"I didn't—"

"You did. Just now."

Leo's voice had gone flat. He wasn't shouting. Taiga almost wished he would.

"What do you want me to do?" Leo asked.

Stay.

The answer sat there, useless.

"I don't know."

"Well, I can't keep guessing."

Taiga swallowed.

"It's not—"

"What?"

He couldn't finish.

Leo waited until the silence became unbearable, then wiped one hand across his mouth.

"So now somebody's said something about us, you don't want me near you."

Taiga shook his head.

"That's not..."

"Then what is it?"

He could hear the fire from here when the wind turned. A distant crack, somebody moving a chair. People were still up there.

"I really thought you were different," Leo said.

Taiga's stomach dropped.

"Leo."

"No. I came out here because I wanted to check on you. That's all."

"I know."

"Then why are you talking to me like this?"

Taiga couldn't answer.

Leo blinked hard and looked past him.

"If being close to you means you get to shove me away whenever somebody notices, then..."

His voice caught. He pressed his lips together.

Taiga took half a step toward him.

Leo held up a hand.

"Then I don't want to do it. Not anymore."

A tear slipped down his cheek. He wiped it away almost immediately.

"I'm done following people who don't want me."

He turned toward the lodge.

Taiga stayed where he was.

Call him.

He'd already called him. Leo had stopped, and he'd wasted it.

The footsteps grew quieter. At the bend, Leo went out of sight.

Taiga watched the empty path until the cold made his hands hurt.

Room 214 was empty when Taiga got back.

He shut the door, put his key on the desk and sat on the bed. His coat was still on. He could feel the cold through the knees of his trousers.

For a while he stared at Scott's towel hanging over the bathroom rail.

His phone buzzed.

**Mercer:** breakfast 7. briefing 8. kickoff 11. get some sleep.

Replies appeared beneath it.

**Scott:** 👍

Then:

**Leo:** 👍

Taiga opened their private chat.

The last messages were from breakfast.

**Leo:** enzo is eating syrup with pancakes attached

**Taiga:** efficient

**Leo:** disgusting

He put his thumbs over the keyboard.

*sorry*

He read it, added a few words and deleted them all.

Then he typed again.

*you didn't do anything*

A key turned in the lock.

Taiga switched the screen off.

Scott came in, closed the door and stayed beside it for a moment. His hair was falling into his eyes. He pushed it away and took a breath.

"I talked to Aaron."

"Good."

"I need to talk to you too."

Taiga kept the phone between his hands.

"What?"

Scott put his coat over his chair.

"What he said was out of line."

"Yeah."

"So was telling him he can't think for himself."

Taiga looked up.

"You heard him."

"I heard both of you."

The answer came without hesitation. Scott sat on his own bed, facing him.

"You were on your feet, shouting at him."

"I didn't hit him."

"I know. You left. That was the right thing to do."

Taiga's jaw tightened.

"But I asked you to stop before that," Scott continued. "Gav did too. Evan. You kept going."

"He kept—"

"I know."

Taiga stopped.

Scott rubbed his palms against his knees. His shoulders were stiff, but he didn't look away.

"You can tell him he's being a dick. You can be angry. You can't turn it into that."

For a few seconds Taiga could hear only the radiator.

"Did you tell him the same thing?"

"Yes."

"What did he say?"

"He knows he fucked up."

Taiga looked down at the phone.

Scott waited, then stood to get his wash bag.

"We've got a match tomorrow. I need you both to listen when somebody's talking to you. Even when you don't like what they're saying."

"Yeah."

"Taiga."

"I heard you."

Scott stayed where he was.

Taiga exhaled through his nose.

"Okay."

"Okay."

He went into the bathroom. Water ran briefly, then stopped.

Taiga turned the phone over.

When Scott came out, he was still sitting there.

"Leo's back?" Taiga asked.

Scott paused.

"Yeah. He's with Enzo."

Taiga nodded.

Scott put his wash bag away and reached for his pajamas.

"Do you want to talk about it?"

"No."

"Alright."

He didn't ask again.

Taiga took his coat off while Scott changed. Then the ceiling light went out, leaving the desk lamp on beside his bed.

He unlocked the phone.

The message was still in the box.

*you didn't do anything*

He knew that. Leo had come after him because he was worried, and Taiga had made him wish he hadn't.

He scrolled up to Friday's photograph.

Leo stood against the railing, hair blowing across his face. Taiga had caught him laughing before he'd had time to pose.

He could still hear it. The whole walk back, the stupid picture of Enzo, Leo arguing about the film afterward.

This morning he'd been sitting opposite him, trying not to laugh into his coffee.

Taiga scrolled down again.

The words waited beneath the conversation.

He put his thumb over the arrow.

Then the screen dimmed.
