Taiga pulled his training top over his head and put it in his bag. Across the changing room, Leo was sitting beside Enzo, winding tape around his ankle.

He looked away before Leo could look up.

His shirt hung above the bench.

AKATORA. Forty-one.

He put it on, then reached for the bib that would cover it.

Outside, the sun had reached the pitch, but the benches were still in shadow. Snow lay in dirty banks beyond the fence. A few people had gathered along the opposite touchline, hands buried in their pockets.

They warmed up with the ball first.

Gav passed to Taiga and moved around the cone. Taiga gave it to Leo.

"Back," Leo said.

He returned it immediately. Taiga passed to Gav.

Again.

On the next turn, Taiga put too much into the pass. Leo stretched a foot out, stopped it and brought it back without saying anything.

"Easy, Akatora," Mercer called.

"Yeah."

Taiga adjusted his sleeves and waited for the ball to come around.

Scott was already shouting from the other end of the pitch. Carter had left somebody unmarked in the practice box, and apparently everyone in the valley needed to know.

Taiga tried to listen to that instead.

***

Kickoff was at eleven.

For the first few minutes, Pinetree let Olympus have the ball near their own goal. Dennis stood well back, pointing people into position. Skylar moved between Carter and Walsh without chasing either of them.

Then Aaron received by the left touchline.

Milo came toward him from the inside, cutting off the pass to Gav. Aaron looked up. Evan had already started forward, but Pinetree's right-back was close enough to follow him.

"Back!" Carter called.

Aaron took another touch.

Milo got closer. One of the midfielders came across behind him.

Aaron finally played backward, hard enough that Carter had to turn and run after it.

Mercer stepped to the edge of his area.

"Earlier, Bennett!"

Aaron lifted a hand.

A couple of minutes later, the same thing happened on the other side. Enzo received, Jonah came forward to help, and suddenly both had a Pinetree player in front of them. Enzo tried to squeeze the ball between two pairs of legs.

Throw-in.

Taiga rubbed his hands together beneath his coat.

From here, he could see where they wanted Olympus to go. Every time the ball reached the side, green shirts moved toward it. The space appeared somewhere else, too far away for the player with the ball to use without help.

Gav kept turning and pointing. Leo came short, then stopped when Isaac received instead. Nobody seemed sure who was supposed to move first.

"Turn!" Taiga muttered when Leo got the next pass.

He had room.

Leo checked over his shoulder, took the ball across his body and looked for Jordan. By the time he played it, Dennis had stepped in front.

Taiga sat back.

On seventeen minutes, Milo moved away from the touchline toward Gav. Aaron followed him a few steps, then checked when Pinetree's right-back ran outside.

Evan was coming back, pointing toward the runner.

Aaron turned. Milo went the other way.

For a second, both of them moved toward him.

The pass went outside.

"BACK POST!" Scott shouted.

The fullback reached the ball before Evan and crossed low. Skylar got across Carter. Scott went down, but the ball was already past him.

The people on the opposite side of the pitch cheered.

Taiga stared at the goal while Skylar ran toward the corner, Milo catching him by the shoulders.

One-nil.

Aaron stood with his hands on his hips. Evan said something to him, pointing back toward where the move had started. Aaron nodded without looking up.

Scott collected the ball and threw it toward halfway.

"Next one! Come on!"

***

Olympus did get forward.

Evan beat his man to a pass from Leo and crossed first time. Jordan reached it, but the goalkeeper got his leg in the way. Taiga stood with the rest of the bench, then sat again when Dennis cleared the rebound.

Enzo had a shot blocked. Gav won a ball near the center circle and found Leo before Pinetree could get back.

This time Leo turned immediately.

Jordan started through the middle. Enzo was already running on the right.

Leo took another touch, and a midfielder caught him from behind.

The ball rolled loose.

"Oh, come on," Taiga said under his breath.

Leo chased back and helped force the next pass out. When he straightened, he pushed his hair off his forehead and looked toward Mercer.

Mercer pointed up the pitch.

"Play it when it's there!"

Taiga looked down at his boots.

The half kept going. Every time Olympus got out of one problem, they seemed to find another. Aaron made a clean tackle on Milo, then waited so long to pass that Evan had to come back for it. Leo called for the ball and received it after the opening ahead of him had closed.

Scott never stopped talking.

By the time Pinetree won a corner, Taiga's jaw hurt from keeping it clenched.

Dennis came up from defense. Walsh stayed close to him while Scott arranged everyone else.

The corner came toward the near side. Dennis got his head to it before Walsh, sending it down into the crowd. Someone tried to clear. The ball came back out toward the edge of the box.

A Pinetree midfielder hit it through the bodies.

Scott moved late. He must not have seen it until it was almost on him.

Two-nil.

Taiga checked the clock.

Thirty-nine minutes.

Mercer turned away from the pitch, pressed his lips together and went back to his seat.

That was somehow worse than shouting.

***

At halftime, Taiga followed the others into the changing room.

Boots scraped against the floor. Someone dropped a bottle, picked it up and sat down. Leo peeled the tape away from one wrist. Aaron stayed bent over, elbows on his knees.

Mercer shut the door.

"How many times did we talk about the space behind their fullbacks?"

Nobody answered.

He looked at Evan.

"It's there."

"Yes. It is. So why do we keep waiting until they've covered it?"

Evan took a drink. Leo was looking at the floor.

Mercer pointed toward the door.

"They want you standing still by the line with the ball. We knew that before kickoff. Yet we keep giving them time to put us there."

Gav nodded. "We need the next pass sooner."

"And someone asking for it. Bennett, when Cole comes short, you can go past him. You don't need to wait until he's got the ball to start."

Aaron straightened.

"Okay."

"Mendez. You had Reyes running twice. Play him."

"Yeah."

"Ellis, keep going when Reyes comes inside. Foster will cover."

Gav nodded again.

Mercer looked around the room.

"You're all waiting for somebody else. Call. Move. If the pass is wrong, we'll deal with it. We can't do anything with a pass you never make."

Taiga turned the cap on his bottle.

Beside the door, Scott pulled his gloves back on. He pressed the fastening down hard, then looked at the others.

"Tell me when you're handing someone over. I can't organize you if you're all watching different people."

Walsh nodded. Carter said, "Yeah."

Mercer checked the time.

"Same eleven. Get back out."

Taiga waited for Jordan to pass before following.

***

The first ten minutes of the second half were better.

Aaron went outside Evan before he received. Evan gave him the ball, moved inside and got it back. The cross went too far, but at least Pinetree had to turn toward their own goal.

Enzo started showing closer to Leo. Once, he took a pass, sent it straight back and ran beyond the fullback. Leo found him.

Jordan's header went over.

Taiga clapped with the others. His hands were cold enough that it hurt.

Then Pinetree kept the ball for a while, moving it across the back until Olympus came forward. Dennis passed into midfield and followed his own ball. A midfielder dropped into the space he'd left.

Skylar came short. Carter went with him.

"Walsh, inside!" Scott shouted.

Milo was already running through.

Scott reached the pass first and kicked it out toward Jonah.

Taiga let out the breath he'd been holding.

Evan had tracked all the way back. When Olympus won a throw-in, he stayed bent over for a second before jogging forward again.

Mercer watched him.

"Akatora. Warm up."

Taiga looked around.

Mercer was still facing the pitch.

"Go."

He put his bottle down and stood.

Lucas came with him. They ran along the touchline, turned and came back. Taiga's first few steps felt stiff. By the third run, his coat was off and he could feel his pulse in his throat.

On the pitch, Aaron slid in late on Milo.

Whistle.

Taiga stopped at the end of his run. Aaron was already standing, one hand raised. The referee showed him a yellow card.

He walked away without arguing.

"Akatora!"

Mercer beckoned him over.

Taiga ran back.

"Cole's coming off. Their right-back keeps going inside with Mendez. I want you staying wide. When he steps in, run behind him."

Taiga nodded.

"Make him turn. We've spent an hour playing in front of them."

"Okay."

"And help Bennett when we lose it."

His stomach tightened.

"Yeah."

Mercer looked directly at him.

"Can you do that?"

Taiga swallowed.

"Yes, Coach."

"Then get your bib off."

***

The clock showed sixty-one when Taiga stood beside the halfway line.

He pulled at the bottom of his shirt. The referee was watching the ball at the far end, waiting for a stoppage.

He had imagined this before. Sitting through the Helios match, warming up against St. Vincent and going back to the bench, watching Evan run until the whistle.

Now Evan was coming toward him.

Taiga's hands felt useless.

Evan slowed by the line.

"He's quick if you let him get close. Start before he turns."

Taiga nodded. Their palms met briefly as Evan stepped off.

Then the referee waved him on.

The pitch felt much wider from here.

He ran into position on the left. Aaron was behind him. Leo stood nearer the middle, looking toward Gav, and Taiga stopped himself from looking any longer.

"TAIGA! CHECK YOUR SHOULDER!"

Scott.

He turned. Pinetree's right-back was coming up behind him.

Right. Fuck.

***

His first pass came from Aaron.

Taiga checked where the defender was, met the ball and sent it back.

Aaron moved it inside to Gav.

Taiga ran forward.

The next time, he received with enough room to turn. He pushed the ball ahead and went after it. The fullback turned with him, but Taiga got there first and tried to send it across Jordan's run.

Dennis blocked it.

Corner.

Taiga jogged toward the box while Gav went to take it. His lungs were already working harder than he wanted.

Aaron came past him.

"Got time on the next one."

Taiga nodded once.

The corner was cleared.

For a few minutes, running helped. There was somebody to follow, a ball to reach, something he could actually do. He tracked the fullback, came back for a pass and gave Gav an option along the line.

Then Leo received facing him.

The fullback had moved inside, close enough to put pressure on Leo if he turned. Beyond him, there was a strip of empty grass.

Taiga started running.

"Leo!"

The name came out too quietly.

He raised his voice.

"Here!"

Leo looked up, but Taiga had already checked his run. The fullback turned and went with him.

Leo took the ball inside instead. A midfielder stepped across. Leo tried to get around him, lost it and chased.

Taiga ran back as well.

Pinetree's next pass went out near halfway.

Leo stopped a few meters away, breathing hard.

"I thought you were coming short."

Taiga looked at the space he'd just run through.

"I was going."

"You stopped."

He had.

Taiga wiped his mouth with his sleeve.

"Yeah."

Leo turned toward the throw-in.

"Just call earlier."

Taiga nodded, though Leo was no longer looking at him.

***

Olympus scored with a little over fifteen minutes left.

Aaron won the ball near the left touchline and gave it to Gav before Milo could close him down. Taiga moved forward, keeping the fullback with him.

Gav found Leo in the middle.

This time, Leo released the ball before the midfielder reached him. It went across to Enzo, who had held his position on the right.

Enzo knocked it past the fullback and sprinted.

Jordan went toward the near post. Taiga ran for the far one.

The cross came low and hard. Jordan reached it before Dennis and turned it past the goalkeeper.

Taiga was still running when the net moved.

"Come on!" Gav shouted behind him.

Jordan went straight into the goal to get the ball. Enzo turned back toward halfway, fist clenched, while Mercer waved everyone toward their own half.

Taiga slowed.

Leo was coming back through the middle. For a second, Taiga almost went toward him.

Then Leo called to Enzo about the pass, and Taiga turned toward his position.

Two-one.

There was still time.

***

Pinetree stopped sending both fullbacks forward.

Taiga could feel the difference immediately. Every time he got the ball, the right-back was already there. If he went past him, Dennis moved across behind.

Once, Taiga tried anyway.

He got around the first player, took a heavy touch and watched Dennis put the ball out before he could reach it.

"Use me!" Leo called from inside.

Taiga looked over. He'd been free.

"Yeah. Sorry."

He went to take the throw.

After that, he tried to keep his head up. He gave the ball to Leo and ran beyond the defender. The return pass came, but Taiga had gone too early.

The flag was up.

He swore and jogged back.

Aaron found him again a few minutes later. Taiga came short, brought Leo into the move and kept running. Leo switched the ball to Enzo.

That cross was cleared.

They did it again.

Nothing about the passes made it any easier to stand near Leo while waiting for a throw-in, or to hear Aaron calling his name behind him. But the ball kept coming, and Taiga kept having to answer.

His calves started burning. He checked the clock and immediately wished he hadn't.

Eighty-eight.

Scott came out of his area to clear a long pass before Skylar could reach it. Gav collected the ball and sent everyone forward again.

The last chance came from a free kick on the right. Enzo put it into the box, Carter got his head to it, and the goalkeeper caught it beneath the bar.

Taiga stood outside the area with both hands on his hips.

The keeper took his time.

When he finally kicked it, the referee checked his watch.

Three whistles.

***

Taiga bent over, hands on his knees.

Across the pitch, Pinetree's players were going toward one another. Someone clapped Skylar on the back. Dennis shook Mercer's hand and went looking for Scott.

Taiga straightened before they reached him.

The right-back offered his hand. Taiga shook it, then waited while the others came through.

"Good game."

"Yeah. You too."

He said it again to the next player.

Leo was a few places ahead, speaking to Milo. Aaron passed behind Taiga on his way toward the bench.

Taiga went to get his coat.

Evan moved his bag off the seat. Taiga sat down and pulled the coat around himself. His fingers fumbled at the zip.

Twenty-nine minutes, plus whatever had been added on.

His first conference match.

Mercer waited until everyone was close enough to hear him.

"Shower. Get something to eat. We'll go through it Tuesday."

Jordan started pulling the tape off his socks. Leo picked up his bag and headed toward the changing room with Enzo.

Taiga got the zip started at last. He pulled it up to his chin and followed the others inside.
