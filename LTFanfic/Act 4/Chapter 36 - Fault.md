“They trusted me.”

The words came out low. Leo didn't answer immediately. Taiga's chest folded around them.

“Mercer put me out there. Aaron trusted me to hold that side. Gav fucking told me where eight was. Everybody did their job and I—”

His voice broke completely. Taiga stopped breathing for a second. Then it all came apart. Not graceful. Not quiet. His shoulders jerked once. He covered his face. Tried to swallow the sound and failed.

Leo stood. Taiga heard the medical sandal scrape the floor.

“Careful,” Taiga managed.

Of course that was what came out. Leo reached him anyway. One hand touched Taiga's arm. Taiga could have moved. He didn't. Leo pulled him in. That was enough.

Taiga folded against him. Forehead first. Then his face pressed into Leo's shoulder. His hands caught the back of Leo's shirt. Leo's arms closed around him. The next breath broke into something ugly.

Taiga gripped harder. His whole body shook. Leo held him. No speech. One hand at the back of Taiga's neck. The other between his shoulder blades. Taiga cried until his face hurt.

Until breathing became work. Until he stopped trying to make it silent. At some point Leo shifted his weight and hissed. Taiga pulled back immediately.

“Your ankle.”

“I'm fine.”

“You're standing on it.”

“Mostly the other one.”

Taiga looked down. Leo was, in fact, balancing badly.

“Sit down.”

Leo laughed softly.

“Okay.”

They moved back to the bed. Taiga sat first. Leo beside him. For a few seconds Taiga stared at his knees. His face felt swollen. His nose was blocked.

Excellent. Leo handed him a tissue from the desk. Taiga looked at it.

“You keep those there?”

“My roommate has allergies.”

Taiga took it.

“Useful.”

“I'll tell him you said thanks.”

Taiga blew his nose. Leo smiled. Taiga gave him a look. The smile disappeared. Mostly.

“My reputation is dead.”

“No.”

“You saw all of that.”

“Yes.”

“Exactly.”

Leo leaned back against the wall.

“You've seen me throw up after sprints.”

“Not the same.”

“I cried when my high school club lost regionals.”

Taiga looked over.

“You did?”

“Like a lot.”

That surprised a laugh out of him. Leo pointed.

“See? Mutual destruction.”

Taiga wiped his face again.

“Were you seventeen?”

“Yes.”

“Doesn't count.”

“Why?”

“Teenager.”

“You say that like twenty makes you ancient.”

“Fuck off.”

Leo smiled. Taiga's breathing finally settled. They sat quietly. The room felt smaller now. Not uncomfortable. Taiga looked at Leo's ankle again.

“You sure nothing's torn?”

“That's what they said. Mild sprain. They want to see how the swelling looks tomorrow.”

“Next week?”

Leo shrugged.

“Too early.”

Taiga's stomach tightened. Leo noticed.

“Hey.”

Taiga looked at him.

“That's next week.”

Taiga nodded. Barely. Leo's hand rested on the bed between them. Taiga looked at it. Then put his own over it. Leo turned his palm. Their fingers locked.

No comment. Taiga leaned sideways until his shoulder met Leo's. Leo rested his head against Taiga's. The room stayed quiet. After a while Taiga said:

“You didn't answer.”

“About what?”

“Trust.”

Leo lifted his head slightly. Taiga stared ahead.

“I said they trusted me.”

Leo was quiet for a second.

“I don't know what Mercer is going to do.”

Taiga swallowed.

“Right.”

“I don't.”

Leo squeezed his hand.

“But I know what I saw before the mistake.”

“You played the whole match. You were good for most of it. Then I went off, you lost your head, and you made a bad decision.”

Taiga's mouth tightened.

“Two.”

“Turn and chase.”

“Okay. Two bad decisions in the same thirty seconds.”

Taiga looked down at their hands. Leo added:

“That doesn't make the first seventy-nine minutes disappear.”

Taiga didn't answer. Leo didn't ask him to. After a while, Taiga's head dropped onto Leo's shoulder. Leo's cheek rested against his hair. His thumb moved slowly over Taiga's knuckles.

Taiga closed his eyes. The exhaustion arrived all at once. Match. Bus. Room. Crying. Everything. He shifted, trying to get more comfortable without thinking about it. Leo moved the pillows.

“Lie down before you fall over.”

Taiga looked at the bed. Then at him.

“Your ankle.”

“I can lie down with an ankle.”

“Debatable.”

Leo rolled his eyes and stretched out carefully, injured foot still elevated. Taiga lay beside him. Not fully. Half on his side. Head against Leo's chest. Leo's arm came around his back.

Taiga stayed there. The shirt under his cheek was soft. His own face was probably still disgusting. He did not care enough to move. Leo's fingers traced slowly between his shoulder blades.

Under the hem of Taiga's shirt. Warm skin. Taiga exhaled. After a minute, Leo said:

“You got tears on me.”

Taiga's eyes opened.

“Seriously?”

“It wasn't a complaint.”

Taiga lifted his head enough to look at him. Leo's mouth twitched. Taiga stared. Then lowered his head again.

Taiga felt it. He kissed the same place again. Slower. Leo exhaled. Taiga settled back against his chest. The hand on his back started moving again. No question.
