The table exploded. Taiga dropped his forehead into one hand. Leo laughed so hard he nearly choked on water. Aaron hit Taiga between the shoulder blades. Scott, somehow, smiled.

Actually smiled. Taiga lifted his head.

“I'm going to kill you.”

Enzo sat down.

“Champions can't be arrested.”

“That is not true.”

“Worth checking.”

The conversation moved on. Eventually. Taiga ate until he physically couldn't anymore. Then dessert arrived. Enzo found room. Of course he did. Leo stole half of Taiga's because Taiga had made the mistake of saying he was full.

At nine-twelve, Taiga's phone buzzed. Caleb. He opened it under the table.

**Caleb:** Congratulations for today.

Taiga smiled. Typed:

**Taiga:** thanks

A second message appeared before he could lock the screen.

**Caleb:** I'm going to Maya's university for tonight.

Taiga stared. No explanation. No follow-up. Nothing connecting the two statements. He read them together. Then separately. Leo leaned slightly closer.

“What?”

Taiga turned the phone face-down.

“Caleb.”

“What happened?”

“Nothing.”

Leo waited. Taiga looked at him.

“He's going to Maya's university tonight.”

Leo blinked. Then looked at the phone. Then back at Taiga.

“Oh.”

Taiga's face warmed. Leo's did too. Neither said anything for several seconds. Across the table, Enzo was arguing that tiramisu counted as hydration. Useful. Taiga picked up his water.

Leo leaned closer.

“Very unrelated message.”

Taiga nearly inhaled the water.

“Shut up.”

Leo smiled into his dessert.

---

Dinner ended at ten. Mercer left first. The assistants followed. The team remained another twenty minutes until the restaurant staff began making increasingly obvious movements with chairs. They finally spilled back into Central.

Night air. Full stomachs. Dead legs. Too much energy anyway. Enzo wanted to go somewhere else. Gav refused. Scott had already started walking toward Residential. Aaron said he was calling Sofia.

One by one, the group broke apart. Taiga and Leo ended up behind everyone else. Leo's limp had become slightly more obvious. Taiga noticed.

“Hurting?”

“Five.”

“You said four on the bus.”

“I also walked around a restaurant for two hours.”

“Idiot.”

“Champion idiot.”

Taiga rolled his eyes. They crossed Central Park. The fountain lights reflected on wet stone. Students still passed them. A few recognized them. One shouted congratulations. Taiga lifted a hand.

Leo did too. They reached the Residential Zone. Taiga slowed at the path split. Leo's building one way. Room 317 another. Leo looked toward his own residence. Then at Taiga.

Taiga shoved his hands into his pockets.

“Come over.”

Leo's eyebrows lifted. Taiga kept walking before the expression could get worse. Leo caught up.

“Okay.”

---

Room 317 was dark. Taiga opened the door. Empty. Caleb's side looked exactly as Caleb always left it before spending a night away. Bed made. Desk clear. Chair pushed in.

One charging cable coiled with unnecessary precision. Taiga turned on his desk lamp. Leo came in behind him. The door closed. For a second they stood there. No team.

No Enzo. No twenty people screaming. No championship banner. Just the room. Taiga dropped his keys on the desk. Leo put his bag on the floor beside Taiga's bed.

“You scored today.”

Taiga looked at him.

“You noticed?”

“I was there.”

“Barely.”

Leo looked offended.

“I contributed.”

“You passed me the ball until I got annoyed.”

“It worked.”

Taiga smiled. Leo smiled back. The silence after that lasted just long enough. Taiga stepped closer. Leo met him halfway. This one was quieter than the locker room.

No surprise. No teammates pressing in from every side. Taiga touched the side of Leo's face and kissed him slowly. When they separated, Leo stayed close. His forehead rested against Taiga's for a moment.

“You're still smiling,” Taiga said.

“I had a very good day.”

“You've said that.”

“It keeps being true.”

Taiga laughed under his breath. Leo sat on the edge of the bed. Taiga looked at the wrapped ankle.

“Put that up.”

“Romantic.”

“Do it.”

“Yes, coach.”

Taiga shoved the pillows toward the wall. Leo stretched out and elevated the ankle. Taiga sat beside him. For a while they talked. Not about anything useful. The goal.

Scott's save. Mercer's smile. Enzo apparently becoming an uncle through mechanisms neither of them understood. The conversation kept breaking whenever one of them remembered something from the locker room and started laughing again.

Eventually Leo's hand slipped under the hem of Taiga's sweatshirt and rested against his side. Taiga looked at him. Leo's expression changed just slightly. Taiga pulled the sweatshirt off.

Leo did the same with his shirt a little later. There was no rush to it. Taiga ran his fingers over Leo's shoulder, down his arm, back again.

Leo watched him.

“What?” Taiga asked.

“Nothing. Keep going.”

Taiga did. His mouth found the side of Leo's neck once, just beneath the jaw. Leo's fingers tightened lightly against his back. After that, the room got quieter.

They already knew how to touch each other. Tonight there was simply more time. More room to stop, laugh, adjust around Leo's ankle, and start again without alcohol or a cabin full of other people ten meters away.

At some point Taiga's phone buzzed on the desk. Neither moved. It buzzed again. Leo glanced toward it.

“Could be important.”

“If the university is on fire, somebody else can deal with it.”

Leo laughed. The phone stopped.

---

Later, the room looked like a minor argument had happened with clothing. Taiga's sweatshirt was on the desk chair. Leo's shirt had somehow reached the floor near the window.

One sock was missing. Taiga suspected the bed. Leo lay under the blanket beside him, hair completely destroyed. Taiga was on his back. Leo's head rested against his chest.

The injured ankle remained elevated on a pillow near the foot of the bed. Logistically stupid. They had made it work. Taiga looked at the ceiling. His body felt heavy in the best possible way.

Leo traced a line across his ribs with one finger.

“You awake?”

“Obviously.”

“You weren't talking.”

“That isn't sleep.”

“With you, difficult diagnostic criteria.”

Taiga flicked his ear. Leo laughed and tucked his face closer against him. Silence settled again. Taiga's hand moved through Leo's hair. After a minute Leo said:

“Today was insane.”

“Yeah.”

“I still don't think I processed the goal.”

“You weren't even in the box.”

“I had an excellent view.”

“You were thirty meters away.”

“Panoramic.”

Taiga smiled. Leo's hand flattened against his stomach.

“You know Enzo is never going to let us live down the locker room.”

“I can kill him.”

“You've threatened that a lot today.”

“One of these will stick.”

Leo laughed. Then quieter:

“I'm glad you did it.”

Taiga's fingers slowed in his hair.

“The goal?”

Leo lifted his head. Taiga looked at him. Leo's expression answered the question.

“Oh.”

“Yeah.”

Taiga felt his face heating again. Ridiculous. Leo smiled. Taiga's hand settled at the back of his neck for a second. Leo rested against his chest again.

“Good answer.”

“Go to sleep.”

“Bossy.”

“You're injured.”

“That has nothing to do with sleep.”

“It does now.”

Leo yawned halfway through whatever response he had planned. Taiga laughed.

“Pathetic.”

“Championship fatigue.”

“Sure.”

Leo pulled the blanket higher. His breathing gradually slowed. Taiga kept his hand in Leo's hair. The room was almost completely dark now. Only the charger light near Caleb's desk.

The faint glow from outside the window. Taiga listened until Leo's breathing became even. He should have been exhausted enough to sleep immediately. Instead he lay there for a while.

Conference champion. First-team starter. Goal scorer. Leo asleep against him. The categories did not arrange themselves into anything useful. They did not need to. Taiga reached toward the bedside table and set an alarm.

Then another thought occurred. He looked across the room at Caleb's perfectly made bed. His phone sat charging beside him. Taiga opened the chat.

**Taiga:** thanks for the warning

No reply. Probably asleep. Or busy. Taiga regretted the message instantly. He put the phone face-down. Leo shifted in his sleep and tightened one arm around Taiga's waist.

Taiga stopped thinking about Caleb. He closed his eyes. This time, sleep came.
