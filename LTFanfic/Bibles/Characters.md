# Olympus Fanfic — Non-Camp-Buddy Character Bible
## Revision Draft for Acts I–IV

This document inventories the **non-Camp-Buddy cast already present in Acts I–IV** and gives each recurring character a usable voice/personality target for the rewrite.

### Scope

Excluded by request:
- Taiga;
- Keitaro;
- Hiro;
- Hunter;
- Natsumi;
- Yoichi;
- Kieran;
- Yoshinori;
- Goro;
- Aiden;
- Yuri;
- Yuki;
- the rest of the Camp Buddy-origin cast.

The document distinguishes between:

- **Established:** already supported by the current Acts / existing character guides.
- **Revision direction:** a proposed sharpening or addition for the rewrite. This is **not canon yet** until incorporated.
- **Thin:** the character exists, but the current manuscript does not support a strong personality yet.

The objective is **not** to make every minor character equally important. It is to make recurring people identifiable by function, behavior and voice so the cast stops collapsing into the same dry, competent, mildly sarcastic register.

# 1. Leo Mendez

## Current role
- Co-lead.
- Olympus freshman.
- Computer Science student.
- More experienced organized soccer player than Taiga.
- Apollo first-team player.
- By the end of Act IV, Taiga's boyfriend.

## Author-only identity / background continuity
- His legal given name is **Julio Mendez**.
- He goes by **Leo** in ordinary life; narration, teammates, classmates and friends should continue to call him Leo unless a formal/legal context naturally uses Julio.
- In his original Jock Studio characterization, the full-name distinction is intimate/familial: **"Julio" is principally a home/family name**.
- Taiga first learns the legal name **Julio** in **Act I, Chapter 4**, when a General Chemistry table list identifies him as **Julio Mendez**. The discovery is casual and does not become a dramatic reveal; Taiga simply understands that Leo is the name he uses socially.
- Taiga does **not** begin calling him Julio after learning it. **Leo remains Leo at Olympus and in their relationship.**
- Act V should therefore not treat Julio as new factual information. Its significance comes from Taiga entering Leo's home environment and hearing his family actually call him **Julio**, letting Taiga experience the familial version of a name he already knows.
- Leo is **Dominican**, raised in **Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic**.
- He and Enzo Rosario attended the same secondary school and played organized soccer together before choosing Olympus in the United States for college.
- Their shared pre-Olympus history should be lightly visible in Acts I–IV through familiarity, shorthand and old references. Enzo can naturally know both names without needing to explain them.
- Leo's family remains primarily in the Dominican Republic, giving Act V a genuine shift into Leo's home environment.

**Soccer surname:** **Mendez**. Mercer/coaches use **Mendez** in formal football contexts; teammates and narration use **Leo**.

## Established personality
Leo is:
- highly social;
- energetic;
- talkative;
- observant about people;
- playful;
- curious;
- quick to include others;
- competitive without being permanently aggressive;
- good with children;
- willing to help people;
- capable of jealousy;
- capable of anger;
- more experienced than Taiga in both soccer and ordinary university social life.

He remembers small details people tell him and often follows up later.

Leo genuinely enjoys crowds and social situations, but his social battery can still run out. Act II establishes a **private reset habit**: when a crowded event becomes too much, he may slip away alone for about five minutes to get silence and then return. This is **not common knowledge** at Apollo. Taiga learns it only because he accidentally finds Leo alone on the balcony, and Leo explicitly asks him not to tell anyone. Other characters should not recognize, anticipate, tease, or knowingly accommodate this habit unless Leo later chooses to tell them. He comes from a loud home environment, so total silence can also feel strange to him.

## Author-only prior relationship history
- By Act II, Leo has already mentioned at least one **male ex** and has reduced that history to a joking shorthand: the ex was "very dramatic." This should later read as deliberate understatement rather than the full truth.
- Planned backstory: during his mid-to-late teens, Leo spent time in a **push-pull relationship** that became emotionally exhausting. The exact severity and details can be decided later; avoid prematurely locking it into a melodramatic or overtly abusive template.
- The core pattern was conditional closeness: the boyfriend would pull Leo close, then shut him out; become jealous or disappear; expect Leo to chase or reassure him; then resent Leo for chasing or being too present.
- Leo's instinct in that relationship was to **talk, fix, reassure and show up**. This made the cycle especially draining because he repeatedly responded to withdrawal by trying harder to restore contact.
- The experience left Leo with a specific boundary: he does not want to remain in a relationship where closeness means being repeatedly welcomed, pushed away, then expected to return on command.
- This history should inform — without being explicitly explained during — the Pinetree confrontation in Act II. When Taiga pushes Leo away after Aaron's comment, then calls him back when he actually leaves, Leo recognizes an old pattern strongly enough to say: **"I don't want to do it. Not anymore."**
- Leo should not consciously compare Taiga to the ex in that scene, and the narration should not explain the parallel there. The history should be revealed later, making the line retrospectively heavier.
- This also creates a deliberate emotional symmetry with Taiga: **both are reacting to damage from previous relationships during the same confrontation without knowing the other's history.**
  - Taiga's fear: when somebody becomes important enough to hurt him, he may lose control and destroy what he has built.
  - Leo's fear: closeness may become a cycle where he is pulled in, pushed away, and expected to keep returning.
- Important distinction: Leo's wound is **not** "Taiga reminds him of his ex" in a generic sense. The trigger is the specific behavioral pattern of being made responsible for another person's discomfort with closeness.
- Keep Leo's earlier "dramatic ex" remark intact. It becomes character-revealing in retrospect: Leo turned a painful history into a short funny anecdote because that was easier than explaining it.

Academically, he is competent but not pristine:
- he writes code that works before it becomes elegant;
- he can duplicate logic rather than abstracting it;
- he has group-project disasters;
- he can become irritated when teammates make reckless changes;
- he overschedules himself;
- he can lose track of practical details.

Soccer flaw already present:
- he can try to solve too much;
- he drops toward the ball when he should sometimes stay high;
- under pressure he can wait for certainty or over-involve himself.

## Voice
Leo should normally speak in **full, flowing thoughts**.

Typical mechanics:
- talks past the point where another character would stop;
- adds examples;
- corrects himself mid-sentence;
- makes jokes while explaining something real;
- occasionally realizes halfway through a sentence that what he said sounded strange;
- uses enthusiasm rather than deadpan as his default comic register;
- can become fast and rambling when excited or irritated.

When tired, socially depleted, jealous or genuinely hurt, his speech should shorten noticeably.

He should **not** default to:
- endless perfect emotional insight;
- therapist language;
- neat summaries of what Taiga is feeling;
- constant patience.

## Revision direction
Act III–IV should repeatedly expose the Leo already established in Acts I–II:
- occasional private five-minute resets / social fatigue after long group days, **without making him crowd-averse or making the habit common knowledge**;
- saying yes to too many things;
- getting snappier when depleted;
- occasional bad planning;
- needing Taiga's company without always knowing how to ask;
- frustration when he cannot fix a soccer problem;
- moments where Taiga supports him rather than the reverse.

The goal is not to give Leo a new dramatic flaw. It is to stop the story from sanding away the flaws he already has.

# 6. Coach Mercer

- Olympus/Apollo men's soccer coach.
- Central authority figure in Taiga's soccer arc.

## Established personality
Mercer is:
- in his forties;
- compact;
- sun-browned;
- demanding;
- loud when necessary;
- practical;
- extremely direct;
- fair;
- not sentimental.

- fullback;
- overlap;
- inside channel;
- pressing trigger;
- why width matters;

but always because Taiga needs the information to perform a task.

# 19. Professor Bell

## Revision status
**Legacy-draft character; remove from Taiga's academic canon.**

- Statistics becomes **Calculus I** under Professor Chen;
