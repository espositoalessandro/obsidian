# Leo Family Bible

Working bible for Leo / Julio Mendez's family and home environment in Santo Domingo.

This file should be expanded character by character as Act V is developed.

The family must feel like a real affectionate social ecosystem, not a collection of functions built only to explain Leo's psychology.

---

# 1. Mercedes — Abuela

## Core identity

**Name:** Mercedes  
**Family role:** Leo's grandmother  
**Maternal vs. paternal side:** **TBD**  
**Primary address in prose:** initially **Doña Mercedes** from Taiga; eventually **Abuela**

Mercedes should exist as a full person, not merely as "the grandmother."

Her exact age, former occupation, spouse history and side of the family remain open until the wider family structure is designed.

---

## Personality

Mercedes is:
- affectionate;
- sharp;
- observant;
- shamelessly teasing;
- socially confident;
- difficult to embarrass;
- very good at embarrassing Leo;
- emotionally perceptive beneath the jokes;
- comfortable letting other people make their own choices.

She has reached the stage of life where nobody at the table has much authority over her, and she knows it.

Her humor is often deadpan.

She can say something outrageous with the facial expression of somebody commenting on the weather.

---

## The "naughty Abuela" rule

Mercedes can be dirty-minded and openly mischievous, especially when Leo is easy to fluster.

The comedy should come from **Mercedes herself**, not from a stereotype about Dominican grandmothers.

Her teasing should be:
- affectionate;
- clever;
- occasionally suggestive;
- never sexually invasive toward Taiga;
- never humiliating in a cruel way;
- aimed especially at Leo because she knows exactly how easy he is to embarrass when romance is involved.

A useful tonal model:

> She looks completely innocent while saying the most devastating thing at the table.

Example energy:

If Taiga says Leo is a bad Spanish teacher, Mercedes may imply that perhaps Leo's mouth is useful for something else.

Leo is mortified.

Taiga understands enough to realize what happened and immediately likes her.

This establishes the beginning of their alliance.

---

## Attitude toward Taiga and Leo's relationship

Mercedes should have **zero dramatic uncertainty** about Leo bringing home a boyfriend.

Do not give her a prolonged:

> "So... what exactly are you two?"

scene.

Julio brought home his boyfriend.

Fine.

More interesting things exist to talk about.

She:
- accepts Taiga quickly;
- evaluates him as a person rather than as a problem;
- enjoys the fact that he pushes back;
- notices almost immediately that his quietness is not meekness;
- recognizes his sarcasm early;
- realizes he is stubborn;
- sees that he cares deeply about Leo even when he is bad at displaying it conventionally.

Mercedes should be one of the first relatives to understand:

> Taiga is not shy in the simple sense. He is watchful, private and very capable of biting back.

Once she discovers that, teasing Leo with Taiga becomes irresistible.

---

## Taiga / Mercedes relationship

### Initial address

Taiga should **not** be told on arrival:

> "Call me Abuela."

That would feel too immediate and emotionally unearned.

Instead, Taiga initially uses:

> **Doña Mercedes**

This gives him a respectful form of address while preserving some social distance.

### Progression toward "Abuela"

The change should emerge naturally after Taiga has been around the family long enough to stop feeling like a formal guest.

Mercedes gradually becomes annoyed or amused by his continued use of **Doña Mercedes**.

Possible dynamic:

> "*Again with Doña Mercedes? Julio calls me Abuela.*"

Taiga:

> "*I am not Julio.*"

Mercedes:

> "*No. You're his boyfriend.*"

The exact dialogue is not locked, but the emotional movement is.

She does not ceremonially "adopt" Taiga on day one.

Instead, she keeps undermining the formality until Taiga eventually gives in.

The first sincere:

> "*Thank you, Abuela.*"

should feel like a small relationship milestone.

Mercedes reacts as though she has won.

Leo realizes the alliance is now official.

---

## Why Mercedes likes Taiga

Taiga makes a strong impression on her because:
- he has prepared Spanish before arriving;
- he is clearly trying even when his Spanish is rough;
- he does not expect Leo to do everything for him;
- he watches before speaking;
- he is not intimidated by family noise for long;
- he can return teasing instead of simply absorbing it;
- he cares about Leo in practical, observable ways.

His imperfect Spanish is part of the charm.

Mercedes is impressed less by technical competence than by the fact that:

> **He cared enough to prepare before meeting them.**

---

## Language dynamic

Mercedes speaks normal native Spanish.

When Taiga understands her, her dialogue should render as natural grammatical English under the Act V provisional Spanish-rendering convention.

Her speech can become one of Taiga's recurring listening challenges because:
- she may speak quickly;
- she does not always simplify automatically;
- she enjoys watching him work something out;
- she can deliberately repeat something more slowly when she actually wants him to understand.

She should never mock his accent or mistakes cruelly.

She may absolutely exploit a misunderstanding for a joke if the mood is safe.

Over time, Taiga becomes better at recognizing her specific rhythms and favorite expressions.

---

## Relationship with Leo / Julio

Mercedes has known Leo long enough to see through him quickly.

She knows:
- he likes being useful;
- he says yes easily;
- he likes making people happy;
- he hates letting people down;
- he can run himself tired without admitting it;
- he often presents tiredness as if it were merely scheduling.

She loves these qualities in him.

She does **not** see his warmth as fake.

However, she may also be one of the relatives least invested in forcing his constant participation.

That gives her an important late-act function.

She has enough confidence in family bonds to believe:

> Julio disappearing upstairs for an hour is not a crisis.

> Julio saying no does not mean he loves anyone less.

This is part of what makes her teasing persona more than comic relief.

---

## Role in Leo's Act V arc

Mercedes should not become Leo's therapist or deliver the act's thesis.

Her contribution is behavioral.

Early in the act:
- she calls for him;
- includes him;
- teases him;
- assumes his presence like everyone else;
- enjoys having him home.

Later, when Leo begins experimenting with boundaries, she can become one of the relatives who **does not catastrophize them**.

Someone may ask:

> Where's Julio?

Mercedes can simply answer:

> He's tired.

And continue what she was doing.

No guilt.

No summons.

No interpretation.

That matters because it quietly demonstrates:

> Some of the people Leo exhausts himself for were always capable of surviving his absence.

She can also be one of the relatives who retrospectively reveals that the family did not always understand his exhaustion because Leo did not show it.

Possible conceptual idea:

> "He's always been like that. If everybody else was having fun, Julio was happy."

The hidden question is:

> What if Julio wasn't?

Mercedes may realize that the family sometimes assumed happiness because Leo made happiness easy to assume.

Do not turn this into a grand confession scene unless the later outline earns it.

---

## Emotional function

Mercedes gives the family three important qualities at once:

1. **Comedy**
   - She can puncture tension.
   - She is dangerous at the dinner table.
   - She and Taiga can form a small anti-Leo alliance.

2. **Immediate acceptance**
   - Taiga does not need to spend Act V proving he deserves to date Leo to every relative.
   - The family conflict is not built around homophobia or romantic rejection.

3. **A model of secure attachment**
   - She loves loudly.
   - She teases relentlessly.
   - She does not require Leo's constant performance in order to feel loved by him.

That third quality becomes especially important as the act progresses.

---

## Guardrails

Do not:
- reduce Mercedes to sexual jokes;
- make every line a punchline;
- make her magically omniscient;
- make her immediately understand Leo's entire psychological problem;
- make her speak for the whole family;
- turn her into a saintly perfect grandmother;
- make her instantly call Taiga her grandson;
- make "Abuela" a forced day-one title;
- use her as shorthand for a generic loud Latina grandmother stereotype.

Do:
- give her mundane interests and routines later;
- let her have opinions unrelated to Leo;
- let her occasionally be annoying;
- let her misunderstand things;
- give her independent relationships with other family members;
- let her warmth coexist with irreverence;
- allow her to become one of Taiga's easiest connections naturally.
