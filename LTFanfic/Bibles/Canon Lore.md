# 4. Core Camp Buddy Scout Group

The core original scout group relevant to this fanfic is:

The six names above remain the **primary emotional scout group**, but Eduard Fitzpatrick and Leonard “Lee” Kurosawa are part of Taiga's wider post-Camp-Buddy circle and **do remain present in the Olympus novel**.

### Eduard Fitzpatrick
- Post-redemption friend of Taiga, no longer treated as a hostile lackey.
- Flamboyant, fashion/aesthetic-minded, openly expressive and willing to be affectionate without embarrassment.
- Source dialogue frequently uses emphatic reactions, camp phrasing and dramatic exaggeration.
- He can appear in group calls/messages, reunions and major friend events, but does not need an independent subplot.

### Leonard “Lee” Kurosawa
- Prefers **Lee**; his full name is Leonard Kurosawa.
- Intelligent, analytical, pedantic and often socially awkward.
- Tends to over-explain factual details, quantify observations and use more formal wording than the people around him.
- Source material explicitly establishes that his complicated way of speaking makes ordinary social interaction harder for him.
- He can appear in group calls/messages, reunions and major friend events, but does not need an independent subplot.

For this novel:
- keep both as recurring supporting members of Taiga's old social world;
- let them have university/interests/lives outside Taiga;
- do not revert them to pre-redemption bullying behavior;
- do not use them purely as exposition devices.

Their voices should be grounded first in `CampBuddyScript.csv`, with project-canon additions layered on top.

# 5. Keitaro — Source-Lore Function

Keitaro is the emotional center of the original Camp Buddy story.

Important source-backed facts:
- photography matters deeply to him;
- he carries a camera;
- he keeps a meaningful journal combining writing, memories, and photographs;
- preserving experiences is central to his identity;
- he is unusually willing to hear people out and offer second chances.

The journal is not disposable stationery.

For Keitaro it represents:
- continuity;
- memory;
- relationships;
- the summer itself;
- his way of making experience permanent.

Therefore Taiga burning it is one of the most serious things Taiga does.

---

# 6. Hiro — Source-Lore Function

Hiro and Keitaro are childhood best friends.

Important background:
- they have known each other for years before Camp Buddy;
- Hiro's mother, Hina Akiba, is ill;
- Hiro learned cooking from his mother and strongly associates food with care;
- as they grew older, Hiro felt the increasing distance between himself and Keitaro;
- Hiro secretly researched Camp Buddy because he feared he might soon have to leave and wanted one meaningful summer with Keitaro.

Hiro's strong reactions to separation are therefore not merely comic.

The successful Hiro-route trajectory ends with Hiro and Keitaro choosing each other.

In sequel continuity:
- they remain together;
- they are still together as boyfriends; they are not engaged.

# 7. Taiga Before His Redemption

Taiga enters the main Camp Buddy summer carrying the aftermath of the previous year's SportsFest.

He is:
- hostile;
- competitive;
- jealous of Keitaro;
- desperate to repair his ruined reputation;
- convinced Keitaro receives acceptance too easily.

The important emotional logic is:

> Taiga returned wanting proof that he deserved another chance, then watched Keitaro receive the belonging Taiga desperately wanted.

That scarcity thinking becomes rivalry.

# 8. Selective Kieran Continuity — Explicit Project Merge

The older lore bible quarantined Kieran because his full history is explored most directly in Taiga-route material.

That quarantine is now **partly superseded**.

For the Olympus fanfic, the following Kieran material is deliberately imported into project continuity:

- Kieran and Taiga were once **best friends and boyfriends**.
- Their relationship predates the main Camp Buddy summer.
- They enrolled at Camp Buddy together during the previous SportsFest era.
- Kieran caused the previous SportsFest riot.
- Kieran framed Taiga so the blame would fall on him.
- Taiga did not expose Kieran afterward.
- Taiga effectively carried the social blame.
- Kieran later recognizes that Taiga protected him.
- Kieran deeply regrets what he did.
- Kieran's actions are tied to a lasting scar on Taiga.
- Their relationship ended badly.
- Kieran remained emotionally attached to Taiga afterward.

Source dialogue directly supports:
- Kieran calling Taiga his boyfriend;
- Kieran later telling Keitaro that he and Taiga "used to be in a relationship";
- Kieran admitting that he caused the riot and framed Taiga;
- Kieran acknowledging Taiga's scar and his own guilt.

## What is NOT imported

The later Taiga-route Kieran reconciliation does **not** already exist in the Olympus past.

Specifically:
- Kieran did not return later and fully reconcile with Taiga before Olympus;
- the Taiga-route reunion arc is not historical fact here;
- the Taiga-route romantic end-state is not historical fact.

The Olympus Spring Break arc is this continuity's **new re-entry point for Kieran**.

This lets the fanfic preserve:
- the past relationship;
- the betrayal;
- the guilt;
- the old familiarity;

without pre-resolving any of it before the current story.

# 9. Previous SportsFest — Project-Safe Summary

The previous SportsFest should be remembered through the following project-canon chain:

1. Taiga and Kieran were close and romantically involved.
2. Their relationship became unhealthy and conflict-ridden.
3. Kieran caused the SportsFest riot.

# 10. Main Camp Buddy Summer — Taiga's Redemption

The defining Camp Buddy summer happens **immediately before Taiga begins at Olympus**, not many years earlier.

Taiga's redemption chain is:

**previous rejection**  
→ returns desperate to repair his reputation  
→ sees Keitaro accepted easily  
→ jealousy  
→ rivalry  
→ sabotage / framing  
→ exposure  
→ Keitaro refuses to abandon him  
→ Taiga escalates  
→ burns Keitaro's journal  
→ Eduard slaps Taiga  
→ emotional collapse  
→ dock/pier truth-telling  
→ Taiga expects hatred or retaliation  
→ Taiga asks Keitaro to stay  
→ remorse becomes action  
→ Taiga contributes memories/notes toward rebuilding the journal  
→ the group begins accepting him  
→ Taiga participates in saving Camp Buddy  
→ belonging becomes something he helps build rather than compete for

This sequence is the emotional foundation of adult Taiga.

# 11. Goro Photo Incident

During the main summer:
- an embarrassing/nude photograph of Goro becomes a major crisis;
- available evidence initially implicates Keitaro;

- the crisis intensifies Taiga's conflict with the group.

# 12. Burned Journal

Taiga burns Keitaro's original journal after his exposure.

This matters because the journal contains:
- writing;
- photographs;
- memories;
- the emotional record of the summer.

The repair effort can replace the object but cannot undo the destruction of the original.

That distinction remains important in Taiga's guilt.

---

# 13. Eduard's Slap

Eduard slaps Taiga during the emotional collapse surrounding the burned journal.

The slap remains part of Taiga's past because it is structurally important to his Camp Buddy breakdown. Present-day Eduard is post-redemption; his current friendship with Taiga does not erase that history.

---

# 14. Dock / Pier Confession

The dock is one of Taiga's most important emotional locations.

It is where:
- the defenses finally fail;
- Taiga explains the desperation underneath his jealousy;
- he expects Keitaro to hit him or hate him;
- Keitaro refuses that script;
- Taiga asks Keitaro to stay.

For Taiga, the dock is linked to:
- shame;
- fear;
- first honest vulnerability;
- the possibility of being seen at his worst and still not abandoned.

# 15. Replacement Journal and Group Acceptance

The original journal is gone.

The group reconstructs/rebuilds a replacement using:
- remembered events;
- photographs/memories where available;
- Taiga's notes and contributions.

This does not erase what Taiga did.

It matters because he participates in repair rather than only apologizing.

The group gradually accepts him.

# 18. Camp Buddy Architecture — Cabin Bathrooms

Camp Buddy does **not** use one central communal bathhouse for the scouts.

Each cabin has its **own bathroom**.

The source visual reference supports:
- tiled wet areas;
- wood/tile camp aesthetic;
- multiple open Japan-like shower/wash stations;
- low dividers / partial openness;
- some more-private stalls or sections.

When characters shower while sharing a cabin, they use that cabin bathroom.

# 35. Lore Core

The essential past beneath the Olympus story is:

**Camp Buddy taught Taiga that being seen at his worst did not automatically mean abandonment.**

**Olympus is where he now has to learn that choosing closeness is different from merely surviving rejection.**

Those are different stories.

The first is lore.

The second belongs in the Story & Romance Bible.
