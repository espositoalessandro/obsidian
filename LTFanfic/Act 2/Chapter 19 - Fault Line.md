The sun had moved behind the trees, and the cold felt sharper without it.

No position changes at first.

Taiga played wide. Aaron was behind him. Leo was opposite with Gav.

Aaron crossed.

Enzo finished.

Bad touches.

Late reactions.

“Okay.”

Good ball.

"Wait, come one I didn't mean it."

“Don't talk to me. I'm fucking done with you.”

For the first time since arriving at Olympus, Taiga wanted to go back home.
