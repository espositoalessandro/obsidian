
### Core concepts

- Novel takes place in the first years from Hogwarts foundation, when the four founders were still looking for students.
- Protagonist is Merlin. The story will follow his journey into becoming the greatest wizard who ever lived.
- The original Arthurian legend takes place roughly 500 years before these events. Present Merlin is a 'reincarnation' of Past Merlin.
- Present Merlin doesn't remember anything of Past Merlin.

### Brainstorm

- The novel could be about the birth of magic itself (meh)
- The novel could end with Merlin's sacrifice
- Old Round Table knights could be wizards as well and the could have followed Merlin into the present, maybe resetting themselves as well. So, the Golden Trio could be Arthur, Guinevere and Merlin, with Arthur being the actual core character (something like Harry is in the original series. Merlin could be the equivalent of Ron). Lancelot, Tristran and the others (like Cadogan) can simply be friends. Eventually, they'll all discover they're part of the same plan.
- The novel could be about history repeating itself. Something like Zelda's core idea of reincarnation, this could be just one of the many "runs" they already had. Merlin's sacrifice will make sure this is the last one.
- Past Merlin or Arthur could have laid out a plan to make sure their next "reincarnation" would be the last. 
- As the story unfolds, the original master plan will resurface and Present Merlin will have to decide whether to follow the footstep of his previous self or take a different path.
- Merlin at some point, could recover all the memories from his past self. That's how a teenager can suddenly become an extremely powerful wizard.
- Making sure Present Merlin recovers his memory could be Past Merlin masterplan's objective.
